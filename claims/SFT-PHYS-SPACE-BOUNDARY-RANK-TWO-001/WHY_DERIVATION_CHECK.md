# Rank-two boundary of three-space

Claim: `SFT-PHYS-SPACE-BOUNDARY-RANK-TWO-001`

## Why

Geometric dilution requires the exact count of independent boundary
coordinates. That count must follow from the forced spatial carrier rather than
from a measured falloff exponent or a conventional surface formula.

## Derivation

One direction is held by the localized source-to-boundary normal relation. The
complete boundary retains every nonnormal direction. Its rank is therefore the
unique positive predecessor whose successor by the held normal reassembles the
forced three-direction carrier: rank two. At scale `r`, the two retained
directions generate the complete pair-cell support `r` paired with `r`.

Nine binary axes and one four-way rank axis generate 2,048 forms and leave the
rank-two complete-boundary form alone.

## Check

Exact witnesses give four cells at scale two and nine at scale three. The
independent validator reconstructs the predecessor and the full candidate
product without importing the primary module.
