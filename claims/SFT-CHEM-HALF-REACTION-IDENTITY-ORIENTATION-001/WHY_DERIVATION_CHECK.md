# Why ECHEM-001 requires a derivation check

A signed half-equation can conceal the identities and direction of transferred carriers. ECHEM-001 therefore generates the complete half-reaction grammar before comparison, retains every species and phase, represents electron transfer by a positive count of held carrier occurrences, and records direction as an exact reversible source/terminal orientation. The complete IUPAC record and all 22 pages of the NIST standard-potential report are opened only after the Fold relation is sealed.
