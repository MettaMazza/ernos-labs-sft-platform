# Activity and non-ideal composition law

Claim: `SFT-CHEM-ACTIVITY-NONIDEAL-COMPOSITION-009`  
Chemistry obligation: `SFT-CHEM-OBL-THERMO-009`

## WHY

SFT does not import a logarithmic chemical activity, fugacity convention, ideal-mixture premise or fitted activity coefficient. The Fold-native activity is the exact count of a component's accessible exchange-support states over its complete held reference-support count at a fully retained composition and condition. Non-ideality is the exact relation between the joint accessible support and the independently composed support.

## DERIVATION

The eight-axis grammar generates 256 forms and leaves one survivor:

`complete-component-exchange-support-account__complete-held-condition-and-component-coordinates__exact-accessible-support-over-reference-support__exact-joint-versus-independent-support-relation__structural-EmptyOne-absent-component__complete-value-free-204-row-identity-seal__complete-nine-dataset-204-row-vector-with-68-EmptyOne-boundaries__depth-independent-exact-support-replication`

The activity ratio is exact and positive. Equality between joint and independently composed support is the independent relation with an `EmptyOne` separation; strict inequality is retained as a positive support separation and held interaction orientation. An absent component is `EmptyOne`, never a numerical zero. Replicating all support counts by the same exact positive count preserves both activity and relation at every finite depth.

## CHECK

A value-free specification sealed before the NIST TRC ThermoML values opened. The raw source is preserved byte-for-byte. Every direct isopiestic relative-water-activity row is retained: six compounds, five binary and four ternary datasets, 204 rows at 298.15 K, complete molality inscriptions, activities, uncertainties, phases, standard states and methods. All 68 external zero-glyph composition coordinates remain in the evidence and become structural `EmptyOne` only at the SFT interface. The complete measured activity range is `4603/5000` through `624/625`, with 34 distinct exact values.

The source article also reports fitted NRTL-, Wilson- and related correlations. None is used as a measurement, proof input, candidate selector or correction parameter.

## FALSIFICATION

The law fails if activity is detached from component, phase, composition or condition; if activity is not an exact positive part of its reference support; if non-ideality requires a fitted coefficient or assumed ideality; if absence becomes numerical zero; if replication changes the result; if any target opens before the complete identity seal; or if any source row, absence boundary, uncertainty, method or adverse value is omitted.
