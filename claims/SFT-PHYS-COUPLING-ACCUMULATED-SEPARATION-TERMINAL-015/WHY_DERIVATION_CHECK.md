# WHY

This successor closes the difference between proving that every finite running
gap is positive and proving what every finite accumulation of those gaps can
do.  It is needed because a shrinking term is not by itself an accumulated
bound, and a completed infinite sum is not an admissible SFT object.

# DERIVATION

The admitted binary and generator-three running sectors are compared only on
their generated common supports.  Their first exact gap is `1/12`.  From the
second gap onward every successor is strictly below half its predecessor.
Finite positive addition therefore keeps every partial accumulation in
`[1/12, 11/60)`.  For every positive rational tolerance, finite binary
succession generates a tail witness below that tolerance.  No terminal sum,
logarithm, continuum limit or measured coupling enters the proof.
