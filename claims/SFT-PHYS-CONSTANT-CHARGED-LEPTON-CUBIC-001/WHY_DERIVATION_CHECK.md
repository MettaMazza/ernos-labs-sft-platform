Claim: `SFT-PHYS-CONSTANT-CHARGED-LEPTON-CUBIC-001`

## Why

V1/V2 recorded the charged-lepton cubic and its exact invariant values. That
record creates a mandatory reconstruction question; it does not supply an
executable premise. V3 must derive the invariant tuple in Physics before a
particle-mass comparison can be admitted.

## Derivation

The independently admitted Fold recurrence supplies generator three. The unique
positive predecessor of three supplies the two Fold fibres. A complete
three-position no-loss partition has root sum One. Pairing the two fibres with
the generator supplies `1/(2*3) = 1/6`. Two complete colour fifth-power supports
have count 486; their positive predecessor is 485, forcing the leading product
`1/485`. The complete neighbouring channel census is two, three and four. The
three-root carrier is itself the generator-three channel, so holding one exact
channel part from 485 forces `1/(485 - 1/3) = 3/1454`. The other generated
channels give `2/969` and `4/1939` and fail carrier identity.

The proof retains only exact symmetric invariants. It neither forms nor rounds
the cubic's irrational roots.

The independent validator rebuilds that product without importing the law
module and separately recomputes `1`, `1/6`, `1/485`, `2/969`, `3/1454` and
`4/1939`. Four adverse controls reject an incomplete carrier, changed source,

External charged-lepton measurements are a separate post-seal validation claim;
they do not enter this formal admission.
