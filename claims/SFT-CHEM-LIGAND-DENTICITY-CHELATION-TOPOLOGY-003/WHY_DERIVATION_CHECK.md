# Exact ligand denticity and chelation from connected donor-site topology

Claim: `SFT-CHEM-LIGAND-DENTICITY-CHELATION-TOPOLOGY-003`  
Chemistry obligation: `SFT-CHEM-OBL-INORG-003`

## WHY

INORG-001 retains every central–ligand incidence and INORG-002 forces the complete positive incidence count. Those laws do not yet distinguish multiple donor sites belonging to one exact ligand carrier from chemically identical sites belonging to separate ligand occurrences. INORG-003 retains that missing carrier identity and asks what complete connected donor-site support forces.

## DERIVATION

Eight binary axes generate 256 forms. Exactly one retains one ligand carrier occurrence, every distinct donor site on that carrier, one central occurrence, positive generated incidence count, the first carrier–centre path closure, distinct separate-site/kappa/eta boundaries, the complete value-free external identity seal and the successor that preserves prior support.

Denticity is the positive cardinality of donor incidences for one carrier and one centre. One donor site has no closed carrier–centre path. Appending the next distinct donor site, with the retained internal carrier path, is the first successor that closes such a path. Every later site preserves the closed topology and increments denticity exactly once.

## CHECK

Before the source vector opened, six complete current IUPAC Gold Book record identities were fixed and twenty-four record surfaces were sealed without definitions, examples, exclusions, classifications, formulae, statuses, provenance text, values or target hashes. After seal, the complete binding-site, chelation, denticity, eta, kappa and ligand records opened. The post-seal vector retains the given-ligand/same-centre denticity relation, the first multiple-site chelation boundary, the bidentate ethylenediamine example, the three single-binding-site exclusions, the kappa/eta distinction and the inorganic/biochemical scope boundary.

## FALSIFICATION

The claim fails if denticity differs from complete distinct donor incidence for one carrier and centre; if equal chemical labels collapse separate carriers; if one site is labelled chelation; if the first multiple-site connected support does not close a path; if any registered source surface changes or is omitted; if kappa, eta or separate-site boundaries collapse; or if imported, fitted, continuum, numerical-zero or target-derived structure enters the law.

## BOUNDARY

The donor-site successor and first-closure result are depth-independent. The external result is finite-complete for six byte-sealed IUPAC files and twenty-four registered surfaces. Coordination geometry, isomerism and ligand-field consequences remain downstream obligations.
