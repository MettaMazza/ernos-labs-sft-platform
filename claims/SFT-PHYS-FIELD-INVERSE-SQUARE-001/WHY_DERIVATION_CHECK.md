# Inverse-square dilution from rank-two boundary support

Claim: `SFT-PHYS-FIELD-INVERSE-SQUARE-001`

## Why

The exponent must be a consequence sent outward to measurement. A measured or
fitted exponent flowing back into the law would violate the foundational
measurement boundary.

## Derivation

The admitted field law retains one conserved localized source across every
complete equivalent boundary. The admitted spatial-boundary law fixes two
independent boundary scale coordinates. A distance ratio `r` therefore creates
the complete pair support `r` paired with `r`. Equal allocation of the unchanged
source over those cells forces response per cell to be `source/(r*r)`.

Ten binary axes and one four-way profile axis generate 4,096 forms. Linear
falloff loses one boundary coordinate, cubic falloff adds the held normal as a
free boundary coordinate, and a fitted power reverses the empirical boundary.
Only inverse-square survives.

exact ninth response at three times the distance and exact recomposition of an
arbitrary positive rational source. Blind physical comparison occurs only
