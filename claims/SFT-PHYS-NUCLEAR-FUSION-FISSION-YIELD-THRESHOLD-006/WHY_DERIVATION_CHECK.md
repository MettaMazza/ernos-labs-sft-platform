## WHY

The predecessor law already closed the binary fusion and fission directions, exact mass/charge/neutron conservation, positive binding gain, retained release carrier, normalized Half-One junction barrier and the unique nickel-62 binding maximum. V1 V-7 additionally required three distinctions that cannot be hidden inside that earlier result:

1. fusion versus fission energy gain **per conserved nucleon**;
2. the physically different carriers that control access to fusion and fission; and
3. dimensional reaction-energy comparison without converting a normalized Fold relation into a universal measured threshold.

Those distinctions matter. A light fusion reaction can release more energy per unit reacting mass while one heavy fission event releases more total energy. Treating those two statements as interchangeable would erase the conserved mass count. Likewise, a collision between two charged nuclear words does not meet the same boundary as a neutral particle captured by one heavy word or a spontaneous deformation internal to that word.

The present successor closes those distinctions. It does not import a conventional reaction model, mass formula, target energy, temperature or cross section into the derivation. It derives and seals the exact structural order first, then compares the sealed result with complete registered AMDC and IAEA records.
