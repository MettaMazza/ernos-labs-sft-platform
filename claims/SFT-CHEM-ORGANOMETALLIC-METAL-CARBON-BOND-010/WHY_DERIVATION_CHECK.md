# Exact metal–carbon organometallic bond

## WHY

INORG-010 asks which structure forces the organometallic bond class without allowing a conventional metal list, a compound name, proximity, a selected formula fragment, an observed example or a fitted species exception to choose it.

## DERIVATION

One chemical entity retains each admitted centre occurrence, each carbon occurrence, their generated direct adjacency and the complete positive held bond-electron support of that adjacency. Positive direct support uniquely forces the organometallic class. The exact bond topology is the complete distinct incidence support and its positive cardinality. Without a direct incidence the result is structural `EmptyOne`, never numerical zero and never a positive classification. Appending one fresh direct incidence increments the count once while preserving all prior support.

## CHECK

The eight binary axes generate 256 candidates and require one depth-independent survivor. A target-inaccessible implementation independently regenerates the full product, the first direct incidence and its successor.

The frozen IUPAC record contributes twelve distinct post-seal comparisons: the direct bond criterion, positive incidence scope, traditional and expanded centre scope, six named examples, the delocalized-anion context boundary and the explicit exclusion when direct carbon–metal structural evidence is absent. The full source, citation, licence and disclaimer remain preserved. None of those outcomes is present in the prediction program.
