# Exact inorganic acid/base, redox and reaction-network law

## WHY

INORG-017 must combine Lewis and redox chemistry without reducing either to a name, an unstructured equation, a signed oxidation-number convention or two independently balanced half-records.

## DERIVATION

One finite network retains every chemical species and every transition. A Lewis step holds one provider, one acceptor, the two forced electron occurrences and the resulting adduct as distinct identities. A redox step holds one positive complete electron support from donor to acceptor; the same support is simultaneously the donor removal and acceptor receipt. Its exact reverse swaps endpoints while retaining the carriers. Every step appears exactly once in the ordered network path, and a fresh step extends species and path support without an exception.

## CHECK

The 256-form grammar has one depth-independent survivor and an implementation-distinct network reconstruction. Fifteen frozen IUPAC surfaces preserve Lewis provider/acceptor and adduct composition, oxidation and reduction transfer correspondence, conventional downstream criteria, and one explicitly introduced but unrendered Lewis-acid example structure. Seven development-observed and eight identity-only/unopened rows are all retained.
