## WHY

SFT does not import a Gibbs equation, logarithm or signed scalar and then call its sign an explanation. A reaction direction is forced only from complete forward and reverse path accounts: exact positive retained-energy requirements and exact positive counts of distinctions closed by the path. One path is favoured only when its complete account is componentwise no greater and strictly smaller on at least one axis. Equal accounts are equilibrium with structural `EmptyOne` separations; crossed accounts halt because any weighted trade-off would be a free parameter.
