# Why ORG-014 requires a derivation check

A major-product list is not a complete selectivity law. ORG-014 must generate the complete finite product support before outcomes, partition it independently by held chemo-, regio- and stereo-coordinates, and preserve every reported product, identifier, amount and absence. The preregistered ORD interval is outcome-blind and complete; the empirical comparison cannot delete minor, adverse, absent or unresolved rows.
