# Exact coordination number from generated central–ligand incidence

Claim: `SFT-CHEM-COORDINATION-NUMBER-INCIDENCE-COUNT-002`  
Chemistry obligation: `SFT-CHEM-OBL-INORG-002`

## WHY

INORG-001 forces the complete coordination carrier, one retained central occurrence, every distinct ligand occurrence and every positive attachment trace. INORG-002 asks what exact count that complete direct-incidence support forces, without importing a coordination-number table, valence rule, geometry catalogue or bonding model.

## DERIVATION

The eight binary axes generate 256 forms. Exactly one counts every distinct direct incidence on the retained central occurrence once, keeps repeated ligand occurrences distinct, excludes structural absence from positive support, keeps external definitional senses separated and preserves all prior traces when the next incidence is appended:

`admitted-complete-coordination-entity__every-distinct-direct-central-incidence__positive-count-of-generated-incidence-traces__occurrence-distinct-incidences-counted-once__structural-EmptyOne-outside-positive-support__complete-23-record-value-free-identity-seal__direct-inorganic-incidence-sense-held__next-incidence-preserves-prior-and-adds-one`

The count is not assumed: it is the positive cardinality of the generated direct-incidence tuple. One direct incidence forces the base count. Appending one new direct incidence to the same centre preserves every prior trace and adds exactly one.

## CHECK

Before values opened, one complete current IUPAC coordination-number record and complete NIST CCCBDB experimental-geometry pages for ScF3, TiCl4 and Fe(CO)5 were fixed. Twenty-three identities sealed without definitions, formulas, point groups, link counts, coordinates, limitations, measurements or target hashes.

## FALSIFICATION

The claim fails if coordination number differs from the positive cardinality of complete distinct direct incidences on the retained central occurrence; if equal ligand-group labels collapse occurrences; if absence is counted; if the successor does not preserve prior traces and add one; if any of twenty-three records changes or is omitted; if the IUPAC senses are mixed; if the NIST vector is not `3`, `4`, `5`; or if an imported, fitted, continuum, numerical-zero or target-derived structure enters the law.
