# Why PHASE-001 requires a derivation check

A multiphase material state is the complete partition of one finite specimen carrier into held phase classes; every exact phase part is retained and the parts recompose the One.

The law is not selected by a named continuum equation, fitted phase diagram, database value, source fragment or favourable specimen. Its complete 256-form grammar is enumerated before target release, independently reconstructed and compared against every preregistered authoritative record.
