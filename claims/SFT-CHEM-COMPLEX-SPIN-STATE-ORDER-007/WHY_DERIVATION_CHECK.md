# Exact complex spin-state order and crossover

## WHY

INORG-006 forces a three-plus-two split support, while the admitted electron law supplies complementary pairs and unmatched fibres. INORG-007 asks whether high spin, low spin and their crossover can be obtained from those exact structures without importing a crystal-field Hamiltonian, a pairing-energy parameter, a spectrochemical table or a measured crossover target.

## DERIVATION

Six retained electron occurrences can be distributed over three lower and two upper supports only as pairs or singles. Quotienting only the permutations inside each equal split block leaves exactly ten complete signatures. No signature is selected or omitted.

The unique least-crossing and then least-unmatched signature contains three lower pairs. Its upper support and crossing count are structural `EmptyOne`; its spin width is the successor One. This is the forced low-spin extremum.

The unique greatest-unmatched and then least-crossing signature contains one lower pair, two lower singles and two upper singles. It retains four unmatched fibres, spin width five and exactly two split crossings. This is the forced high-spin extremum.

Ordering composes two already admitted positive path classes: each complementary pair closes one path, and each occupied upper occurrence crosses the retained split once per interaction recurrence. At diluted structural `EmptyOne` recurrence, only pair closure remains: high costs one path and low costs three, so high precedes low. At the first retained recurrence, high and low each cost three, forcing the crossover coincidence. At the second retained recurrence, high costs five and low remains three, so low precedes high. Every later positive successor adds two high-state crossing paths and none to the low state, making the result depth-independent.

Admitted rank-two dilution maps larger separation to weaker retained interaction and smaller separation to stronger retained interaction. No dimensional distance or temperature enters the law.
