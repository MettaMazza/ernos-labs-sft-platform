# Exact complete-support competing-channel branching relation

Claim: `SFT-CHEM-COMPETING-CHANNEL-BRANCHING-006`  
Chemistry obligation: `SFT-CHEM-OBL-KIN-006`

## WHY

Once finite paths and transition boundaries are admitted, a single reaction may retain multiple product channels. Selecting only the dominant or favorable product erases support and changes every reported share. Importing probability normalization or a conventional branching equation would assume the relation. The Fold record instead retains every registered product channel and derives the whole and its shares from that complete support.

## DERIVATION

Each source-ordered product channel retains its reaction, condition, product and row identity together with exact positive support or structural `EmptyOne`. The complete support is forced as the exact sum of every positive retained channel. Each branch share is then uniquely forced as its exact channel support divided by the complete support. Positive shares reconstruct foundational One exactly. An absent registered row remains `EmptyOne`; it is not omitted and no value is invented.

The eight-axis product contains 256 forms and one survivor:

`complete-registered-product-channel-support__exact-sum-of-complete-retained-support__exact-channel-support-over-complete-support__held-reaction-condition-product-and-source-row-identities__structural-EmptyOne-channel-retained-without-invented-value__complete-source-ordered-support-share-uncertainty-and-adverse-record__experimental-vector-separated-from-calculated-and-analysis-disclosures__value-free-complete-eight-channel-identity-seal-and-depth-independent-successor`

Base: two competing registered channels with positive complete support. Successor: append the next source channel, retaining every earlier identity and raw support while uniquely recomputing complete-support shares.
