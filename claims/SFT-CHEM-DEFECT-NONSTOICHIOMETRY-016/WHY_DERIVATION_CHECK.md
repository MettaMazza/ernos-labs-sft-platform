# Exact defect chemistry and non-stoichiometry law

## WHY

INORG-016 must represent a missing occupant without turning absence into a number, and it must compare reference and observed composition without negative or fitted stoichiometric values.

## DERIVATION

One finite local motif retains every reference site and expected species. Every site has exactly one occupancy record: a retained species occurrence or structural `EmptyOne`. Interstitial occurrences are additional explicit sites. Exact occurrence reconciliation creates separate positive missing and added supports; it never produces a signed difference. Gcd reduction gives unique primitive reference and observed formulas. Vacancy, substitution and interstitial classes follow from the support, while foreign observed species force extrinsic origin and reference-member-only interruptions force intrinsic origin.
