# Exact metal-cluster and metal-bonding law

## WHY

INORG-014 must distinguish cluster grouping, direct metal bonding and ligand bridging without assuming that every cluster is held together by either bond class.

## DERIVATION

One cluster retains at least two distinct metal centres and the complete connected relation graph. Direct metal bonds, bridge-supported paths and held grouping relations are separate. Each class has positive support or structural `EmptyOne`. A fresh centre attached by one relation preserves connectedness and increments the centre count once.

## CHECK

The 256-form grammar has one depth-independent survivor and an independent connected-graph reconstruction. Ten frozen IUPAC rows preserve generic cluster topology, the non-necessity of bonding, iron–sulfur bridging, two named composition examples and external charge inscriptions downstream only.
