## Evidence boundary

Three kinds of external evidence must remain distinct:

1. Authoritative terminology correspondence tests whether a sealed structural
   definition preserves the distinctions recorded by IUPAC.  This is not a
   measured numerical law.
2. Measured-value validation compares a sealed exact consequence with a
   source-bound CIAAW or NIST record, including its interval or uncertainty.
   The target remains inaccessible until the derivation and prediction are
   sealed.
3. Smithium, the g-block beyond the known table and the proposed periodic
   endpoint are pre-observation predictions.  Agreement with the known table
   can validate their generated predecessor law, but the unobserved successors
   cannot be described as measured.

Generic labels generated by the predictor are not external observations.  A
claim cannot enter the model merely because its predicted label was copied into
its own target row.
