# Medicine and Health Sciences branch roadmap

## Repository prerequisite

Medicine now has its registered `sft/medicine/` scientific directory,
categorical `SFT-MED-` prefix, frozen foundational inventory and publication
boundary.  Its first seventy-two laws are admitted through the canonical sealed
engine and the branch is current-evidence closed at that declared foundation,
while remaining explicitly open to lawful correction, falsification and
extension.  This roadmap records the larger field still to be reconstructed; it
does not itself create additional scientific or admission state.

## Scientific ownership

Medicine owns health, disease, diagnosis, intervention and patient- or
population-level outcomes.  Biology supplies living mechanisms; Chemistry and
Materials supply substances and devices; Medicine must still establish its own
clinical claims with purpose-matched evidence.

## Layer One — foundational reconstruction

The foundational Medicine paper must establish:

1. patient, population, health state and clinical observation;
2. normal variation, dysfunction, disease and injury boundaries;
3. symptom, sign, measurement and diagnostic classification;
4. cause, association, mechanism and confounding distinctions;
5. risk, prognosis and outcome under exact observation classes;
6. intervention, comparator and counterfactual boundary;
7. dose, exposure, response and adverse event;
8. efficacy, effectiveness, safety and benefit-harm composition;
9. clinical-trial, observational-study and evidence-synthesis structures;
10. screening, prevention, treatment and rehabilitation;
11. individual versus population inference; and
12. ethical, consent, privacy and uncertainty boundaries for empirical work.

## Layer Two — complete field reconstruction

The complete field census covers:

- anatomy, physiology and pathology at the medical boundary;
- epidemiology, biostatistics and causal clinical inference;
- diagnostics, imaging, laboratory medicine and biomarkers;
- pharmacology, pharmacokinetics, toxicology and therapeutics;
- infectious disease and antimicrobial response;
- immunological, genetic, metabolic and developmental disorders;
- cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, gastrointestinal and endocrine medicine;
- neurology, psychiatry and behavioural health with explicit handoffs to
  Consciousness and Cognitive Science;
- oncology, haematology and cellular pathology;
- dermatology, musculoskeletal, rheumatological and autoimmune medicine;
- reproductive, maternal, neonatal, paediatric and ageing medicine;
- surgery, anaesthesia, critical care, trauma and rehabilitation;
- dentistry, vision, hearing and other specialist systems;
- emergency medicine, primary care, family medicine and integrated care;
- nursing, allied health and care-process evidence at their scientific boundary;
- public, occupational, environmental and global health;
- nutrition and preventive medicine;
- personalised and precision medicine;
- health-services research, implementation evidence and healthcare-system
  outcomes without treating institutional policy as a biological law;
- clinical decision theory, health informatics and verified medical AI;
- medical devices and biomaterials at the Engineering handoff; and
- evidence quality, replication, adverse outcomes and limits of generalization.

## Publication sequence

The first Medicine paper was published after completion of the branch scaffold,
763-entry ownership audit and all seventy-two foundational clinical evidence
admissions at
[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21630785](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21630785).
Later versions expand specialty by specialty. No medical claim is accepted
merely because a biological mechanism or predictive model appears plausible.

## Handoffs

Medicine consumes Biology, Chemistry, Materials, Information and Computation.
It exports validated health constraints to Engineering and Social Systems while
preserving patient-level ethical and empirical boundaries.
