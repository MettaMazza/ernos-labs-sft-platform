# Astronomy and Cosmology branch roadmap

## Scientific ownership

Astronomy and Cosmology own astronomical objects, populations, observations and
cosmic history.  Universal propagation, gravity, matter and physical constants
remain Physics-owned dependencies.  Contingent initial conditions and observed
catalogues are not relabelled as premise-free laws.

## Layer One — foundational reconstruction

Status: current-evidence complete and extension-open. All 72 required laws are
engine-admitted from 18,432 enumerated candidates, with 72 unique survivors,
288 adverse-control passes, 72 implementation-distinct reconstructions and 72
post-seal comparisons. The paper and evidence release are published at
[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21640812](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21640812) and
[GitHub release v1.0.0](https://github.com/MettaMazza/ernos-labs-sft-platform/releases/tag/astronomy-cosmology-v1.0.0).

The foundational paper must establish:

1. source, observer, signal, distance and observation relations;
2. astronomical object identity and classification;
3. luminosity, spectrum, motion and temporal record;
4. stellar formation, structure, evolution and terminal states;
5. planetary, satellite and small-body systems;
6. interstellar medium, chemical enrichment and star formation;
7. galactic structure, dynamics and populations;
8. compact objects and high-energy sources;
9. large-scale structure and cosmic expansion records;
10. early-history, background and abundance observations;
11. dark-sector, horizon and multiverse claims with exact admissibility
    boundaries; and
12. law, historical inference, standing prediction and unobserved-domain
    distinctions.

## Layer Two — complete field reconstruction

The full census covers:

- observational methods, calibration, selection effects and survey inference;
- astrometry, celestial mechanics and time-domain observation;
- solar and stellar physics, populations, nucleosynthesis and remnants;
- planetary science, exoplanets, atmospheres and habitability;
- interstellar and circumgalactic media, astrochemistry and dust;
- galactic formation, morphology, dynamics and evolution;
- black holes, neutron stars, transients and high-energy astrophysics;
- solar, heliospheric and space-weather systems at the Earth handoff;
- gravitational-wave, neutrino, cosmic-ray and multi-messenger astronomy;
- galaxy clusters, lensing and large-scale structure;
- expansion history, distance ladder and cosmological parameter observations;
- cosmic background, thermal history and primordial abundances;
- structure formation, dark matter and dark-energy observations;
- early-universe, inflation-equivalent and origin hypotheses;
- relativistic cosmology, alternative expansion structures and model
  discriminators derived only after their SFT laws exist;
- horizon, topology, cyclicity and ultimate-fate questions;
- astrobiology and technosignature searches at declared evidence boundaries;
- unexplained observations and alternative model discriminators; and
- exact separation of universal law from source-dated cosmic history.

## Publication sequence

The foundational edition joins admitted physical laws to astronomical
observation without allowing data to select the formal survivor.  Later versions
add complete object, population and cosmology families.  Missing observations
remain standing tests, not manufactured confirmations or dismissals.

## Handoffs

Astronomy consumes Physics, Chemistry, Materials, Biology and Earth Science as
applicable.  Engineering owns instruments; instrument data and calibration
remain part of astronomical evidence custody.
