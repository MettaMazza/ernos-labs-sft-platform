# Engineering Translation branch roadmap

## Scientific ownership

Engineering Translation turns admitted laws into bounded designs, instruments,
processes and demonstrations.  It does not own or select the fundamental laws
it uses.  A failed implementation may falsify a claimed operating boundary; a
successful implementation cannot retroactively force the law.

## Layer One — foundational reconstruction

Status: current-evidence complete and extension-open. All 72 required laws are
engine-admitted from 18,432 enumerated candidates, with 72 unique survivors,
288 adverse-control passes, 72 implementation-distinct reconstructions and 72
post-seal comparisons. The paper and evidence release are published at
[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21640816](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21640816) and
[GitHub release v1.0.0](https://github.com/MettaMazza/ernos-labs-sft-platform/releases/tag/engineering-translation-v1.0.0).

The foundational Engineering paper must establish:

1. requirement, function, constraint and operating boundary;
2. component, interface, architecture and system composition;
3. resource, efficiency, reliability and failure;
4. measurement, calibration and acceptance test;
5. design alternative, trade-off and optimization boundary;
6. control, feedback, stability and fault response;
7. safety, hazard, resilience and fail-safe operation;
8. verification, validation and traceability;
9. reproducibility across macOS, Windows and Linux where software is involved;
10. accessibility without heavy infrastructure dependencies;
11. separation of scientific evidence from design performance; and
12. lawful return of a discovered anomaly to its categorical science owner.

## Layer Two — complete translation programme

The complete engineering tree covers:

- systems and requirements engineering;
- mechanical and manufacturing systems;
- electrical, electronic and photonic systems;
- computer hardware, software, networking and control;
- telecommunications, signal, embedded and cyber-physical systems;
- civil, structural, transport and infrastructure systems;
- chemical, process and industrial systems;
- agricultural, food and biological-process systems;
- materials selection, processing and lifecycle engineering;
- aerospace, space and marine systems;
- nuclear, geological, mining and subsurface systems;
- energy generation, storage, transmission and conversion;
- environmental, geological and resource engineering;
- biomedical, clinical and assistive systems;
- robotics, autonomy and human-machine systems;
- microtechnology, nanotechnology and precision systems;
- measurement instruments, laboratories and scientific tooling;
- safety, reliability, security, maintainability and resilience;
- sustainable design and full lifecycle evidence; and
- bounded frontier applications including the fresh Fold Protein, Fold Chess,
  Fold Go and Unison AI rebuilds when their prerequisite branches are ready.

## Publication sequence

The foundational edition defines the translation constitution and evidence
boundary.  Later versions document complete engineering domains and reproducible
demonstrations.  Each application paper names every upstream receipt, design
choice, operating limit, test, failure and external result.

## Final V4 handoff

When the V3 knowledge tree is sufficiently complete under its explicit census,
Engineering Translation supports—but does not independently authorize—the
fourth clean-room reconstruction in SFT-derived self-hosted language, compiler,
runtime and proof principles.
