# Mathematics branch roadmap

## Scientific ownership

Mathematics owns exact structures and proof relations forced from Foundation.
It does not import conventional axioms as SFT premises and does not own physical
measurements merely because they are expressed mathematically.

## Layer One — foundational reconstruction

The foundational Mathematics paper must establish a connected kernel across:

1. exact arithmetic and number structure;
2. finite collections, relations, maps and induction;
3. combinatorial generation and recurrence;
4. graphs, paths, reachability, cycles, cuts and networks;
5. algebraic structures and witnessed operation properties;
6. order, partial order, conditional totality, meet and join;
7. finite geometry, incidence, topology and continuity;
8. exact support-based probability and statistics;
9. optimization, dominance and exact bounds;
10. finite dynamical systems, recurrence and stability;
11. logic, proof and formal consequence; and
12. category, type and compositional structures where forced.

The scientific calculator is an admitted translation and law explorer, not an
alternate admission route.  Calculator output can become submission evidence
only when the proposed claim separately satisfies the complete engine protocol.

## Layer Two — complete field reconstruction

The field census expands through these families:

- arithmetic, divisibility, fractions, recurrence, sequences and exact number
  theory;
- finite extensions corresponding to algebraic-number constructions, with
  irrational or complex conventional objects represented only by certified
  exact structures or enclosures rather than imported proof scalars;
- enumerative, algebraic, extremal, probabilistic-correspondence and structural
  combinatorics;
- graph, hypergraph, network, matroid and finite relational theory;
- linear algebra, multilinear structure, tensors, matrices and exact spectral
  correspondence;
- groups, monoids, semigroups, rings, modules, fields, algebras, representation
  and homological structures only where their operations are forced;
- order theory, lattices, closure systems, domain structures and fixed-point
  boundaries;
- Euclidean-correspondence, affine, projective, convex, discrete, algebraic,
  differential and computational geometry under lawful finite representation;
- point-set-correspondence, algebraic, geometric and computational topology,
  homotopy structures and computable invariants;
- calculus reconstructed as exact finite difference, accumulation and enclosure
  relations rather than a presumed continuum;
- real-, complex-, functional-, harmonic- and operator-analysis correspondence
  using exact generated supports and convergence certificates;
- ordinary, partial, difference, integral and functional equation structures
  with explicit discretization and convergence boundaries;
- measure, integration and distribution correspondence over generated support;
- exact probability, inference, estimation, hypothesis testing, Bayesian-
  correspondence and finite stochastic correspondence without ontic randomness;
- optimization, operations research, variational structure, control, games and
  decision laws;
- discrete, symbolic, nonlinear and exact dynamical systems, chaos and
  bifurcation correspondence;
- logic, model theory, proof theory, computability interfaces, set-like finite
  collection theory and foundational boundaries;
- category, type, operadic, sheaf-like and compositional mathematics where the
  grammar forces them;
- exact numerical analysis, approximation theory, interval enclosure, stability
  and error propagation;
- symbolic mathematics, equation solving, transforms and constructive methods;
  and
- mathematical interfaces required by Physics, Computation and every later
  science.

Every conventional theorem selected for reconstruction must first be entered as
a question in the field census, assigned a categorical owner and given a lawful
finite or successor boundary.  Unsupported continuum or infinity statements
must be translated to generated finite families, exact enclosures or remain
open with the missing certificate named.

## Publication sequence

The foundational edition documents the common mathematical kernel and the
calculator.  Later versions add complete subject families and their evidence
maps.  The current-knowledge-complete edition requires the whole registered
mathematical census, not merely the original twelve-family inventory.

## Handoffs

Mathematics exports exact structures to Information Science and all downstream
branches.  Natural constants and measured laws remain owned by their empirical
sciences.
