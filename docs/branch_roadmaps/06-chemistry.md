# Chemistry branch roadmap

## Scientific ownership

Chemistry owns chemical identity, composition, bonding, molecular organization,
reaction and chemical transformation.  Physics supplies admitted physical
constants and interaction laws; Materials owns bulk material organization;
Biology owns living organization.

The active continuation checkpoint and immutable receipts, not this roadmap,
define present Chemistry progress.  Pausing the branch does not erase completed
admissions or move its restart position.

## Layer One — foundational reconstruction

The foundational Chemistry paper must establish:

1. chemical entity, species, substance, formula and traceable observation;
2. element, isotope, ion, valence and atomic-weight records;
3. periodic order, recurrence, group, period and observed table boundary;
4. composition as exact retained-identity partition;
5. stoichiometric conservation, primitive balancing and limiting support;
6. chemical bond, covalent, ionic and metallic support;
7. bond order, polarity, length and dissociation distinctions;
8. molecule, geometry, isomerism and intermolecular organization;
9. reaction identity, mechanism, rate, equilibrium and catalysis;
10. acid/base, electronegativity, redox and electrochemistry;
11. thermochemistry, phase and solution foundations; and
12. analytical traceability plus exact branch handoffs.

## Layer Two — complete field reconstruction

The full Chemistry census is organized into the following dependency-ordered
families:

- measurement, identity, nomenclature and analytical traceability;
- elements, isotopes, ions, periodicity and chemical predictions;
- composition, mixtures, solutions and stoichiometry;
- electronic state, orbital-support and quantum-chemical correspondence;
- bonding, molecular geometry, symmetry, spectroscopy and molecular
  properties;
- thermochemistry, chemical thermodynamics, phases and nonideal systems;
- equilibrium, acid/base, redox, electrochemistry and coupled transport;
- reaction dynamics, elementary transitions, mechanisms and kinetics;
- catalysis, surfaces, interfaces, colloids and photochemistry;
- inorganic, coordination, solid-state and organometallic chemistry;
- organic structure, functional groups, stereochemistry and complete reaction
  families;
- polymer, supramolecular and network chemistry;
- analytical, separation, spectrometric and measurement sciences;
- physical chemistry, statistical chemical correspondence and chemical
  dynamics;
- theoretical, mathematical and computational chemistry at the Chemistry-owned
  boundary;
- electrochemistry, sonochemistry, mechanochemistry, flow chemistry and other
  energy-coupled transformation families;
- nanochemistry, cluster chemistry and chemistry at confined scales;
- medicinal, pharmaceutical, food and agricultural chemistry at their exact
  downstream handoff boundaries;
- industrial and process-chemistry laws separated from engineering design;
- atmospheric, environmental, geochemical, astrochemical and nuclear-chemical
  handoffs;
- biomolecular and medicinal-chemistry handoffs without absorbing Biology or
  Medicine; and
- every Chemistry-owned V1/V2 value, standing prediction and adverse result.

External validation must use authoritative chemical sources appropriate to the
claim.  A source identity is captured once where shared, but each consequence
retains its own preregistration, sealed target, comparison and result.  Smithium,
the proposed g-block and any other unobserved result remain standing predictions
until suitable external observations exist.

decisions, 86 unique survivors, 344 controls, 86 independent reconstructions

[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21627782](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21627782)

current-knowledge-complete edition requires the full live Chemistry field
census and does not treat an archived narrower paper or the 176-law present
surface as that finished census.

## Handoffs

Chemistry exports admitted identities, transformations and property relations
to Materials, Biology, Medicine, Earth Science and Astronomy.  Each handoff must
name what is exported, what remains Chemistry-owned and what the recipient must
derive independently.
