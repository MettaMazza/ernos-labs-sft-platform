# Chemistry branch roadmap

## Scientific ownership

Chemistry owns chemical identity, composition, bonding, molecular organization,
reaction and chemical transformation.  Physics supplies admitted physical
constants and interaction laws; Materials owns bulk material organization;
Biology owns living organization.

The active continuation checkpoint and immutable receipts, not this roadmap,
define present Chemistry progress. Chemistry is now complete at **272 of 272**
registered obligations to the current standard and remains open to lawful
extension. Completion does not permanently lock the field against discoveries.
