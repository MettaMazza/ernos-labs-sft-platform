# Physics branch roadmap

## Scientific ownership

Physics owns universal physical relations, physical constants and dimensionless
physical values, measurement consequences, matter, fields, spacetime and
physical cross-domain laws.  A downstream science may consume an admitted
physical value but may not become its substitute owner.

## Layer One — foundational reconstruction

The foundational physical kernel must connect exact Fold structure to sealed
measurement across:

1. observation, measurement and metrology;
2. relational position, change, motion and interaction;
3. force, transfer, conservation and recurrence;
4. fields, sources, propagation and inverse dilution;
5. waves, phase, interference and resonance;
6. thermodynamics, statistical support and retained information;
7. physical quantum correspondence and measurement;
8. matter, particles, nuclei and interactions;
9. spacetime, causal order and gravitation;
10. collective matter, fluids, plasmas and critical systems;
11. physical constants and exact measured-value comparison; and
12. handoff boundaries to Chemistry, Materials and Astronomy.

Every derived physical value must be front-loaded in the paper as a scientific
result, with its exact expression, decimal rendering, measured source, units,
uncertainty relation and post-seal comparison—not hidden behind file names or
hashes.  The hashes remain evidence beneath the result.

## Layer Two — complete field reconstruction

The complete Physics census covers:

- metrology, units, calibration, uncertainty and observation theory;
- kinematics, classical mechanics, analytical-mechanics correspondence,
  elasticity and continuum-bounded mechanics;
- nonlinear dynamics, chaos, stability and complex physical systems;
- electromagnetism, electrodynamics, electronics-law boundaries and field
  interactions;
- geometrical, physical, nonlinear and quantum optics;
- acoustics, vibration, wave propagation, scattering and resonance;
- thermodynamics, statistical physics, kinetic theory and nonequilibrium
  systems;
- quantum foundations, quantum mechanics correspondence, atomic, molecular and
  optical physics at the physical-law boundary;
- particle physics, interactions, symmetries, masses, mixing and decay;
- nuclear structure, reactions, stability and heavy-element boundaries;
- relativity, gravitation, causal structure and gravitational waves;
- condensed-matter, solid-state, low-temperature, mesoscopic and nanoscale
  physical laws before material-specific organization;
- fluid mechanics, aerodynamics-law boundaries, plasma, soft matter, complex
  fluids and critical phenomena;
- biophysical, chemical-physical, geophysical and medical-physics handoffs
  without absorbing their downstream empirical objects;
- astrophysical law interfaces without importing contingent cosmic history;
- all known physical constants and values at their correct categorical owner;
- additional forces, vacuum and absence-state questions, energy-accounting and
  extraction claims, stability islands and other prior-corpus obligations;
- standing predictions with no present measurement, kept distinct from measured
  agreements; and
- newly discovered physical obligations admitted through versioned extension.

## Publication sequence

The current Physics paper and its immutable receipts remain the baseline.
Further versions add only newly admitted physical families, stronger evidence,
corrections or genuinely new current-knowledge obligations.  No roadmap edit
reopens or repeats an admitted claim; the live checkpoint identifies the next
real extension.

## Handoffs

Physics exports constants and universal relations.  Chemical identity belongs
to Chemistry; material organization to Materials; objects and cosmic history
to Astronomy and Cosmology; planetary records to Earth and Environmental
Sciences.
