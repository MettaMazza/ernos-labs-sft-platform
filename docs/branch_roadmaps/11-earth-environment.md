# Earth and Environmental Sciences branch roadmap

## Scientific ownership

This branch owns Earth history, planetary-scale coupled systems and
environmental observations.  It consumes universal Physics, chemical identities,
material properties and biological processes while retaining its own contingent
records and system-level laws.

## Layer One — foundational reconstruction

The foundational paper must establish:

1. Earth-system object, reservoir, boundary and observation;
2. geological material, layer, cycle and historical record;
3. planetary energy, matter and information budgets;
4. atmosphere, ocean, land, ice and biosphere composition;
5. transport, mixing, circulation and coupled feedback;
6. weather, climate and timescale distinctions;
7. rock, mineral, soil and sediment transformations;
8. tectonic, volcanic and seismic processes;
9. water cycle and hydrological organization;
10. biogeochemical and ecological coupling;
11. proxy, reconstruction, model and forecast evidence classes; and
12. hazard, uncertainty, attribution and falsification boundaries.

## Layer Two — complete field reconstruction

The complete field census covers:

- geology, mineralogy, petrology, sedimentology and stratigraphy;
- geophysics, geodesy, geomagnetism and planetary interior;
- plate tectonics, volcanology, seismology and mountain building;
- geochemistry, isotope records and biogeochemical cycles;
- palaeontology and Earth-history interfaces with Biology;
- soils, geomorphology, erosion and landscape dynamics;
- physical geography, cartography, geospatial measurement and Earth observation;
- hydrology, groundwater, rivers, lakes and cryosphere;
- oceanography, circulation, marine chemistry and ecosystems;
- atmospheric science, meteorology and weather;
- climate dynamics, palaeoclimate, forcing, feedback and attribution;
- ecology, biodiversity and coupled human-natural systems at the Earth boundary;
- pollution, toxic transport and environmental chemistry;
- freshwater, marine and terrestrial environmental quality;
- natural hazards, risk and resilience;
- resource cycles, sustainability and environmental accounting;
- remote sensing, field measurement, data assimilation and model validation;
- human-environment interaction at the explicit Social Systems handoff;
- planetary comparison at the Astronomy handoff; and
- contingent history distinguished from universal law.

## Publication sequence

The foundational edition establishes coupled Earth-system laws and evidence
classes.  Later versions expand discipline by discipline and retain source-
dated observational records.  Forecast performance alone cannot establish an
opaque law; the premise-to-result relation must remain inspectable.

## Handoffs

Earth Science exports planetary and environmental constraints to Social Systems,
Medicine and Engineering.  Other planets and cosmic populations remain owned
by Astronomy and Cosmology.
