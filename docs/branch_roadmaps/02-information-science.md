# Information Science branch roadmap

## Scientific ownership

Information Science owns distinguishability, representation, uncertainty and
lawful transformation of information.  It stops at information support where
operational quantum dynamics belongs to Quantum Computation.

## Layer One — foundational reconstruction

The foundational kernel derives:

1. symbols and distinguishability;
2. observation classes and retained distinctions;
3. encoding and decoding;
4. exact information quantity;
5. entropy and uncertainty from complete support;
6. compression and redundancy;
7. channels and capacity;
8. noise and error;
9. coding and correction conditions;
10. mutual and conditional information;
11. conservation, loss and transformation; and
12. classical, probabilistic and quantum-support correspondence.

Every quantity must be an exact count, part or generated symbolic enclosure.
An analytic logarithm may appear only as a typed correspondence unless its form
is independently forced inside the SFT domain.

## Layer Two — complete field reconstruction

The full field programme covers:

- alphabets, codes, grammars and representation equivalence;
- data, metadata, records, provenance and representation integrity;
- source, sequence, process, spatial and network information;
- combinatorial, operational, descriptive and algorithmic information;
- sampling, quantization, signal representation and reconstruction boundaries;
- lossless and lossy compression with exact distortion boundaries;
- single-user, multi-user, broadcast, multiple-access, relay and feedback
  channels;
- capacity under exact resource and observation classes;
- noise transport, detection, estimation and correction;
- block, convolutional, network, erasure and adversarial coding structures;
- conditional, mutual, directed and shared-information relations;
- sufficient records, information bottlenecks and lawful coarse-graining;
- indexing, retrieval, ranking and knowledge-organization measures at the
  information-law boundary;
- inference, detection, filtering and estimation as information transforms;
- privacy, secrecy and leakage interfaces with Cryptography;
- information thermodynamics and retained-record costs;
- classical, deterministic-support, probabilistic and quantum information
  correspondence; and
- biological, semantic and social-information handoffs without treating meaning
  as a raw symbol count; and
- empirical validation against communication and coding data where the law
  produces an observable consequence.

## Publication sequence

The foundational paper fixes the exact information vocabulary and core laws.
Later versions add complete channel, coding, compression and information-
measure families.  Quantum phase, gates, entanglement dynamics and fault
tolerance remain cited downstream rather than duplicated.

## Handoffs

The branch exports representation and information-resource laws to Classical
Computation, Quantum Computation, Biology, Consciousness, Social Systems and
Engineering Translation.
