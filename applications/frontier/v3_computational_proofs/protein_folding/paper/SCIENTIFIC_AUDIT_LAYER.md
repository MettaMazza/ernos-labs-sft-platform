## 5. Corrected and adverse records

Every record in this section is bound to the method and chronology that
produced it. A later constructor or solver may supersede the operational route
without changing the historical result. The incomplete full six-axis predictor
has not produced a primary empirical row, so none of the development results is
presented as a standalone verdict on that system.

### 5.1 V2 generalisation boundary

The published V2 24-structure result predominantly sampled one structural
family and used a target-derived material relation. Its median `tm_repo` was
`0.9255486262`; that value remains historically preserved. The later fresh
diverse four-target V2 panel returned mean TM `0.0742934939`, median TM
`0.0781022432`, maximum TM `0.0987934258` and median RMSD95 `14.117 A`. The
generalisation control is adverse.

### 5.2 Generic transition boundary

The reversible one-coordinate relation retains the whole tested glycine fibre
as one recurrent class. This is an adverse result for fold discrimination, not
a failure of transition enumeration.

### 5.3 Carbonyl correction

The original whole-chain coordinate mapping produced seven atom pairs below
`0.5 A`; its closest retained pair was `O(4)`–`N(5)` at approximately
`0.14477577631934946 A`. The corrected trans-peptide-plane rule produces zero
pairs below `1 A` on the same carrier. The original archive and the
pre-summary path-normalisation abort remain preserved. The correction changes
oxygen coordinates only and therefore leaves the C-alpha evaluation unchanged.

### 5.5 Six-coordinate result and later reconciliation

All six structural laws are current. At the historical v3 census, two exact
torsion-dependent partial coordinates supplied lawful progress but none of the
six families supplied a complete executable protein coordinate; there were
zero source-transport failures. The later authorised joint-microstate route
lawfully replaces those missing scalar magnitudes with six separately typed
preserving relation axes. All six are now constructed in the registered form,
and the exact fixed-geometry compiler and generic solver are verified. The
full-solver status nevertheless remains unavailable because the complete
fixed-geometry frontier and arbitrary-sequence torsion–protonation–solvent
recurrence have not been executed. The reconciliation is recorded in
`audits/full_coordinate_completion_reconciliation_v4.json`.

### 5.6 Sequence-only development transfers

The fresh 4B19 baseline was sealed before target access and was adverse. A
post-target diagnosis selected the exact canonical alpha state under a strict
hydrophobic-majority relation. Its first unseen transfer, 8HJC, retained six
cysteines and was adverse, preserving the rule's missing disulphide-topology
boundary. A narrowed rule adding `count(C) <= One` was frozen before selecting
1JDM. That unseen 31-residue target produced median official TM-score `0.45609`,
median lDDT-C-alpha `0.7322201390516365` and median C-alpha RMSD95
`4.068700185717891 A` over all 16 deposited NMR models. The result is positive
blind development evidence for the narrow registered class. It is not the
certified full-V3 predictor, a primary campaign row or AlphaFold parity.

### 5.7 Generalised partial-coordinate recurrence

The target-free recurrence implementation accepts arbitrary registered protein
sequences and conservatively retains interval-overlap states as incomparable.
It closes on all three real-sequence development controls and every closure
passes independent verification. The matched post-closure panel contains 46
comparisons. Relative to the preserved earlier evaluations, median TM-score
changes by `+0.06279` on 4B19, `-0.042745` on 8HJC and `-0.210905` on 1JDM.
Consequently, computational recurrence generalisation is demonstrated at this
partial-coordinate boundary, while structural-accuracy generalisation is not.
No current run is blind and no AlphaFold comparator row exists.

### 5.8 Solver-control reconciliation

The depth-12 independent-extrema census, degree-strengthened depth-8 census and
complete 59-stratum census were adverse for pruning: none excluded a registered
surface. The initial spatial-hierarchy repulsion floor was also weaker than the
predecessor degree floor, and its overbroad label was corrected. Subsequent
cardinality-RLT work raised the certified repulsion floor from
`147.619752821...` to `192.197997235382...`. Coupled-envelope and
attraction-conditioned RLT controls still failed to close the highest band, so
the exact threshold census evaluated all 583 registered occupations, dominated
573 and retained 10. This closes the high-attraction slice and operationally
supersedes those failed bounds at that boundary; all adverse control records
remain preserved, and the lower bands remain open.
