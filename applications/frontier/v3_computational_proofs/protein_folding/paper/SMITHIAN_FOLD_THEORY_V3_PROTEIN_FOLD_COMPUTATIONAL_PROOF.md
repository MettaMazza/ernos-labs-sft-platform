## 4. Mathematical constitution

### 4.1 Sequence and condition carrier

Let a sequence be the ordered word

\[
S=(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n), \qquad a_i\in\mathcal A,
\]

where `A` contains the twenty registered standard amino-acid identities. Each
position retains chirality, modification, ambiguity and source fields. The
primary campaign admits explicit L chirality for non-glycine residues and an
achiral record for glycine. A condition carrier retains temperature, pressure,
pH, solvent, ligand, method and source fields; unavailable fields remain
unavailable rather than receiving defaults.

### 4.2 Torsion-word surface

Every registered backbone or side-chain rotor is assigned one of twenty-four
ordered sectors. If `r(S)` is the complete side-chain rotor count, the word
width is

\[
k(S)=3(n-1)+r(S),
\]

and the raw finite word count is

\[
N_{\mathrm{word}}(S)=24^{k(S)}.
\]

The product is represented lazily; its size is not confused with an executed
exhaustion. Exact sector reversal and source-graph automorphism actions are
retained. No word is selected by a target score, hash order or lexicographic
identity.

### 4.3 Source-bound coordinate emission

Backbone coordinates use the registered CCP4/PROCHECK residue-class means for
`N–CA`, `CA–C`, `C–N`, `N–CA–C`, `CA–C–N` and `C–N–CA`. Side-chain heavy atoms
retain the wwPDB Chemical Component Dictionary ideal-reference shape in the
local `N–CA–C` frame. The source values are conventional coordinate records;
they are not exact SFT proof values.

The first implementation left a carbonyl oxygen in its local frame while the
successor nitrogen moved with the peptide torsion. The resulting seven
sub-`0.5 A` atom pairs were detected by the complete interaction ledger. The
corrected rule retains the same source `C–O` length and `CA–C–O` angle and
places each nonterminal oxygen trans to the successor nitrogen in the peptide
plane. The defective carrier and its archive remain preserved.

### 4.4 Complete atom-pair custody

For `m` retained heavy atoms, every unordered pair is emitted:

\[
N_{\mathrm{pair}}=\binom m2=\frac{m(m-1)}2.
\]

Every pair retains atom identity, residue position, elements, source formal
charge inscriptions, shortest polymer-graph separation, residue separation and
the source-bound squared distance as a canonical floating-point hexadecimal
record. No contact or distance cut-off is applied.

The complete shared-atom correlation count is

\[
N_{\mathrm{corr}}=m\binom{m-1}{2}
=\frac{m(m-1)(m-2)}2.
\]

For the corrected 4APD control, `m=239`, so
`N_pair=28,441` and `N_corr=6,740,517`. Endpoint incidence is exactly
`2N_pair=56,882`.

## 7. Derivational and implementation sequence

### 7.1 Exact local geometry and polymer graph

The kernel begins with canonical sequence identity, exact rational direction
grammar, local `N–CA–C` frames, explicit chirality and peptide adjacency. The
standard peptide graph removes the registered terminal leaving atoms, retains
the released water carrier and adds every ordered peptide `C–N` bond. Exact

### 7.2 Side-chain closure programme

Successive registered grammars close alanine and glycine, cysteine and serine,
valine, threonine, proline, isoleucine, leucine, aspartate and asparagine,
glutamate and glutamine, methionine, lysine, arginine, phenylalanine, tyrosine,

equal-unit adverse results, mirror-closure failures, storage and preflight

uniform current-baseline catalogue covers all twenty residue codes; it does not
claim that one ideal reference is a universal physical conformer.

### 7.3 Rotor, word and sector mapping

side-chain variables. Polymer templates append `phi`, `psi` and `omega`. The
lazy 24-sector word surface preserves complete variable order, exact reversal
and source-graph symmetry. The sector renderer preserves every source-graph
bond distance, rotation-axis endpoint and fixed atom over 912 single-rotor
cases. Primary and independent reconstructions agree.

### 7.4 Scale, condition and interface custody

Seventeen geometry and condition records map to 60 local assignments and 1,600
ordered residue interfaces. The complete plural protonation surface contains

The exact named-proton graph adds 114 strict local edges across 163 states and
preserves 55 isolated states; all 12,996 donor-by-acceptor templates remain in
the audit archive. This closes occurrence and lawful local-transfer custody but
deliberately leaves condition-specific selection and hydrogen geometry open.

### 7.5 Whole-chain carrier and correction

The word renderer composes the registered backbone means, peptide interfaces,
local CCD reference shapes and torsion sectors. Its first full development

same control while leaving every C-alpha coordinate and metric unchanged.

### 7.6 Interaction ledger and execution gate

The interaction ledger emits all atom pairs and both endpoint incidences,
classifying graph separation without assigning a physical interaction class.
Formal charges are not promoted to partial charges. The historical partial

fixed-geometry frontier and the torsion–protonation–solvent recurrence remain

### 7.7 Evaluator and parity decision

The local evaluator computes lDDT-C-alpha, C-alpha RMSD95, Kabsch C-alpha RMSD,
dRMSD and the explicitly labelled historical `tm_repo` diagnostic. `tm_repo`
performs one full-chain Kabsch fit and is not substituted for the registered
external full-chain TM-score. Official TM-align version 20220412 is now
source-captured, compiled and hash-bound. Its campaign normalisation is the full
registered sequence length supplied with `-u`; every deposited NMR model is
retained. The 100-row decision kernel requires twenty targets in each of five
length strata and retains complete, failed, invalid and unavailable rows.

Parity is a strict conjunction: V3 representative coverage must be 100/100;
median full-chain TM-score and lDDT-C-alpha must meet or exceed AlphaFold 3;
median C-alpha RMSD95 must be no worse; V3 must win or tie on TM-score and
lDDT-C-alpha on at least 50 targets; median TM-score must meet or exceed the
comparator in every length stratum; and no row may be censored or replaced.
