## Restricted development corpus

DailyDialog source and derived development rows are held under the declared
`CC BY-NC-SA 4.0` local, non-commercial development boundary. Corpus rows,
source archives and bulk teacher-signal derivatives are excluded from the
ordinary paper/release payload. Their registered identities and custody records
remain in the local project evidence and reproducibility record.

## Frozen GPT-2 reference and teacher material

The project records hashes and functional references to the frozen official
GPT-2 materials. It copies no teacher parameter into the UnisonFold-native
model and distributes no GPT-2 weights in this preliminary release package.
The applicable upstream notices are retained in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and
the provenance registry.
