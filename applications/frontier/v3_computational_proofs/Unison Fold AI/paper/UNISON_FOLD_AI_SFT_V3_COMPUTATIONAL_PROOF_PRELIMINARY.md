## Abstract

Conversational language modelling tests whether an architecture can preserve
finite symbol identity, causal order, selective attention, bounded memory,
learning and generation while remaining inspectable at its mathematical
boundary. This paper reports the preliminary Smithian Fold Theory (SFT) V3
Unison Fold AI computational proof programme. Its one-to-one requirement is
narrow and explicit: the functional operation roles of a GPT-2-class
attention-based causal transformer are translated into the registered SFT
mathematical domain. It is not a copy of Unison AI V2, GPT-2 code, GPT-2
weights, GPT-2 dimensions, or GPT-2 numerical functions.

The registered operation inventory contains 40 rows. Thirty-eight cover the
reference tokenizer, parameter, representation, transformer, training and
generation path; two are downstream conversational and Discord application
operations. Every row names an SFT form, interface, dependency route and
required falsification check. Existing project records classify the 40-row
implementation as passing at its declared finite operation boundary. The
architecture retains a fresh reversible byte-BPE tokeniser. The architecture
also retains bounded causal context, token and position carriers, pre-layer
normalisation, joint query/key/value projection, masked multi-head attention,
residual junctions, a nonlinear feed-forward path, a tied language-model head,
causal learning, cached autoregression and finite generation. Conventional
exponential softmax and tanh GELU are not imported as proof premises. Their
function roles are translated respectively by exact finite normalisation and
an oriented rational gate, and numerical equality with GPT-2 is not claimed.

The translation grammar crosses eight binary mathematical choices. All
`2^8 = 256` candidates were generated, all were decided, 255 were
eliminated, and the unique preserving survivor is
`UNISON-TRANS-001`. The current dependency inventory contains 27 registered

## Detailed preliminary-results synopsis

The programme begins from a separation that earlier Unison work did not hold
reliably: architecture, learning evidence, conversational evidence and
transport evidence are different objects. A Discord reply can show that a
socket works without showing that a transformer organ exists. An exact
forward trace can show that an attention mechanism executes without showing
that its learned distribution supports a useful conversation. A frozen
teacher can provide behavioural supervision without becoming the student's
architecture, parameters or runtime. The V3 programme therefore binds each
statement to the smallest evidence class that warrants it.

The architecture reference is the frozen published and archived GPT-2
functional path. The reference may supply operation names, order, tensor
interfaces and causal behaviour. It may not supply SFT laws, survivor
selection, source code for the V3 implementation, vocabulary, merge ranks,
pretrained parameters, answers or runtime responses. Unison AI V2 is even
narrower: it is a read-only observational comparison object used to retain
earlier failure modes. V2 is not the template being reproduced.

The SFT translation places every proof-facing scalar in a carrier of
structural absence or an oriented positive rational part. Counts, labels,

exact attention scale requires an integral square root of head width.
Layer-normalisation square roots are held by rational enclosure.

The candidate census makes these commitments testable. It combines the
allowed and prohibited choices for scalar carrier, attention normalisation,

The old widest exact artefact is important because it separates proof of

The route to manual testing is therefore finite but unfinished. The active

## 5. Mathematical constitution

### 5.1 Exact scalar and tensor carrier

Every proof-facing scalar is either structural absence or an orientation
paired with a positive rational magnitude:

`OrientedPart = absent | (positive, q) | (negative, q)`, where
`q` is a positive fraction.

Host-language integer zero may implement absence but is not serialised as an
SFT number. Tensor shapes, indices, positions, layer identities, head
identities and vocabulary identities are generated finite carriers. No
floating equality, completed infinity or hidden irrational value is accepted
as proof evidence.

### 5.2 Exact finite attention normalisation

For the complete causally available compatibility support, let `m` be the
retained minimum and let `d_i` be the exact oriented distance of score
`i` above `m`. The translation assigns:

`w_i = (d_i + One) / sum_j(d_j + One)`.

Every available item receives a positive part, ties remain ties, ordering is
monotone and the complete support composes exactly to One. Future positions
are absent from the available support but remain named in the exact
proof-trace exclusion ledger. This `FINITE_NORMALIZE` operation serves the
role of attention normalisation; it is not numerically equal to exponential
softmax.

### 5.3 Oriented rational feed-forward gate

The pointwise nonlinear organ is retained by `ORIENTED_GATE`. For a
positive input of magnitude `q`, the gate part is
`(One + q)/(Two + q)`. For a negative input it is
`One/(Two + q)`. Structural absence remains absent. Multiplication by the
input preserves orientation and supplies a rational nonlinearity. It is a
function-role translation, not a numerical claim about tanh GELU.

### 5.4 Normalisation, scaling and causal exclusion

Layer normalisation retains exact mean centring, a rational square-root
enclosure and the selected rational representative before applying its affine
scale and bias. Attention divides by the registered integral square root of
`head_width`; a non-square head width is rejected at this boundary.
Causal masking names every future candidate and records why it is excluded
rather than assigning a negative numeric sentinel.

### 5.5 Ordered model path

For one finite token word, the sole model path is:

`TOK → CTX → token/position REP → [BLK × layer_count] → final LN → tied HEAD → causal selection → append`.

Each block is:

`LN → QKV → split heads → causal attention → merge → projection → residual → LN → expansion → oriented gate → projection → residual`.

## 11. Learning constitution

Existing readiness audit v1 states that serving and exact learning
observations mutate and invoke the same active native transformer path. This
paper adopts that recorded boundary. Causal examples are formed from the
reversible conversation serialisation, and the response-only objective
retains the user prompt in the forward context while masking it from the
assistant-target loss. Every accepted exact update must bind its before and
after model identities, development examples, objective,
rounding/enclosure records and parent lineage.

A floating process may propose parameters for engineering efficiency. Such a
proposal is explicitly non-proof. The current proposal code uses PyTorch
floating tensors, conventional square root and softmax, cross-entropy and
Adam; these are declared optimisation instruments, not SFT proof scalars or
the Discord runtime law. The proposal becomes eligible only after conversion
to the exact rational carrier and comparison under the registered exact
fixed-grid objective. Exact local coordinate closure retains unchanged,
positive-junction and negative-junction candidates. The saved model, tokenizer
and receipt form one identified bundle.

That exact acceptance objective is a 40-example causal target-margin check;
it does not independently reconstruct the floating teacher soft-mass loss.
Useful transfer from the teacher is consequently an empirical question for
the frozen conversation and comparator gates, neither of which has a passing
active result at this snapshot.

Holdout identities, frozen conversation prompts and manual observations are
prohibited from proposal training and exact calibration. A later online
learning successor must preserve its parent bundle and complete ordering
record. Online learning is disabled for the first manual observation so that
the tested artefact remains fixed.

## 23. Conclusion

Unison Fold AI has reached an exact but deliberately bounded result. The
functional computation of a GPT-2-class causal transformer is represented by
40 registered operation-role translations, supported by 27 direct SFT
dependencies. An eight-axis grammar generates 256 complete translation
candidates and leaves one unique preserving survivor. An identified
eight-layer, eight-head native artefact executes that path, improves the
registered exact objective after quantisation and passes independent forward
reconstruction.

The major conflation resolved by the programme is the difference between
mechanism and conversation. The verified artefact still failed unseen
conversation, and it remains rejected for hand-off. Eleven failures are
preserved. The response is further learning, not a scripted answer, response
table, runtime teacher or copied parameter set.

The active rebuild distils finite behavioural evidence from frozen GPT-2 124M
into a fresh native student while copying zero teacher parameters. It has no
scientific result in this version and, once accepted, must persist without the
teacher. Exact acceptance, independent verification, conversational readiness,
comparison and manual Discord evidence remain open for the active candidate.

The current evidence therefore establishes an auditable SFT translation and
exact proof-trace implementation mechanism at the boundary recorded by the
existing project evidence. No GPT-2 parity, conversational upgrade, newly
admitted SFT law or completed conversational model follows. That narrower
result is the correct computational-proof statement to carry into the next
amendment.
