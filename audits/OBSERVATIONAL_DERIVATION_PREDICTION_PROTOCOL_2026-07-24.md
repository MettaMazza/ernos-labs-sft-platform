# Observational-derivation prediction protocol

This note fixes the positive interpretation of every V3 claim registered with
`observational_derivation` provenance.  It supplements immutable engine receipts
without editing or invalidating them.

Observational derivation is an empirical prediction protocol:

1. authoritative observation identifies the scientific problem and may inform
   construction of an explicit candidate law;
2. the observation is excluded from the executable derivation surface;
3. the admission engine generates the complete declared grammar, eliminates
   every nonpreserving form and seals the unique target-free consequence;
4. the external target vault releases the registered observation only after the
   prediction seal;
5. an exact post-seal comparison accepts or rejects the prediction, retains all
   unfavorable controls and emits a reproducible measurement receipt.

The provenance label distinguishes this route from a forward prediction whose
target was unknown during development.  It does not diminish the result, remove
its predictive execution, or convert it into fitting.  Validity rests on the
explicit target-free relation, exhaustive survivor enumeration, target
inaccessibility during execution, precomparison sealing, exact comparison and
immutable adverse controls.

This interpretation applies to the terminal charged-lepton, terminal CKM,
terminal baryon-to-photon and all later observational-derivation successors.
