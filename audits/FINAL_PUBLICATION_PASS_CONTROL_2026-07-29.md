newer authoritative record merely because it is easier to read.

## Publication stop conditions

Stop before external mutation if:

- a scientific edit is not unambiguously authorised by current records;
- a claim, count, source, hash, receipt, chronology or status fails to
  reconcile;
- a candidate PDF has not passed complete mechanical and rendered-page QA;
- the existing Zenodo concept lineage is ambiguous;
- a Zenodo action would create a new concept record;
- the publication package differs from the approved hashes; or
- Maria Smith has not approved the final candidate and recorded ambiguities.

Version drafts may be prepared only against the existing concepts. Publication
must occur one branch at a time, with the returned record DOI, concept DOI,
files and checksums verified before proceeding to the next branch.
