Date: 2026-07-24
Status: `open_blocking`
Effect: every published branch artifact remains preserved, but no published

## Executive finding

The branch-paper gate proved a narrower proposition than Maria Smith required.
It proved that a manuscript covered every claim in its own frozen inventory.
It did not prove that the inventory contained every relevant result already
registered in V1, V2 and the prior observational record. A self-consistent but
incomplete inventory could therefore pass.

That is a publication-compliance failure. It does not erase any admitted V3
claim or alter any immutable engine receipt. It withdraws the broader labels
`complete`, `current` and `fully reconciled` until the missing obligation audit,
same-strength reconstruction and required empirical comparisons are complete.

## Binding interpretation of “forced to measured value”

For every physical constant, coefficient, dimensionless physical ratio or
measurable physical law already registered as an SFT result, branch closure
requires all of the following before publication:

1. register the prior result as a mandatory V3 reconstruction obligation;
2. assign exactly one categorical owning branch;
3. independently generate the V3 candidate grammar without importing the
   earlier answer into the runtime;
4. enumerate the complete declared candidate space;
5. force one exact survivor from the foundational theorem with zero axioms,
   zero free parameters and only permitted exact arithmetic;
6. freeze the value and derivation seal before external target release;
7. compare the sealed result with the complete authoritative measurement,
   including units, uncertainty, source identity and falsifier;
8. preserve every failed comparison and adverse control;
9. halt branch completion if the value is absent, non-unique, conditional,
   empirically disjoint or weaker than the registered prior result; and
10. publish the full chain in the paper belonging to the owning branch.

Observation can identify what must be reconstructed and can test the sealed
result. It cannot choose the candidate grammar, eliminator or survivor.

## Engine repair

The current-compliance gate now performs the check that the original paper
gate omitted. Before any Zenodo publication action it requires:

- complete categorical ownership of every prior obligation;
- same-strength closure of every obligation owned by the selected branch;
- equality between the live branch census and the manuscript evidence map;
- an internally ready paper manifest; and
- explicit publication authorization, which remains separate.

Run the audit with:

```text
python3 tools/verify_publication_compliance.py
```

Enforce a branch publication attempt with:

```text
python3 tools/verify_publication_compliance.py --branch physics --require-ready
```

The Zenodo publication tool now requires the branch identifier and invokes the
same fail-closed check before any network mutation when `--publish` is used.
Draft inspection remains non-publishing.
