## Audit question

This audit asks whether V3 status prose, frontier declarations, claim-local
exclusions or dependency analyses have been allowed to imply that Smithian Fold
Theory lacks a result which the authoritative V2 corpus already derives, or
that measurement may supply a value which must instead be forced before
measurement.

The audit does **not** import a V2 result into the V3 model. A V2 theorem and a
V3 engine admission are different facts. The lawful status for a V2 result not
yet independently regenerated by V3 is **V3 clean-room replication gap**, not
"absent from SFT", "measurement must supply it", or "scientifically open".

## Classification law

Every candidate statement was placed in one of five classes.

1. **Valid claim-local boundary.** The sentence limits only the registered
   claim and does not speak for all of SFT.
2. **V3 clean-room replication gap.** V2 contains the result, but V3 has not
   yet admitted an independently reconstructed chain.
3. **Unsupported dismissal.** V3 prose incorrectly presents a V2-derived
   result as absent or scientifically unavailable.
4. **Measurement-direction reversal.** Observation or fitting is positioned as
   a source of a structural law rather than a downstream falsification check.
5. **Scope/completion overclaim.** A frozen inventory is described as complete
   science despite unreconciled V2 results outside that inventory.

Claim-local exclusions such as "this categorical claim does not predict a
numerical value" remain valid only when explicitly scoped to that claim. They
must never be read as a global SFT nonclaim.

### A-06 — numerical Chemistry boundary sentences

**Class:** valid claim-local boundaries, with global-scope warning.

Statements that a categorical terminology or record claim does not itself

Future prose must say "this claim does not..." and must reconcile any broader
nonclaim against V2 before publication.

### A-07 — publication/status synchronization

**Class:** status desynchronization, not a derivation failure.

Some frontier prose still says publication authorization is false while the
corresponding branch is published. Published receipts, source hashes and DOI

updated by a new versioned record rather than rewriting those artifacts.

## Corrective rules now binding

1. Every V3 frontier assertion must be checked against all 407 authoritative V2
   step headings and the relevant full step text.
2. A V2 result absent from V3 is labelled a replication obligation.
3. A measurement method may consume a sealed structural consequence; no

4. A frozen branch inventory is complete only at its explicit inventory
   boundary unless every relevant V2 result is reconciled.
5. Historical receipts and hashes remain immutable. Corrections are additive,
   versioned and linked to the affected evidence.
6. Nothing becomes model-admitted through this audit. Each reconstructed law
   still requires complete grammar generation, one survivor, root trace, zero
   axioms, zero free parameters, adverse controls, independent regeneration and
   the single official engine route.
