## Purpose

This audit corrects a categorical blind spot in the earlier V3 reconciliation.
The earlier Physics atom count considered only rows that had already been
labelled Physics-owned.  A prior statement could therefore disappear from the
reported gap count by being labelled "another categorical branch or
corpus-level synthesis" without being assigned to an actual V3 owner.  The
Tesla corpus row `V1-XIV-10` is the decisive example: it was excluded before the
zero-gap count and has no dedicated V3 reconstruction.

That counting method cannot establish prior-corpus completeness.  From this
audit onward, every V1/V2 statement must receive one of three content-level
dispositions:

1. **reconstructed** — the complete scientific content is present in one or
   more admitted V3 claims with named categorical owners;
2. **partly represented** — a narrower V3 law exists, but one or more stronger
   prior obligations still require independent derivation, enumeration and
   evidence; or
3. **return required** — no admitted V3 claim owns the scientific content.

Reputational labels, conventional plausibility and present popularity cannot
select the disposition.  Prior work is not executable proof authority, but it
is a mandatory observation and reconstruction target.  A stronger historical
claim is not closed by renaming it, narrowing it, bundling it with a different
claim that halts, or assigning it to an unnamed future synthesis.
