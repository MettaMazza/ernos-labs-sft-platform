# Comprehensive branch-paper protocol

A branch paper is written only after every V1/V2/prior-observation entry has
been reviewed against that branch, every entry containing a branch-owned
component has been decomposed without loss, every resulting atomic obligation
has exactly one categorical owner, every obligation owned by the branch has a
same-strength V3 disposition, the branch's current-knowledge inventory equals
its live claim census, and every required obligation has a model-admitted
engine receipt. Entries reviewed as non-owning remain explicitly enumerated
and hash-bound; they are not silently skipped.

This branch-specific full-source review permits dependency-ordered successor
publication without pretending that later branches are already closed. The
global all-branch owner ledger remains mandatory for the final TOE paper.

The paper must cover every registered derivation in dependency order and state:

- the question and exact theorem statement;
- generated candidate grammar and boundary;
- complete census and rejected alternatives;
- forced survivor, minimality and named-shape uniqueness;
- independent certificate and adverse controls;
- empirical registration, blind seal and external measurements where required;
- correspondence with established work only after the SFT derivation;
- meaning, consequences and exact limitations; and
- a complete evidence map from prose to source, receipt and artifact hashes.

The branch publication gate halts if an obligation is missing, unowned,
mapped only at weaker strength, conditional, unclassified, frontier,
unvalidated or absent from the paper evidence map. A frozen inventory cannot
hide a known prior result merely by omitting it.

Before any successor is labelled current or any Zenodo publication action is
attempted, run:

```text
python3 tools/verify_publication_compliance.py --branch BRANCH --require-ready
```

The earlier claim-set publication receipts remain immutable evidence that the
archived papers covered their declared inventories. They are not substitutes
for this stronger current-knowledge compliance result.
