## Publication rule

A successor is a new exhaustive academic paper, not a silent edit of a DOI
artifact and not a short erratum. The v1 Markdown, PDF, source archive,
checksums, manifests and receipts remain preserved. A successor receives new
version metadata only after its complete owning-branch gate passes and Maria
Smith explicitly authorizes publication.

## Required front-loaded architecture

Every successor paper must begin with the strongest exact and empirical
results rather than an inventory count:

1. result-bearing title and subtitle;
2. one-sentence principal scientific claim;
3. headline findings in ordinary scientific language;
4. an exact empirical scoreboard;
5. strongest failed result and adverse control;
6. decisive falsification conditions;
7. categorical ownership and non-claim boundary;
8. complete dependency map to the foundational theorem;
9. generated grammar, enumeration and rejected alternatives;
10. full derivations in dependency order;
11. blind post-seal measurement checks where applicable;
12. meaning, correspondence and consequences;
13. limitations, open obligations and standing predictions; and
14. one-command reproduction, evidence map and immutable receipt ledger.

The empirical scoreboard records the forced exact result, generated candidate
count, prediction seal, external source, value and units, complete uncertainty
interval, comparison result, falsifier and evidence class. Formal computation
uses executable proof, exhaustive finite census and tamper controls rather than
mislabeling those checks as physical measurement.
