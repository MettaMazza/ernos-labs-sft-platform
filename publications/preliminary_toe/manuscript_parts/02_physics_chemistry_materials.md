#### 15.2.2 Why the rotation orders stop at six

The rank-two integral rotation boundary admits only orders whose relevant

registered bounds \(a\leq2\), \(b\leq1\), with the simultaneous terminal case
excluded. The survivors are one, two, three, four and six.

This proof explains why five is excluded from periodic crystallography without
making fivefold organisation impossible. Five becomes the first aperiodic or
quasicrystalline boundary. The classification is therefore more informative

Later complete-field claims add critical-field organisation, vortex matter and
pinning, coherence length, superfluid excitation and critical flow. Each is a
property of an identified specimen and state. The exact state classification
does not supply one universal transition temperature or critical field.

### 15.4 Mechanical, thermal, electronic, magnetic and optical response

The property families all use the same general relation: a fully identified
specimen and state are subjected to a named perturbation, and a method-bound
response is recorded. The shared grammar does not imply that all properties
are the same quantity.

Mechanical claims distinguish elastic return, plastic retention, strength,
fatigue, creep, fracture and toughness. Load mode, direction, geometry,
strain rate, temperature and preparation are part of the evidence. A modulus
is not a universal exact number generated from composition alone.

Thermal claims distinguish stored response, transport, expansion and phase
change. Electronic and ionic claims distinguish carriers, transport paths,
band or gap relations, junctions and environmental dependence. Magnetic and
superconducting claims retain order, field history, hysteresis, transition and
measurement protocol. Optical claims retain wavelength, direction,
polarisation, absorption, transmission and emission. Exact structural
relations may constrain these families, but conductivity, strength,
transition temperature, index and attenuation remain values of identified
specimens under identified methods and conditions.

This exact-versus-measured distinction is one of the branch's central results.
It prevents two opposite errors: fitting a structural law to a preferred
specimen value, and dismissing a genuine exact relation merely because real
specimens exhibit conditional variation. Formal structure and empirical
response meet through an explicit interface; neither replaces the other.

#### 15.4.1 Mechanical response and deformation history

Stress records transferred load over the declared support; strain records the
resulting change of shape or extent. Their tensor-resolved relation preserves
direction and load mode. Elastic recovery closes when removal of the
perturbation restores the retained state. Plastic deformation preserves a
changed state. Elastic modulus is a response ratio on the registered interval,
not a universal scalar of a chemical formula.

Transverse-to-longitudinal strain adds a directional comparison. Viscoelastic
response retains memory and recovery over time; viscoplastic flow retains both
rate and non-restoring change. A yield surface is a boundary in the complete
loading-path support. Work hardening preserves deformation history rather than
assigning the same state to a previously deformed and undeformed specimen.

Strength and hardness are distinct boundaries. Fracture energy and toughness
retain crack creation and propagation. Stable and unstable crack growth are
separate regimes. Fatigue distinguishes initiation from propagation under
cyclic loading; creep distinguishes mechanism and rupture-time custody under a
sustained condition. Impact and high-rate response require their recurrence
scale. A result measured slowly cannot be copied into the shock boundary.

Friction relates two contacting surfaces and their state. Lubrication adds an
intervening carrier; a tribofilm is a generated and retained interface.
Rheological response distinguishes flow and relaxation classes. Every
mechanical measurement therefore names geometry, orientation, rate,
temperature, preparation and method where those fields affect the falsifier.

#### 15.4.2 Thermal storage and transport

Heat capacity is excitation storage per temperature relation at the declared
state. Thermal conductivity records energy transport through the specimen,
while diffusivity composes conductivity with stored response and density.
Thermal expansion is an oriented change of extent. None is inferred solely
from the existence of lattice modes.

Interfacial thermal-boundary resistance belongs to the interface, not either
bulk constituent alone. Phonon scattering retains the complete mean-path
ledger. Radiative transport in a material retains absorption, emission and
geometry. Thermoelectric response is a coupled electrical and thermal
relation, with its performance boundary explicitly named rather than
presented as a context-free efficiency.

Phase-change storage distinguishes sensible storage from the retained phase
transition. Thermal shock combines gradient, constraint and mechanical
response; thermal fatigue adds recurrence and accumulated damage. The
validation vector keeps specimen, temperature range, direction, method and
uncertainty. A favourable conductivity row does not validate a different
microstructure or temperature regime.

#### 15.4.3 Electronic and ionic transport

Electrical conductivity and resistivity are reciprocal response relations
only at the compatible state and method boundary. Carrier mobility and
concentration are separated so that one measured conductivity does not
uniquely infer both. The Hall response provides a carrier-sign and density
ledger under its declared model and field. Dielectric permittivity and loss
retain frequency, direction and condition.

Ionic conductivity records mobile chemical carriers through organised matter;
transference partitions their contributions. Mixed ionic–electronic transport
retains both carrier classes. Tunnelling is a finite barrier relation.
Band alignment and interface offset belong to a pair of materials and their
interface; heterostructure confinement adds the resulting bounded carrier.
Defect and trap states preserve their structural source, while screening and
depletion retain the response length.

Electrochemical insertion changes the material state as a chemical carrier
enters or leaves. Chemistry owns the species and reaction; Materials owns the
phase, strain, defect and transport response. This distinction prevents a
successful half-cell reaction from being advertised as a complete electrode
material lifetime.

#### 15.4.4 Magnetic, superconducting and superfluid response

Ferromagnetic, antiferromagnetic and ferrimagnetic order differ by how local
magnetic carriers compose. Paramagnetic and diamagnetic responses are
perturbation-bound classes. Spin-glass freezing retains disorder and history.
Domains and walls add mesoscale organisation, and the hysteresis loop is a
complete path ledger rather than one magnetisation number.

Magnetocrystalline anisotropy attaches the response to crystal orientation.
Magnetoresistance retains electrical and field coordinates. Spin transport
and relaxation preserve preparation, path and decay of the held spin record.
Critical fields, vortex matter, pinning and coherence length describe distinct
boundaries of a superconducting specimen. Superfluid excitation and critical
flow likewise retain state and apparatus.

The branch does not infer a universal mechanism from a transition alone. A
measured critical temperature is condition- and specimen-bound. Flux
quantisation and topological class may be exact structural relations while the
observed plateau, field or transition remains empirical. The validation vector
keeps those evidence classes separate.

#### 15.4.5 Optical and photonic response

Optical absorption and extinction retain wavelength, path and material state.
Reflection and transmission close an energy ledger at the interface.
Luminescence separates generated excitation, emitted record and quantum yield;
the yield is source- and method-bound. Elastic and inelastic scattering retain
whether the material state changed.

Birefringence follows from optical anisotropy. Nonlinear mixing requires the
complete participating frequencies, orientations and material response.
Waveguides retain confinement and loss. A photonic band gap is an organised
propagation boundary; a defect mode is a permitted state inside that boundary.
Plasmonic response is collective. Exciton generation, transport and
recombination retain separate parts of the state trajectory.

An optical classification does not automatically establish device utility. A
measured emission line, propagation loss or yield must be tied to specimen,
preparation, direction, wavelength, excitation and detector. Engineering later
owns whether those relations satisfy an application requirement.

#### 15.4.6 Evidence across property families

The mechanical and tribological, thermal and transport, electronic/ionic and
dielectric, magnetic/superconducting/topological, and optical/photonic
validation vectors each preserve their own source rows. Cross-source
reproducibility asks whether independent records retain the same
structure–property relation. It does not require every specimen to have the
same magnitude.

An adverse row may show that a proposed universalisation fails while the
narrower condition-bound law remains. An absent feature is different from an
unavailable source. An out-of-bound measurement may be accurate but unable to
test the registered claim. The complete adverse vector retains all of these
states, which is why the branch-level empirical status cannot be read as
“every reported measurement agreed”.

### 15.5 Material classes, soft matter and nanoscale organisation

Material classes group recurring structure–property organisations without
making the class name the cause. Metals, ceramics, polymers, composites and
other classes are defined through their retained chemical, structural and
response relations. A polymer's Chemistry owner defines repeat units and
chemical architecture; Materials owns morphology, phase, orientation,
processing and bulk response. A composite requires constituent identities,
interface and spatial organisation, not merely an averaged composition.

Soft matter adds fluctuation, relaxation, viscoelasticity, colloidal relation,
gelation and self-organisation at condition-sensitive boundaries. The branch
retains the timescale and protocol under which an object appears solid-like or
fluid-like. Nanomaterials add size, confinement and surface-to-volume
organisation. “Nano” is not used as a universal property claim; the evidence
must name the dimension and the response that changes at that scale.

Surface and interface science is required wherever neighbouring materials do
not reduce to either bulk constituent. Adhesion, wetting, boundary energy and
interfacial transport depend on both sides of the boundary and on its
preparation. This interface carrier is a direct prerequisite for biomaterials
and for the Biology handoff.

#### 15.5.1 Metals, ceramics, polymers and composites

A metallic material retains collective electronic support together with its
crystal, defect and microstructural organisation. A ceramic material retains
its ionic or covalent chemical carrier and the corresponding structural
relations. A polymeric material retains the chemical chain architecture and
adds morphology, phase, orientation and bulk response. A composite contains
distinct constituents and the interfaces through which load, heat, charge or
damage transfers.

These classes are generated through their retained relations, not through a
list of familiar examples. Glass and amorphous organisation lack long-range
periodic order while preserving local structure. Porous materials add a void
network whose size, fraction, shape and connectivity affect the response. The
molecular-to-bulk correspondence states which chemical and physical
coordinates survive the scale change. Anisotropy retains direction. Size and
surface effects state when a boundary contribution is no longer negligible.

The complete-field class family then separates solid solutions and alloy
phases, ordered intermetallic compounds, compositionally complex alloys,
refractory and ultra-high-temperature classes, cementitious and concrete
composites, fibre- and particle-reinforced composites, metallic glasses,
structural and functional ceramics, thermoplastics, thermosets and elastomers,
graded materials and architected cellular materials.

Each class supplies a grammar for what must be retained. “Compositionally
complex” does not mean composition is irrelevant; “metallic glass” does not
mean crystal defects can be copied unaltered into the description;
“functionally graded” requires a spatially retained variation; and an
architected material requires geometry in addition to constituent identity.
Measured properties remain attached to the actual specimen and processing
route.

The water bulk-response ledger provides an explicit molecular-to-bulk case.
Hydrogen-bonded molecular identity is inherited from Chemistry, while the
collective response belongs to Materials. The source rows test the declared
bulk relation; they do not turn one water condition into a universal model of
all hydrogen-bonded materials.

#### 15.5.2 Soft matter and timescale

Colloidal stability retains particle identity, medium, interaction and
aggregation route. Gelation creates a percolated soft network. A foam retains
cells, films and drainage; an emulsion retains droplets and continuous phase.
Liquid crystals preserve orientational order without requiring full
translational order. Membranes and thin films add a bounded geometry whose
surface and transport cannot be treated as bulk.

Granular matter retains packing and force-chain support. Jamming is a boundary
between rearranging and mechanically retained organisation under the declared
load and recurrence scale. Responsive materials change state under a named
stimulus. Active materials consume a retained energy source and remain
nonequilibrium organisations; “active” is not permission to omit the energy
ledger.

Soft response is inseparable from timescale. The same specimen may be
solid-like on one observation interval and fluid-like on another. A complete
claim therefore retains recurrence, perturbation, recovery and history.
Classifying a material by one photograph or instantaneous modulus would lose
the relation the family is designed to preserve.

#### 15.5.3 Nanoscale confinement and collective state

A nanomaterial claim begins with a measured size or confinement coordinate.
Nanoparticles retain a size and shape distribution, not one nominal diameter.
Nanowires have one-dimensional confinement; two-dimensional materials retain
layer and stacking; quantum dots retain finite confinement in every spatial
direction. Surface-to-volume dominance is a relation between those exact
supports.

Nanoscale phase and melting boundaries remain material- and condition-bound.
Quantum materials are classified by their collective state, not simply their
small size. Moiré and twisted-layer structures add a superlattice relation.
Nanocomposites retain interface density. Aggregation and dispersion custody
must distinguish the primary particle from the state actually measured in the
medium.

The branch therefore avoids using “nano” as a promotional adjective. A source
must name which dimension lies in the registered range, which surface or
confinement relation changes and which observation discriminates it. A small
particle without a demonstrated scale-dependent relation remains a small
specimen, not evidence for every nanoscale law.

#### 15.5.4 Surfaces, interfaces and coatings

A surface free-state and energy relation arises because the boundary lacks the
same neighbourhood as the bulk. Wetting and contact angle retain the liquid,
solid, vapour, preparation and measurement geometry. Adhesion and work of
separation record the complete interface ledger. A coating adds a layer and
substrate whose individual identities and joining relation remain distinct.

Roughness is scale-bound: the numerical record changes with sampling length,
resolution and filtering. Surface reaction and catalysis are handed to and
from Chemistry through the identity and reaction carrier, while Materials owns
surface organisation and resulting response. Tribofilm formation retains the
generated interfacial layer; interface fracture and delamination retain the
path by which that relation fails.

An interface is not merely the average of its neighbouring bulk phases. It can
carry a distinct composition, structure, energy and transport relation. This
is why interface custody recurs in electronics, composites, corrosion,
biomaterials and processing. The one-owner graph permits those uses while
preventing five different branch sections from claiming to derive the same
primitive interface.

### 15.7 Processing, degradation, computation and extreme conditions

Processing supplies the path by which chemical constituents and physical
conditions become a specimen. The complete relation is process → structure →
property, with intermediate states retained where the evidence permits. A
heat treatment, deposition, deformation or synthesis route can change phase,
grain, defect, texture or interface and thereby change response. Reproducible
materials science therefore requires the path, not only the final label.

Degradation is the time-dependent inverse problem. Corrosion, wear, chemical
attack, fatigue and environmental damage alter the retained organisation.
Lifetime is a conditional reconstruction or measurement, not an intrinsic
number detached from load, environment and method. Missing exposure data or a
failed source capture remains unresolved or unavailable, not a favourable
durability result.

Computational materials claims preserve representation, model, approximation,
scale, resource and validation. Simulation may reconstruct a mechanism or
predict a registered response; it becomes empirical evidence only through the
declared comparison. Test success establishes implementation identity at the
tested boundary, not physical confirmation. Resource halts and low-confidence
outputs remain in the audit surface.

Extreme-condition claims retain the high- or low-temperature, pressure, field
or rate boundary at which a response was measured. An independently generated
label that cannot be resolved from the current source remains a halt rather
than being harmonised by prose. Sustainable-material claims similarly state
their bounded resource, recovery, durability or life-cycle relation. They do
not absorb Earth-system consequences or Engineering deployment decisions.

#### 15.7.1 Degradation as a complete change ledger

Oxidation-scale growth composes chemical reaction with transport through the
new layer. Corrosion rate retains the coupled electrochemical path, specimen
area, exposure and time. Passivation is the formation of a protective state;
film breakdown is a distinct boundary. Stress-corrosion cracking composes a
mechanical crack path with the corrosive environment rather than attributing
failure to either in isolation.

Hydrogen uptake and embrittlement retain ingress, distribution,
microstructural interaction and mechanical consequence. Abrasive, adhesive and
erosive wear are different transfer relations. Radiation damage accumulates
defects and may also permit recovery. Physical ageing records drift in a
retained state without requiring a change of chemical identity. Environmental
attack and weathering preserve the complete exposure condition.

Service life and failure time are evidence boundaries. A predicted lifetime
is a reconstruction from a named load and environment; an observed failure is
a source-bound event; a censored specimen that has not failed by the end of a
test is neither an infinite lifetime nor a null degradation result. Those
statuses must remain visible in the complete table.

The degradation family therefore carries favourable durability results and
early failures under one constitution. A later coating or processing change
may lawfully create a new specimen and new status, but it does not delete the
adverse history of the earlier state.

#### 15.7.2 Processing as the construction of a specimen

Casting retains the mould-filling and solidification history. Thermomechanical
forming adds deformation and texture. Machining creates a surface and possible
damage state. Additive manufacture retains every layer, melt-pool relation and
build history required by the claim. Thin-film deposition records incoming
material and growth; epitaxy adds lattice matching and orientation.

Welding, brazing and joining create an interface whose thermal, chemical and
mechanical histories differ from either parent material. Polymer processing
retains flow and orientation. Powder processing retains particle identity,
packing and compaction. Heat treatment, sintering and densification already
introduced in the foundation are composed into the complete process–structure
route.

A process window is a source-bound region of conditions that produces the
registered state with the stated reproducibility. Provenance includes machine,
feedstock, geometry, atmosphere, calibration and post-processing where the
falsifier requires them. A nominal process name cannot substitute for those
coordinates.

Processing evidence is not merely procedural detail. If two specimens have
different histories, treating them as one observation can conceal the actual
structure–property relation. Conversely, a reproducible path can explain why
different laboratories obtain compatible response without asserting that
every uncontrolled specimen must agree.

#### 15.7.3 Computational representation and forward simulation

An exact material-structure representation identifies constituents, sites,
relations, phase, boundary and scale. A structure–property computation names
the law by which that input produces a response. A finite numerical simulation
adds discretisation, update rule, termination and error. Multiscale composition
specifies how outputs and uncertainties cross scales.

Numerical stability and error propagation remain implementation evidence.
Passing a convergence test verifies the behaviour of the registered
discretisation; it does not prove that the underlying physical model describes
the specimen. The inverse materials problem reverses the relation from target
response to candidate structure and must retain non-uniqueness. Machine-learning
inference adds training provenance, representation, applicability and
uncertainty. A pretrained model is not an admissible proof premise.

Materials databases require identity and provenance. Two rows can be compared
only where material, phase, processing, specimen, direction, method and
condition are compatible. Phase-field, molecular-dynamics and
electronic-structure correspondences each operate on a different carrier and
scale. Their outputs cannot be merged as if they were three measurements of
one quantity.

Simulation-to-experiment validation is separately registered. The sealed
simulation, input state, comparison metric and complete observation rows must
be retained. A model may be useful and compatible without being uniquely
confirmed. A failed run, missing potential, unavailable input or resource halt
remains in the archive.

#### 15.7.4 Extreme conditions and path custody

High pressure, high temperature and cryogenic response each define a material
state under a named boundary. High electric and magnetic fields add direction,
magnitude and exposure. High strain rate and shock add a short recurrence
scale and propagation path. Extreme radiation adds source, dose, spectrum and
recovery. Combined extremes require the complete order in which conditions
were applied.

Path custody matters because material state can be hysteretic or metastable.
A specimen compressed and then heated may not equal one heated and then
compressed. A cryogenic value cannot be extended to room temperature by
silence. A shock response cannot be inferred from a quasi-static curve without
the registered correspondence.

The independent extreme-label halt records a source whose current information
does not resolve the required generated class. The correct scientific action
is to preserve the halt. Choosing the closest familiar label would manufacture
agreement and destroy the falsifier.

#### 15.7.5 Sustainability and bounded system claims

Embodied material and energy ledgers retain the inputs required to produce the
declared specimen. Critical-material availability is a source- and
time-dependent boundary. Reuse and remanufacture preserve enough identity and
function for a further cycle; recycling separates and recovers constituents;
yield retains every residual route.

A circular material flow is a network ledger, not the claim that no material
leaves the system. Durability and life extension compare complete service
histories. Material toxicity is handed to Medicine and Environmental Science

dispersed, stored and unresolved residues.

These claims are deliberately bounded. Materials Science can derive and test
the material flow, durability or recovery relation. Earth and Environmental

whether a design meets safety and function. Economics and Social Systems own
institutions, incentives and access. A favourable recycling yield cannot be
promoted into a complete sustainability judgement without those downstream
relations.

handoff, durability, recycling and end-of-life carriers. The downstream branch
adds environmental compartment, transport, ecological interaction and system
history. A bounded recycling yield or weathering test cannot establish global
environmental benefit without those relations.

Astronomy receives material-state, phase, optical, thermal, high-pressure and
extreme-condition carriers where they are needed to interpret celestial
matter. It must add the astronomical source, distance, observation and system
model. Historical step 291 is routed there precisely because planetary and
galactic evidence cannot be owned by Materials simply because matter is
present.

#### 15.9.2 Handoff evidence does not duplicate evidence

A downstream claim may cite an upstream source through its receipt, but that
use is not an independent replication of the upstream observation. Independent
replication requires a distinct implementation or source route under the
registered protocol. The one-owner graph counts dependency edges, not extra
confirmations.

Corrections also travel forward. If an upstream source is superseded or a
status becomes adverse, dependent claims must be audited for impact. They do
not automatically fail if their relation did not use the changed coordinate,
and they do not automatically survive if it did. The explicit dependency route
makes that impact analysis possible.

What remains unresolved at this boundary is the defining problem of life.
Neither a molecule nor a material specimen explains why a bounded organisation
maintains itself, uses inherited information, regulates competing processes,
develops, reproduces or evolves. Biology must introduce those relations and
their evidence without taking physical constants, chemical reactions or
material interfaces back as new biological axioms. The next part therefore
begins with the living carrier and the distinction between organised matter
and organisation that acts to preserve and reproduce its own conditions.

### How this part can change

A lawful correction begins with a discrepancy against one of the current
authorities. If the active paper, inventory and machine record disagree, the
records are traced to their source and explicit supersession. A clerical
identity can be corrected when the authoritative direction is unambiguous. A
scientific disagreement—different count, value, chronology, evidence class or
owner—must remain visible for Maria Smith unless the repository itself
authorises the resolution.

A lawful extension registers a new question, candidate grammar, dependency
route, controls, evidence boundary and receipt. It may add a new physical
measurement, chemical species, material class or downstream use. It may not
change a failed historical row into success, call a new source an old blind
target, or insert a measured value into the earlier proof grammar.

Falsification is likewise local and exact. A disjoint CODATA interval would be
adverse to the registered fine-structure empirical claim. A qualifying
Smithium experiment that fails the sealed identification protocol would be
adverse to that protocol; no experiment leaves the status unresolved. A
crystallographic classification outside the complete registered grammar would
challenge the relevant Materials claim. The wider corpus would then require a
dependency impact audit rather than a rhetorical declaration that everything
either survives or collapses.

The dependency relation among the branches is now explicit:

1. Physics turns exact formal carriers into quantities, dimensions, fields,
   interactions, constants and physical measurement interfaces.
2. Chemistry uses those carriers to derive stable chemical identity,
   composition, bond, reaction, periodic extension and source-bound molecular
   evidence.
3. Materials Science organises physical and chemical carriers across phase,
   defect, microstructure, surface, process and scale to derive
   structure–property relations.
4. Biology receives the resulting biochemical and material interfaces but
   must derive living organisation in its own right.

### Three worked dependency routes

The fine-structure route shows how an upstream exact carrier becomes a
measured physical constant and then a downstream constraint. Foundation
supplies binary distinction and reversible return; Mathematics supplies exact
finite ratios and order; Information Science supplies immutable record and
custody; Computation supplies enumeration and checking; Reversible and Quantum
Computation supplies the reversible process boundary; Physics supplies the
three-generator, least covers, ladder and exact \(\alpha\). Chemistry then uses
the resulting atomic boundary without re-deriving \(\alpha\), and Materials
uses the chemical and physical carriers in its electronic and optical response
claims. One law is depended upon many times but owned once.

The Smithium route begins with the Physics nuclear closure
\((126,184)\), orbital capacity and fine-structure endpoint. Chemistry adds
elemental identity, electronic configuration, entrance-partition grammar,
decay coordinates, ion and oxidation relations, transition classes, chemical
states and identification protocol. The empirical status remains a standing
prediction because the required synthesis and identification observations are
not present. Materials could later study a Smithium phase or specimen only
after Chemistry supplies the observed chemical carrier; it cannot manufacture
that observation from a formal materials model.

The biomaterial route begins with physical quantity, field, transport and
mechanical response; adds chemical species, bond, reaction, solvent and polymer
identity; then adds material phase, pore connectivity, surface, interface,
degradation and scaffold organisation. Biology receives those carriers and
must add cell, function, regulation, development and living-system evidence.
Medicine must further add clinical population, intervention, comparator and
outcome. A favourable early interface result cannot leap over those dependency
boundaries.

### Exact structure and empirical magnitude

Across the three branches, exactness has a consistent meaning. The
fine-structure fraction, cover depths, nuclear closures, periodic capacities,
entrance-partition count, rotation orders, crystal systems, Bravais classes and
acoustic branch count are exact at their registered grammars. Their exactness
does not depend on a displayed decimal or sample mean.

Measured values have a different completeness. A CODATA interval is complete
for the named release and uncertainty statement. A molecular property is
complete only with species, state, method and condition. A material modulus is
complete only with specimen, direction, preparation, scale, method and
condition. Exact rational interval arithmetic can preserve those records
without promoting them into universal proof values.

This separation also supports persuasion. The paper can state that the exact
\(\alpha^{-1}\) lies inside the registered CODATA interval, that the aromatic
support sequence is generated and its energy surface is positive at the
registered boundary, and that seven crystal systems and fourteen Bravais
classes survive complete enumeration. It need not weaken those results with
generic doubt. It must, however, preserve the different chronology and
evidence class that makes each statement true.

### Why the order cannot be reversed

Chemistry cannot precede Physics because element, isotope, electron state,
energy, charge, spatial orientation, transition and measurement already have
physical meanings. Treating them as chemical axioms would duplicate upstream
ownership and conceal their derivation. Physics cannot complete Chemistry
because a physically possible collection of nuclei and electrons does not by
itself select species identity, bond, stereochemistry, reaction route,
solvation or analytical equivalence.

Materials cannot precede Chemistry because composition, bond, phase identity
and reaction supply the constituents whose organisation is measured. Chemistry
cannot complete Materials because chemical formula does not fix grain,
texture, defect population, interface, processing history or specimen response.
The same chemical carrier can support materially distinct states, and that
difference is not an error to be averaged away.

Biology cannot be inserted before the physical–chemical bridge because living
systems use quantity, energy, molecule, reaction, membrane, scaffold, soft
matter and material transport. Yet Materials cannot complete Biology because
maintenance, heredity, regulation, development, reproduction and evolution
are new relations. The handoff is therefore a dependency, not a reduction.

The empirical order has the same direction. A target is not allowed to select
the formal grammar. A molecular database cannot define the Physics constant
used to structure it. A material measurement cannot be used to revise a
chemical identity silently. A biological outcome cannot retroactively make an
adverse interface test favourable. Later evidence may test, extend or falsify
upstream work; it does not become an earlier premise.

The order also clarifies the role of computation. Enumeration, independent
reconstruction and tests execute the registered relations, but successful
execution does not own the physical, chemical or material result. The
application programmes—including Protein Fold—consume these branches through
explicit dependencies. Their eventual successes or failures will be important
evidence at their registered application boundaries, not selectors of the
foundational laws already sealed.

This dependency discipline is what allows the three branches to form one
continuous theory without becoming one undifferentiated vocabulary. Every
handoff carries enough structure to make the next problem possible and leaves
enough unresolved that the next branch has genuine scientific work to do.

This sequence prevents both reduction by assertion and independence by
forgetfulness. Chemistry is constrained by Physics but is not merely renamed
particle physics. Materials is constrained by Physics and Chemistry but is not
merely a list of compositions. Biology will be constrained by all three but
cannot inherit function or life from them without a registered biological
relation.

For an unfamiliar specialist, the shortest verification route is therefore
claim-specific. Begin with the exact statement and owner; inspect its declared
dependencies and grammar; reproduce the candidate census, decisions, survivor
and controls; then inspect the evidence package only if the statement makes an
empirical claim. Check source identity, target chronology, preserving
coordinates, all-row treatment and falsifier before reading the interpretation.
Finally, follow the outgoing handoff to see whether the next branch uses the
result at the same strength. This order prevents a compelling conclusion from
concealing a weaker receipt and prevents a machine success from being mistaken
for a measurement.

The same route permits efficient criticism. A critic need not reject or accept
the entire theory. They may challenge one dependency, candidate omission,
decision, source adapter, uncertainty interval, chronology field or ownership
edge. A valid defect remains local until the explicit graph shows its
downstream consequences. The public archive is designed to make that impact
trace possible and to preserve the correction if the challenge succeeds.
