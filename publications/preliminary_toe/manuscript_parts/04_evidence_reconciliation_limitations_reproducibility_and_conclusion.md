## 25. Corpus-wide evidence reconciliation

The theory-wide result is not a sentence assembled from the strongest result
in each branch. It is the complete retained state of every branch at the
release freeze. The global audit therefore carries formal, computational,
empirical, historical and publication status as separate coordinates.

At the current claim boundary the reconciliation is exact:

- 2,751 registered claims are model-admitted;
- 892,246 generated candidates are retained in the aggregate census;
- 2,751 unique survivors are retained;
- 11,004 of 11,004 registered controls pass;
- 2,751 candidate-census identities reconstruct;
- 2,751 claim receipt identities match;
- 2,751 admitted claims trace to the single root; and
- no declared dependency is missing from the current claim ledger.

These results establish current claim-package closure. They do not erase the
separate final-ToE obligations recorded by the programme ledger. In
particular, the all-branch heavy verification has not been rerun since the

one-owner lineage merge remains older than the current graph, and several

boundaries.

### 25.1 Formal reconciliation

Formal reconciliation asks whether each admitted statement has exactly one
owner, a complete dependency route, a registered candidate grammar, the
declared number of decisions, the required survivor state, controls and a
receipt matching the accepted package. The complete claim inventory binds
these fields compactly. The branch audit volumes preserve the complete record
without forcing thousands of repeated receipt blocks into the conceptual
narrative.

An exact formal result remains limited by its grammar. The global total does
not convert every depth-independent branch statement into a theorem about an
unregistered extension. Where the claim is a bounded census, the bound is
retained. Where it is a correspondence, the correspondence class is retained.
Where an external observation is required, the formal survivor does not
replace that observation.

### 25.2 Computational reconciliation

Computational reconciliation asks whether the registered source reconstructs
the stated census, decisions and receipt; whether an implementation-distinct
checker agrees; whether controls fail closed; and whether the public archive
identifies the producing source and artefacts. Repository tests are evidence
for those implementation questions. They do not manufacture empirical
confirmation.

The current lightweight repository validator passes at the 2,751-claim
boundary. The canonical engine seal verifies sixteen runtime files, and the
verification-authority seal verifies thirty-three protected files. The last
complete heavy run at the earlier all-foundations boundary replayed 1,319 of
1,319 derivations and passed 969 of 969 tests. This paper does not silently
promote that earlier heavy run into a 2,751-claim heavy verification. The final
global run remains an explicit open operation.

### 25.3 Empirical reconciliation

Empirical reconciliation preserves the method and chronology attached to each
external result. A current result may be:

- independently replicated;
- empirically tested and independently replicated;
- a sealed prediction awaiting measurement;
- a compatible but non-discriminating observation;
- a post-target reconstruction;
- a development-observed result;
- adverse;
- null;
- unavailable because transport or access failed; or
- unresolved because the required record is incomplete or has not been run.

The same words are not used interchangeably. A claim may possess exact formal
closure and an unresolved empirical extension. A branch may have field-wide
closure at its current-evidence boundary while a proposed later application
remains open. A historical
application may show measured success while its clean V3 recreation has not
started.

## 26. Corrections, adverse results and historical custody

### 26.1 Correction is part of the result

V3 treats a correction as a relationship between records, not as permission to
rewrite history. The corrected record must identify the earlier artefact, the
source of the mismatch, the new authoritative direction and the scope of the
change. If the correction affects only a file identity or clerical reference,
the record must say that no scientific count or evidence class changed. If it
changes a grammar, output or empirical conclusion, both the earlier result and
the lawful successor status remain visible.

The clean-room reconstruction itself is a corpus-scale reconciliation. Earlier
SFT versions remain historical provenance but are not proof premises for V3.
Their observations and applications can be compared after the corresponding
V3 result seals. Agreement is not assumed, and disagreement is not removed to
make the history appear linear.

### 26.2 Protein-folding chronology

Protein folding provides the clearest current example. The V2 lineage retained
strong local three-residue geometry correspondences while its sealed
76-residue whole-chain ubiquitin construction was adverse at the global
structure boundary, with TM-score `0.0269927379497572` and C-alpha dRMSD
`52.89314678065376` Å. Those exact measurements remain historical
implementation evidence. They are neither erased by the local successes nor
treated as the result of the new V3 six-axis system.

V3 then rebuilt representation, coordinate and interaction custody. A
historical 4APD whole-chain control exposed an inter-residue carbonyl-frame
defect. The corrected constructor preserved the complete defective record,
reconstructed every unordered atom pair and shared-atom correlation, and
removed the sub-angstrom collision. The correction did not improve the
C-alpha structure score: the retained control remains adverse. Later
sequence-only and partial-recurrence development tests contain both improvement
and regression. Their exact target chronology remains visible, and none is
silently relabelled as a primary blind test of the incomplete full V3 system.

The stronger work supersedes specific implementation obstacles only at the
boundaries it closes. It does not turn the historical adverse rows into
confirmation. The registered AlphaFold-generalised campaign remains future
evidence until the capability gate permits target access and all matched rows
have been evaluated.

### 26.3 Historical application results

The V2 Chess Fold, Go Fold and Unison Fold AI records are retained with their
producing-source identities, measurement protocols and known defects. Chess
retains its measured match tallies and exact legality controls. Go retains its
exact small-board censuses, GNU Go results and the recovered KataGo harness
record, including the disclosed round-one synchronisation defect and

These records motivate and constrain the clean V3 recreations. They do not
authorise a statement that a recreation exists when its workspace is absent,

### 26.4 Field evidence

The branch papers preserve their own adverse, corrected, failed-transport and
unresolved rows. Examples include adverse astronomical ranking comparisons,

establish an unbounded theorem, and corrected chemical and materials evidence
routes. Their branch chapters state the exact outcome and lawful successor
status. The global paper does not compress them into a claim that all external
tests were favourable.

## 27. Limitations and open frontier

### 27.1 Meaning of completion

Every use of *complete* in this monograph names its object and boundary.

- **Formal closure** means that the registered derivation and candidate grammar
  close.
- **Implementation closure** means that the executable construction and its
  declared checks close.
- **Foundational branch closure** means that the registered foundation of a
  branch closes.
- **Field-wide branch closure** means that the frozen current-evidence
  discipline census closes.
- **Empirical validation** means that the named evidence protocol has been run
  and met.
- **Publication completion** means that Maria Smith has approved and released
  the bound artefact.

None means the end of science. A frozen current inventory can be complete while
new measurements, discovered phenomena, improved source captures and lawful
extensions remain possible.

### 27.2 Open branch work

The present open branch programme is explicit:

   424-obligation full-field census.

   Sciences, Astronomy and Cosmology, Social and Collective Systems, and
   Engineering Translation retain complete foundations and current return
   families, but their complete full-field censuses are not yet uniformly
   frozen and closed.

   family, while the final current-graph one-owner merge and terminal global
   reconciliation remain open.

### 27.3 Open computational-proof work

Protein Fold V3 must complete and independently verify its remaining exact
fixed-geometry bands, reconcile the global frontier, compose the complete

controls, freeze all target and comparator infrastructure, preregister the

### 27.4 Source and transport limitations

Some scientific evidence assets exceed GitHub's ordinary 100 MB object limit.
Their omission from normal Git storage is declared, and their byte identities
and archival routes remain in the relevant manifests. A later large-file
distribution may improve access without changing the claims. Failed source
access and unavailable transport are retained as transport states, not
converted into absence or null evidence.

The release must also be self-contained with respect to its publication
authority. Historical ToE text stored outside this repository may be cited by
published DOI or imported as a checksum-bound archival input; it cannot remain
an unbound local prerequisite of the public package.

### 27.5 Editorial and interpretive limitations

The monograph spans formal science, natural science, computation, biological
and social systems, and engineering. A single narrative cannot replace the
specialist detail retained in each complete branch paper and audit package.
The three-layer design is therefore part of the scientific method: conceptual
argument here, complete audit in the branch volumes and complete execution in
the machine archive.

Readers should not infer that an unfamiliar vocabulary indicates an imported
conventional object, or that a familiar word retains every conventional
assumption. Each SFT object is defined by its carrier, dependency route and
relation. Conversely, a structural correspondence to a conventional object is
not automatically numerical identity or empirical confirmation.

## 28. Reproducibility

### 28.1 Release artefacts

The publication candidate contains three explicit levels.

1. **Conceptual monograph:** the complete dependency-ordered paper, rendered
   PDF and source Markdown.
2. **Scientific audit:** the complete 2,751-row claim inventory, seventeen
   branch audit volumes, source and evidence status, chronology, correction and
   publication tables.
3. **Machine archive:** the frozen corpus inventory, content matrix, claim
   packages, receipts, candidate censuses, controls, certificates, source
   identities, tests and release manifest.

The release manifest records the size and SHA-256 identity of every uploaded
file. The immutable Git revision binds the repository-resident machine archive.
Files excluded from ordinary Git because of size retain their separate
archival identity and are not implied to be part of the Git commit.

### 28.2 Reproduction sequence

A verifier begins from the release manifest rather than from prose:

1. verify every uploaded file identity;
2. verify the authoritative active-paper identities;
3. reconstruct the global claim ledger and branch totals;
4. verify all candidate-census and receipt identities;
5. verify one root trace and no missing dependency for every claim;
6. run the current lightweight corpus verifier;
7. run branch-specific verification commands recorded in the branch packages;
8. compare the generated paper, claim inventory and audit volumes against the
   frozen inputs; and
9. render the paper and inspect every page for layout defects.

The final approval dossier records commands, environment, exit status, counts,
hashes, page totals and any skipped or unavailable heavy operation. A missing
dependency, stale hash, malformed table, unresolved placeholder or
scientifically material discrepancy halts publication rather than being
silently repaired in prose.

### 28.3 Independent criticism

Reproduction is not restricted to the included verifier. Anyone may construct
an independent implementation, challenge candidate completeness, identify an
unregistered alternative, dispute a source classification, rerun an empirical
comparison or expose a defect. A valid challenge enters through a public
record and receives the same source, control and chronology custody as a
favourable result.

## 30. Conclusion

Smithian Fold Theory V3 now exists as one public dependency system rather than
as a collection of disconnected claims. It begins from the operational
impossibility of presenting nothing as a counterexample, derives the
structural One and the minimal Fold, and develops exact mathematical,
informational and computational structures before approaching physical
measurement. Physics reconstructs quantities, fields, matter, constants and
their evidence interfaces; Chemistry reconstructs identity, composition,
bonding and reaction; Materials reconstructs collective structure and
property. Biology and the later branches then receive these objects without
being permitted to alter their ownership.

The present corpus contains 2,751 admitted claims generated from 892,246
candidates, with one retained survivor per claim, 11,004 passed controls,
matched receipts and complete root traces. Seven branches from Mathematics
through Materials close their frozen current-evidence full-field scopes.
Biology and the later natural, cognitive, collective and engineering branches
retain completed foundations and returned families while their wider
full-field programmes remain explicit. Cross-Branch Synthesis closes the
current twelve-claim inherited-return surface without pretending to own the
primitive laws it composes.

The major conflation resolved by the V3 structure is the conflation of a law
with its measurement, implementation, reputation or later application. A
formal survivor is not an observation. A successful test is not automatically
a derivation. A conventional equation is not an SFT premise. A measured
decimal is not permitted to select the exact law it later compares with. A
historical program result is not silently promoted into the clean V3 system.
These separations permit strong claims where the exact record warrants them
and equally visible adverse or unresolved status where it does not.

The computational-proof programme is the next empirical edge. Protein Fold V3
has already advanced from local historical results to exact whole-system
representation, interaction custody, certified search and progressively
larger closed physical boundaries. Its remaining frontier and AlphaFold
campaign are declared before completion. Chess Fold, Go Fold and Unison Fold
AI retain their earlier measured histories while the V3 recreations are
required to earn new status independently.

The open work is therefore named rather than hidden: Biology continuation,
the downstream full-field censuses, the final one-owner lineage merge, the
release-frozen heavy verification and the four computational-proof programmes.
Their dependency positions and present evidence states are preserved so that a
later result can extend this edition without rewriting its chronology.

This preliminary edition is therefore neither a short announcement nor the
claim that inquiry has ended. It is the full current scientific object,
presented at the scale required to expose its dependency structure and audit
surface. Later versions will add the remaining field-wide and computational
results, corrections and new evidence while preserving the history reported
here. Completion means completion of the registered project investigation and
release boundary—not the end of science.
