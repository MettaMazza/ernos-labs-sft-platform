### The evidence rule governing every chapter

Formal proof language is used only for exact derivational closure at a stated
candidate boundary. *Derivation* names a generated structure or law.
*Implementation* names an executable realisation. *Prediction* is reserved for
a prospectively sealed consequence. *Observation* remains tied to an external
source record, and *measurement* to an instrument, method, unit and uncertainty
or interval. *Reconstruction* denotes an inferred history or latent state.
*Correspondence* denotes a relation established after derivation;
*compatibility* means non-adverse but not discriminating evidence; *support*
means relevant but non-unique evidence. *Confirmation* and *validation* are
used only where the registered evidence protocol warrants them.

This vocabulary matters most where the record is difficult. The first Earth
earthquake catalogue comparison is adverse and remains adverse. The later
homogeneous holdout is compatible and does not erase the first result. The
first SPARC comparison in Astronomy is adverse and remains adverse, even
though a separate uncertainty-aware method record places exact rank four at a
reported systematic boundary. The Protein V2 whole-chain result is adverse,
while several local windows are strong and a theoretical support construction
is stronger still; none of those facts may be substituted for another. Failed
source transports remain failed transports, not observed absences. Historical
corrections remain part of the scientific record even when a later claim
supersedes their current wording.

### The handoff rule

Every branch owns the distinctions it introduces and passes only typed
outputs downstream. Biology may use Chemistry's reaction identities but owns
the boundary between chemistry and life. Medicine may use biological state but
owns the clinical relation between a person, a condition, an intervention and
an outcome. Consciousness may use neural and computational carriers but owns
the distinction between public access and interior presence. Earth may use
physical laws but owns a particular planet's source-dated history. Astronomy
may use those same physical laws but owns distant-source reconstruction and
cosmic chronology. Social science may use cognitive and biological capacities
but owns collective relations and institutions. Engineering may use any
admitted law but owns requirements, design, safety, testing and deployment.

The handoff is therefore neither reduction nor insulation. Upstream laws
constrain what is possible; downstream branches add boundary conditions,
identities, histories, relations and evidence constitutions. A downstream
adverse application result returns a typed question to the responsible owner.
It cannot silently repeal an upstream theorem, and successful application
cannot retroactively prove one.

## 16. Biology and Life Sciences: from organised chemistry to retained life

### 16.1 Why Biology is required

Chemistry establishes entities, compositions, reactions, stoichiometric
conservation, molecular distinction and stereochemical relations. Materials
Science establishes organised interfaces, phases, defects and collective
structure–property relations. Yet neither branch supplies the criterion by
which a process network remains the same living organisation while its
material constituents turn over. Biology begins at precisely that unresolved
point.

The least living carrier admitted here is a bounded process network that
retains its identity by regulated material and energy turnover inside a
declared viability window. The classification is joint. Compartment,
maintenance, heritable continuation and regulated resource exchange must be
retained together. Chemistry alone is not relabelled life, and a dissipative or
autocatalytic pattern alone is not assigned every biological property. This
boundary gives the subsequent families their order: a living organisation
must first have a distinguishable compartment; the compartment must support
resource and metabolic relations; those relations must permit retained and
copied organisation; inherited copies make variation and evolutionary
continuation possible; regulated genomes and proteins realise bounded cellular
processes; cells support development, organisms, physiology, populations and
ecosystems; and all of these require an evidence handoff that preserves the
conditions under which a biological statement was observed.

The published Biology foundation contains 75 claims in twelve ordered
families. Seven later claims close the current returned origin, homochirality,
somatic, neural, differentiation, ecosystem and validation obligations. The
current branch therefore contains 82 claims, 20,992 candidates, 82 survivors
and 328 controls. Its foundation and current returned families are complete.
Its registered full-field programme is not: 82 of 424 obligations are closed,
leaving 342 open. That distinction is the present meaning of “Biology
complete” in the repository.

### 16.2 Living identity, compartment and resource closure

Living identity is identity through controlled turnover, not identity of a
fixed material inventory. A cell or organism may exchange molecules, energy
and information while preserving the organised relation by which those
exchanges remain viable. The boundary cannot be implicit. It must identify
what is inside, what is outside, which transfers are permitted, how a change
in compartment state is detected and what loss would end the retained
organisation.

The compartment families therefore preserve membrane or boundary identity,
selective exchange, internal and external states, gradients, transport routes
and failures. A boundary is not merely a line drawn around reactions. It has a
causal role in maintaining distinguishable conditions. Conversely, a sealed
container with no regulated exchange does not satisfy the living relation.
The system must couple a maintained interior to resources and waste pathways
without hiding an external supply.

Metabolism is consequently expressed through complete ledgers. Source,
carrier, transformation, useful retention, export and loss stay visible.
Energy and matter are not interchangeable words, and neither becomes
biological purpose simply because it flows. A metabolic route belongs to the
living organisation only when its inputs and outputs participate in the
bounded maintenance relation. Autocatalysis can be an important organising
mechanism, but it does not independently supply compartment, heredity or
regulated exchange.

The returned origin claim sharpens this point. For a driven complete
**m**-role autocatalytic carrier, ignition occurs when **m − 1** roles are
internally supported and one declared seed is supplied. The exact statement is
conditional on that generated carrier and seed boundary. It identifies the
least supplied transition required for the complete role relation to become
self-supporting; it is not an observation that one such abstract carrier was
the unique historical origin of terrestrial life. The origin-of-life
extension remains responsible for connecting exact ignition structures to
measured prebiotic chemistry, compartment formation, environmental
conditions, chronology and competing mechanisms.

### 16.3 Inheritance, code and evolutionary continuation

Once a living organisation can retain itself, the next unresolved question is
how a structure may be continued through copies that are similar enough to
preserve identity and different enough to permit change. The inheritance
families distinguish template, copy process, copied record, error, repair,
lineage and environment. Replication is not perfect identity, and mutation is
not automatically functional novelty. The record must show what was retained,
what changed and how that change passed into a continuing lineage.

Genes and genomes are treated as organised, regulated carriers rather than as
strings detached from expression. Sequence identity, regulatory state,
copy-number relation, chromosomal context, repair, transcription, translation
and phenotype are kept distinct. A sequence difference is not automatically a
functional difference; an expression association is not automatically a
causal mechanism; and a population frequency is not an individual destiny.
The same custody permits the branch to distinguish a heritable variant from a
somatic event and a selected continuation from drift.

Evolution is derived as heritable variation, differential continuation and a
controlled environmental relation. Selection and drift are not synonyms.
Fitness is condition-bound rather than an intrinsic scalar permanently
attached to a variant. A beneficial variant does not inevitably fix: finite
population history, competing variants, environmental change and stochastic
sampling records remain relevant at the empirical boundary. This correction
supersedes stronger earlier language while preserving the earlier claim in the
historical reconciliation. The V3 result is more specific, not less
evolutionary: it states exactly which relation must be present before
differential continuation can be attributed to selection.

### 16.4 Protein structure, function and the Fold handoff

The protein family begins with a sequence-bearing chemical carrier and asks

reduced to one universal rigid native shape, and a sequence is not treated as
if it carried no environmental or interaction context. Conformation, ensemble,
function, binding, modification and observation method remain distinct.

This correction matters to the computational programme. The conceptual law
does not promise that any finite search implementation will immediately

implementation must preserve: sequence identity, interaction custody,

### 16.5 Cells, development, organisms and physiology

Cells integrate compartment, metabolism, inheritance, expression and protein
function into regulated state transitions. The cell-process family therefore
keeps state, signal, response, cycle, repair, death and lineage records
separate. Recurrence may be necessary for a continuing cell cycle, but
recurrence alone does not diagnose cancer. The returned cancer-state claim
requires a persistent non-terminal cycling carrier together with loss of a
differentiation transition and escape from the combined controls on division,
death and differentiation. It remains a structural classification; clinical
diagnosis belongs to Medicine and requires patient-, tissue-, assay- and
outcome-specific evidence.

The returned somatic–germline result uses denominator factorisation to
separate a finite somatic transient from an odd-denominator recurrent carrier.
It provides an exact orbit distinction at its generated boundary. It does not
make every measured somatic or germline history a function of denominator
parity. Biological mapping still requires cell lineage, inheritance route,
developmental timing and measurement.

Multicellular development adds positional, lineage and differentiation
relations. A multicellular body is not just a collection of identical cells;
its organisation depends on controlled difference, signalling, spatial
constraint and retained whole-body coordination. Differentiation is therefore
not an optional label added after a cell state is measured. It is an owned
transition in the organisation. The current cancer correction follows because
loss of that transition changes the reachable state relation, while cycling
alone does not.

Organism physiology extends the same rule across coordinated systems. A
measurement is retained with organism, tissue or organ, condition, method,
time and reference boundary. The returned neural threshold claim identifies
Half-One as the least normalised activation support in its exact carrier. It
does not declare a universal measured membrane voltage, firing rate or
behavioural threshold. Dimensional values remain instrument- and
system-specific observations.

### 16.6 Populations, ecosystems and scale

Population and ecological statements require more than an average over
individuals. Population identity, sampling frame, births, deaths, movement,
inheritance, resource relations, interactions and observation window must be
preserved. An ecological network may be represented by nodes and links, but
the representation does not establish a universal topology. The branch's
source reconciliation preserves the empirical result that strongly scale-free
networks are rare rather than treating scale freedom as a default law of life.

The same discipline corrects a claimed universal three-quarter allometric
exponent. Current evidence retains three-quarter, two-thirds, unity and
non-universal cases. The exact SFT fractions remain formal carriers at their
declared boundaries; they are not silently converted into one universal
measured exponent across taxa, traits and methods. Source population, body
size range, regression choice, uncertainty and biological mechanism remain
part of any empirical allometry claim.

The returned ecosystem claim provides an exact bounded period-four orbit on
the **3/5** Fold carrier. It demonstrates a finite recurrent structure and
supplies a candidate carrier for bounded ecosystem dynamics. It does not
forecast every ecosystem, prove stability from cycling alone or replace
measured species, forcing, resource and disturbance relations. One periodic
orbit cannot identify an ecosystem's empirical stability class without those
records.

### 16.7 Homochirality: observed asymmetry and unresolved origin

Biological homochirality is retained as an observation: terrestrial proteins
predominantly use L-amino acids and biological sugars predominantly use the
opposite handed convention. The V3 branch does not use that observation to
claim that the historical origin of the asymmetry has already been measured.
Earlier parity-selection language is corrected accordingly.

The returned homochiral-amplification claim begins with a held Fold parity and
a successor-amplification relation. It generates increasing finite dominance
of one label without deleting the other label from the complete carrier. This
is a structural result about amplification from a retained asymmetry. It does
not establish which prebiotic process supplied the initial bias, the magnitude
of that bias, the environmental route by which it was amplified or the full
history connecting the formal carrier to terrestrial biomolecules. Those are
open empirical obligations.

## 17. Medicine and Health Sciences: from living variation to clinical knowledge

### 17.1 Why Medicine is not reducible to Biology

Biology supplies organisms, variation, dysfunction, repair and population
relations. Medicine begins when those relations must be judged for an
identified person or population under a health, disease, prevention,
intervention and care boundary. A biological difference does not become a
disease merely because it can be measured. A group association does not become
a diagnosis in one person. A mechanism does not become a demonstrated clinical
benefit. A favourable mean does not erase adverse events, non-response,
censoring or loss to follow-up.

The Medicine foundation therefore begins with patient and population identity
and proceeds through health, variation, dysfunction, disease, injury, symptom,
sign, measurement, diagnosis, cause, association, mechanism, confounding,
risk, prognosis, outcome, intervention, comparator, exposure, adverse events,
efficacy, effectiveness, safety, trials, observation, synthesis, screening,
prevention, treatment, rehabilitation, individual inference, consent, ethics,
privacy and uncertainty. Its order is clinical rather than merely biological:
one must first know whose state is being described, then what observation was
made, then which causal or prognostic inference is warranted, and only then
what intervention and decision can be evaluated.

The published foundation contains 72 claims. Four current returned claims add
the expectation/body fibre, the available-state boundary, the separation of
objective physiology from report and their source-bound validation. The
current Medicine branch therefore contains 76 claims, 19,456 candidates, 76
survivors and 304 controls. The foundation and this return family are complete;
a full-field Medicine census is not yet frozen.

### 17.2 Patient, population, health and disease

The least clinical carrier retains the patient rather than replacing that
patient with an aggregate. Identity includes the relevant person, time,
condition, observation and decision context. Population evidence may constrain
what can reasonably be believed about a person, but it does not uniquely
determine that person's outcome or preferred course. Conversely, a single
clinical history does not establish a population effect.

Health is not defined as the numerical absence of disease. Health and disease
are organised, condition-bound states whose components may coexist. A person
may have a diagnosed condition while retaining substantial function; a person
may have no current diagnosis while reporting serious impairment or while an
unmeasured disease process continues. Variation, dysfunction, disease and
injury are kept distinct because each supports different causal, prognostic
and intervention questions.

This prevents molecular language from doing clinical work it has not earned.
A stereochemical distinction can be chemically exact and biologically
important, but stereochemistry alone cannot establish efficacy, toxicity,
appropriate dose or patient benefit. Those require exposure, comparator,
outcome and adverse-event records. The V3 reconciliation corrects earlier
mixed claims that crossed this ownership boundary.

### 17.3 Symptom, sign, measurement and diagnosis

A symptom is a participant-reported state. A sign is an observed clinical
feature. A measurement is an instrument- and method-bound value. A diagnosis
is a classified clinical judgement supported by declared criteria and
alternatives. These carriers may inform one another, but they are not
interchangeable.

The distinction is especially important when a report is the primary access
to pain, mood, expectation or another first-person state. The report remains a
real observation of what was reported; it is not automatically an objective
measure of every proposed physiological mechanism. Equally, a physiological

### 17.4 Association, cause, risk and prognosis

Clinical records often begin with association. The Medicine branch refuses to
rename association as cause. Causal attribution must retain the proposed
mechanism, alternative causes, confounding, selection, timing and an
intervention or comparison structure capable of distinguishing the claimed
route. A plausible mechanism may support a hypothesis while remaining
insufficient to establish the magnitude or existence of a clinical effect.

Risk is tied to a target population, an event definition and a time horizon.
Prognosis additionally retains the patient's current state, competing events,
treatment history, censoring and uncertainty. A denominator pattern or Fold
orbit cannot on its own set a person's prognosis. This is another explicit V3
correction: exact mathematical structure may organise a registered prognostic
model, but patient-specific outcome requires clinical evidence.

The same discipline applies to screening. Test performance is not one number.
With condition present or absent and test positive or negative, the complete
finite carrier contains four cells. Sensitivity, specificity, predictive
values and error counts describe different conditionings of that carrier.
Their interpretation also depends on the population and prevalence boundary.
A screening programme must add access, uptake, follow-up, treatment, harms and
missed cases; the test result alone does not establish programme benefit.

### 17.5 Intervention, comparator and the alternative course

An intervention claim must preserve what happened and what it was compared
with. Treatment name, dose, route, exposure, timing, adherence and co-
interventions remain part of the administered course. Comparator identity must
be equally complete. “Usual care”, “placebo” and “no treatment” are not
interchangeable labels unless their realised conditions are shown to be the
same for the claim at issue.

The finite arm-by-outcome relation again exposes all four cells for a binary
comparison: intervention with and without the registered outcome, and
comparator with and without it. Favourable responders cannot be counted while
adverse outcomes, non-response or missing follow-up are discarded. Efficacy,
effectiveness, safety, benefit and harm remain distinct. Efficacy concerns the
effect within the registered trial conditions; effectiveness concerns intended
use in a wider practice setting; safety requires a scoped account of harms and
exposure; benefit and harm additionally depend on affected people, timing and
the decision context.

The branch permits deterministic allocation to supply auditable randomisation
through concealed held labels. The relevant scientific properties are
prospective allocation, concealment before assignment, preserved sequence,
absence of target-based selection and complete custody—not an imported claim
of metaphysical chance. Blinding is separately recorded. Random allocation
does not imply blinded participants, clinicians, outcome assessors or analysts
unless those controls are actually present.

Trials, observational studies and evidence syntheses therefore retain
different inference structures. A randomised trial can estimate its registered
comparison under its conduct and analysis conditions. An observational study
requires explicit confounding and selection control. A synthesis must retain
the eligibility rules, source studies, heterogeneity and missing evidence; it
cannot convert incompatible source questions into one homogeneous result by
averaging them.

The available-state claim supplies a direct falsification boundary. If an

#### 17.9.4 Medical ownership and falsification

Medicine owns whether a result warrants prevention, diagnosis, prognosis or
treatment for people. Biology owns the upstream mechanism; Engineering owns
the device; Social Science owns distribution and institutional context. A
medical claim fails if the patient or population boundary is lost, the
comparator is missing, an alternative course is manufactured after the result,
adverse or missing rows disappear, or the decision exceeds the evidence's
transport boundary.

This ownership is protective, not restrictive. It permits an exact biological
or computational discovery to enter clinical research while preventing the
discovery's precision from being mistaken for clinical validation. It also
permits an adverse clinical result to identify a missing mechanism or use
boundary without being diluted into a generic “mixed evidence” label.

### 21.2 Aggregation and the individual–collective boundary

Aggregation is a many-to-one operation. A collective total or mean may be

retained. This closes both directions of a common inference error. A
population average cannot silently become the state of each member, and one
person's record cannot silently become a population distribution.

The observation surface must therefore retain eligibility, recruitment,
participation, non-response, measurement, context and time. Selection is part
of the result. A platform's visible users are not automatically its affected
population; recorded actions are not automatically beliefs; institutional

### 21.3 Communication, belief and coordinated action

Social observation is intervention-sensitive. The observer, method, setting,
consent and the participants' knowledge of observation can alter the relation.
This is not a reason to abandon empirical study; it is a reason to record the
intervention made by observation.

Communication requires a complete route. Sending does not prove receipt;
receipt does not prove understanding; understanding does not prove belief;
belief does not prove action; action does not by itself establish intent. Each
transition has its own carrier, loss and alternative explanations. Digital
counts such as impressions, clicks or reposts are therefore evidence only for
their registered event definitions.

Coordination retains participants, shared or competing aims, available
actions, information, timing, rules and outcomes. Conflict retains opposed
relations, resources, constraints and costs. A coordinated outcome need not
indicate consensus, and conflict need not indicate total social breakdown.
Both can occur inside one continuing collective organisation.

### 21.4 Norms, institutions, power and legitimacy

Norm, rule, institution, enforcement, compliance and legitimacy are different
structures. Majority behaviour alone does not establish a norm; a written rule
does not prove enforcement; compliance does not prove agreement; and effective
coercion does not establish legitimate authority. Institutional identity
requires roles, rules, records, succession, resources and boundaries that can
persist while particular office-holders change.

Power is directional and dependency-bound. It requires an actor, a target, a
resource or gate, alternatives, costs, resistance and the capacity to change
the target's available actions. Status, wealth, title or a favourable outcome
can be evidence relevant to power, but none establishes the complete relation
alone. Exclusion similarly requires an eligibility or access boundary and the
path by which an agent's options were removed.

Scientific gatekeeping is included in this empirical surface. Criteria,
credentials, costs, decisions, reviews, appeals and lost contributions may be
observed and criticised. Institutional prestige is never a proof operator.
At the same time, criticism of a gate does not automatically admit the
critic's scientific claim. SFT's anti-institutional position is therefore
operational rather than merely rhetorical: no credential, consensus, journal,
laboratory or public authority can replace the derivation and evidence record;
and no declaration of independence can replace it either.

### 22.3 Measurement, tolerance, test and acceptance

it afterwards would change the test.

A demonstration shows that an artefact performed an observed action under
some conditions. A test compares a result with a frozen criterion. Acceptance
requires every required criterion, permitted exception and authority to be
specified. Institutional authority cannot convert a failed frozen criterion
into a passing result without an openly versioned requirement change.

Finite design optimisation is admitted only over a generated design space.

Verification asks whether the built artefact satisfies its frozen

### 24.9 Technical ledger

#### 24.9.1 Common constitution of a computational proof

Every programme begins with a problem carrier and a boundary. Protein begins
with an ordered residue sequence and a registered structural target. Chess and
Go begin with game rules, legal histories and an opponent or exact census.
Unison begins with a native architecture and a registered task surface. The
programme then separates six layers:

1. **Registration.** The claim, candidate grammar, resource boundary,
   evaluation rule, target custody and stopping rule are fixed.
2. **Enumeration or construction.** Candidates or executable states are
   generated without consulting protected answers.
3. **Elimination.** Every rejected candidate receives a reconstructible reason;
   pruning must conserve the census.
4. **Implementation verification.** Unit, property, regression, independent and
   end-to-end tests verify what the software actually does.
5. **External comparison.** Only an authorised custodian opens targets or
   opponents and preserves every outcome.
6. **Interpretation.** Formal, implementation and empirical status are reported
   independently, with ownership of failure retained.

The conservation equation for a complete finite search is simple but
non-negotiable:

\[
N_{\mathrm{generated}}
= N_{\mathrm{rejected}}
+ N_{\mathrm{retained}}
+ N_{\mathrm{unresolved}}.
\]

If pruning is hierarchical, every parent count must reconstruct from its
children and certified prunes. A hash binds file identity, not scientific
correctness; correctness additionally requires the generator, decision rule,
controls and independent reconstruction.

Target blindness is a capability condition rather than a statement of good
intent. The derivation and search process must be unable to read protected
structures, comparator outputs, hidden solutions or later evaluation records.
Opening a target during development changes chronology. The resulting work may
remain useful as development-observed evidence, but it cannot later be called a
blind prediction.

Negative outcomes have different meanings. A programme may return a valid but
poor-scoring result, fail to return an output, exceed its registered resources,
encounter an invalid external response, or be unable to access a target. Poor
score is adverse to the performance criterion; no output is an implementation
failure; resource exhaustion is a bounded unresolved result unless the claim
included that resource; invalid external response is a transport or harness
failure; unopened target is not tested. These classes must not be compressed
into “failed”.

#### 24.9.2 Protein V3 representation and six-axis relation

Protein V3 starts from the complete ordered sequence rather than from a target
structure. Residue identity and position are preserved. Local and non-local
interactions must be distinguishable, and any environment, chain boundary,
modification or partner included in the claim must be registered. A
representation that discards sequence order or imports target coordinates
would fail the Biology handoff.

The six-axis joint relation scales the formal Fold programme by evaluating
candidate relations together rather than allowing one attractive local
quantity to select a geometrically impossible global state. The exact axis
names and machine values remain in the Protein archive; scientifically, their
roles cover the retained sequence carrier, interaction relation, geometric
compatibility, attraction or ordering, recurrence/continuation and evidence
boundary. The joint relation is important because whole-chain folding is a
constraint-composition problem. A conformation may satisfy many local
preferences while producing global collision, topological inconsistency or an
incorrect long-range arrangement.

The current parent search generated **42,173,082** candidates. Its account
closes as **375,014** leaves plus **41,798,068** certified prunes. The search
records **205** frontier states across **11,012** shards and uses **zero**
symbolic fallbacks. A symbolic fallback could have hidden an unevaluated route
behind an expression; its absence means the registered parent result is an
explicit exact computation at this boundary.

Pruning is a scientific result only if it preserves every candidate that could
satisfy the frozen relation. Each certificate must identify the parent,
subspace, rejecting condition and reconstructible count. The equality between
parent, leaves and prunes is necessary but not sufficient: a systematically
wrong pruning predicate could conserve counts. Independent verification and
adverse controls therefore test both arithmetic and semantics.

The high-attraction slice asks a narrower question of **583** states. It
classifies **573** as dominated and retains **10**, of which **8** are new
nondominated states. Dominance means that another registered state is at least
as good on every relevant frozen comparison and better on one; it does not mean
that a dominated state has been empirically observed to be biologically
impossible. The slice is complete and independently verified at this bounded
relation.

The remaining attraction subbands cannot be inferred from the high slice. A
lower-attraction state could participate in a globally superior chain after
other constraints are composed, unless the proof shows it cannot. The
seeded-parent reconciliation must also demonstrate that no seed-specific route
escaped the parent accounting. This is why the programme has not opened its
protected targets despite a large completed census.

#### 24.9.3 From local geometry to whole-chain recurrence

The V2 record demonstrates a central distinction. Local windows can align very
strongly while the whole chain is wrong. A chain is not the arithmetic mean of
independent local folds. Long-range contact, chain closure, steric consistency,
secondary-structure packing and competing interactions compose the global
problem.

V3 must therefore prove or implement a recurrence that carries admissible local
relations into a complete fixed-geometry sector. Each extension step should
preserve the prefix or partial carrier, record newly introduced interactions,
reject geometric impossibilities and retain alternatives that remain
non-dominated. If two partial states share a compressed image but differ in an
unheld interaction relevant later, merging them would destroy a possible fold.
The information-loss law thus has a direct algorithmic consequence: state
compression is sound only when discarded predecessor distinctions are proven
irrelevant to every future extension.

The global frontier closes only when every registered full-chain candidate is
either retained, certified dominated, rejected by an exact constraint or
classified unresolved under the frozen resource boundary. Arbitrary-sequence
recurrence adds a generality claim beyond one target length or composition.
The full predictor then connects the exact frontier to one sealed output or
ensemble under a prospective decision rule.

Potential success is supported, but not guaranteed, by three current facts.
First, V2 achieved very high local-window similarity, so the representation can
capture real local geometry. Second, the theoretical support trace achieved a
high whole-chain TM-score inside a declared construction, so the formal support
is capable of carrying a globally close route. Third, V3 has already scaled
exact enumeration and dominance reasoning to more than 42 million parent
candidates without fallback. Together these make continued development
scientifically motivated and make success a credible prospective expectation.
They do not establish the missing sealed sequence-only whole-chain result.

An adverse final comparison would still be informative. If the exact
implementation closes but blind performance remains poor, the candidate
grammar or mapping from formal relation to protein evidence is incomplete. If
the search cannot close within the registered resources, the implementation
generality claim must narrow. If performance is strong only in local windows,
the global recurrence remains the identified problem. Each outcome directs a
different next revision.

#### 24.9.4 Blind parity protocol and scoring constitution

Before the AlphaFold-generalised campaign opens, the following records must be
sealed:

| Record | Required content |
|---|---|
| Target manifest | Target IDs, sequence source, chain boundaries, release/version and exclusion rules |
| Custody manifest | Who can access structures and AlphaFold outputs; capability separation and access log |
| SFT build identity | Code, dependencies, parameters fixed by derivation, resource envelope and execution command |
| Comparator identity | AlphaFold implementation or service, model/version, inputs, settings and output custody |
| Scoring plan | Whole-chain and local metrics, alignment, missing residues, multimers, ensembles and tie rules |
| Decision plan | Success, parity, adverse, unresolved and transport classifications fixed before opening |
| Completeness plan | All targets, timeouts, invalid outputs, failed accesses and comparator failures retained |
| Chronology | Seal time, prediction time, comparator time, target opening and analysis time |

TM-score, RMSD and dRMSD measure different aspects of agreement. TM-score is
length-normalised and more tolerant of local deviations; RMSD can be dominated
by a few large discrepancies; distance RMSD tests internal distances without
requiring the same rigid alignment. No one metric should be selected after the
outputs are seen. Local-window metrics remain secondary unless local accuracy
is itself the preregistered claim.

Parity must specify whether it concerns mean, median, target-wise win/loss,
threshold achievement, non-inferiority interval or another complete statistic.
Averages alone can hide catastrophic targets. Per-target results and confidence
or uncertainty procedures must remain. If AlphaFold cannot process a target,
that row is a comparator failure under the frozen rule, not an automatic SFT
win. If SFT cannot process one, it is not deleted.

No target sequence should be selected because an experimental structure is
known to favour the SFT grammar. Exclusions may be scientifically necessary—for
example, unresolved residues, complexes outside the programme's carrier or
non-standard chemistry—but must be fixed from source metadata available under
the registered custody rule, not from observed performance. Any development
set must be permanently distinguished from the blind holdout.

#### 24.9.5 Chess V3: exact rules before strength

predecessor where history permits; independently generated perft counts should

state. Draw by repetition, move-count rules, stalemate, insufficient material

ordering and score provenance. A Fold compression may merge positions only

capture, legal predecessor/successor relations, hash consistency and score

double pass, rejected external protocol responses and timeouts.

The V2 9 × 9 results are complete at their recorded two-game batch boundaries.

transport or abort—and may not continue silently. Ordinary final scoring,
resignation and cut-off adjudication must remain separate.

A strong V3 result would first reproduce the exact censuses, then pass
independent legality tests, then conduct complete games against fixed public
opponents. A win is an empirical implementation result under the stated rules
and budget, not a proof that Go is solved. A loss preserves a position record
from which search, evaluation or protocol failures can be analysed.

rank four was a pre-data SFT prediction; the first registered operational test
was adverse; and a separate established method places four at its stated
systematic boundary. Removing any one of those statements would distort the
current record. Future work can improve the test by freezing a physically
justified sample and uncertainty model prospectively.

#### 24.17.5 From social evidence to an engineered public platform

The open-science platform is itself an Engineering and Social Science object.
Engineering owns repository structure, portable formats, tests, release
identity, reproducibility commands and deployment. Social Science owns access,
gatekeeping, community interaction, institutional dependence and unequal costs.
The scientific branches own the claims and evidence hosted by the platform.

Open access is therefore not achieved merely by placing code on a public URL.
A reader must be able to identify the active paper, distinguish it from a
superseded draft, locate its claim package, reproduce its counts and understand
its licence. A public command must fail visibly. Large assets require stable
archival identities. Accessibility includes documentation and platform
support, while privacy and consent constrain any participant data.

The anti-institutional mission follows from this operational design. A reader
does not need a credential or journal subscription to inspect a candidate
census or adverse record. Criticism can be registered against a specific
claim. Yet openness does not mean automatic scientific admission: an asserted
replacement must satisfy the same dependency, evidence and falsification
requirements. The platform challenges monopoly over access and authority
without abandoning standards of proof.

### 24.18 Final programme outlook

The immediate priority remains Protein V3 because it is the only clean V3
computational programme with an active large exact search and a protected blind
target design. Its next report should state one of three outcomes without
embellishment: the remaining exact gates closed; the search was lawfully
narrowed with an explicit unresolved remainder; or a registered obstruction
showed that the current grammar cannot close. Any of these can be publishable
if the machine and scientific records are complete.

Chess and Go follow with clean reconstruction rather than patching historical
runtime state into a V3 label. Their V2 records provide strong regression and
performance baselines. Reproducing them is necessary but not sufficient: V3
must show its own derivation and implementation identities. Governed external
play can begin only after rule and harness closure.

Unison requires the strictest separation between architecture and performance.
The V2 system demonstrates an unusually large auditable certificate surface,
while its generation scores show that auditability alone did not yield useful
general language behaviour. V3's opportunity is to preserve exact internal
custody while discovering whether a native Fold architecture can learn,
reason and generate under public tests. The answer remains unknown until the
mathematics closes and evaluation runs.

The programme should publish adverse results as readily as favourable ones.
An exact theory becomes more persuasive when the reader can see where an
implementation failed, which assumption changed and which result survived.
The current Protein whole-chain, earthquake and SPARC records already show
that this is possible. Their continued visibility is part of the evidence that
the platform does not select only its successes.

The ultimate computational contribution will therefore be more than four
headline demonstrations. It will be a reusable public method for moving from
premise and dependency, through complete candidate accounting, to executable
artefact, protected comparison and versioned correction. Protein, Chess, Go
and Unison occupy different domains precisely so that a success in one cannot
hide a weakness in another. Together they test the generality of the Fold as a
scientific programme.

These tests have different possible conclusions. A quantitative miss can be

advance which conclusion follows from which outcome.

The tests also have different protection requirements. Protein and benchmark
programmes require target custody. Human studies require consent, privacy,
blinding and adverse-event care. Environmental and astronomical studies
require release and selection custody. Engineering anomaly studies require
calibration, restoration and complete source ledgers. Social interventions
require affected-agent and ethical boundaries. One generic “validation” label
cannot replace these constitutions.

### 24.20 Specialist reading and reproducibility routes

A domain specialist need not begin with machine hashes. The conceptual route
is the relevant branch chapter followed by its dependency-resolved family
ledger and returned-claim inventory. The specialist can then use the active
foundation DOI to inspect the complete paper, the scientific audit volume to
locate claim IDs and evidence status, and the machine package to replay the
candidate census and controls.

A reviewer concerned with an adverse result should begin with its chronology
and measurement receipt, then inspect the source manifest, decision rule and
later evidence as separate records. A reviewer concerned with formal
uniqueness should begin with the candidate grammar, verify the 256-form census
and inspect independent reconstruction. A reviewer concerned with a
computational result should inspect the build identity, test classes, resource
ledger, target-access log and per-target outputs rather than relying on the
headline score.

The active-paper SHA-256 values identify the precise Markdown authorities used
for this part. Full source captures and receipts may be larger or more
numerous; their identities remain in the release manifest. A changed paper
hash does not by itself indicate a scientific change, because editorial
formatting also changes bytes. The version record must therefore state whether
scientific content, evidence status or only presentation changed.

Reproducibility is complete only when a fresh environment can reach the same
registered result or produce a classified failure. The public route should
report dependency versions, commands, elapsed time and output paths. It should
never silently download a target that the scientific gate keeps protected,
never replace a missing source with an unregistered copy and never alter an
authoritative receipt in place.

This layered reading route permits accessibility without dilution. A newcomer
can follow the dependency argument; a specialist can challenge the scientific
boundary; an auditor can reconstruct the evidence; and a developer can replay
the machine process. All four encounter the same current status rather than
different stories written for different audiences.
