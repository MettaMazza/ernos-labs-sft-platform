## Historical trajectory and V3 reconciliation

Smithian Fold Theory did not appear as a finished monograph with a settled
branch architecture. It developed during a concentrated sequence of
foundational writing, derivation, implementation, correction, public release
and clean-room reconstruction. The first public lineage asked whether
*nothing* could ever be presented as a counterexample to occurrence. The
following synthesis extended that root towards mathematics and physics.
Specialist papers separated particular constants, quantum relations,
mathematical boundaries and proposed physical consequences. Protein folding,
Chess Fold, Go Fold and Unison Fold AI then tested whether Fold relations could
be made executable outside the proof papers. The V3 programme finally rebuilt
the growing corpus as a dependency-ordered, one-owner knowledge tree with
claim-level candidate censuses, controls, evidence classes and machine
receipts.

That development is scientifically relevant, but history is not current
authority. This section therefore performs two tasks at once. It preserves
what each earlier version actually recorded, including favourable results,
adverse results, defects, failed transports and open objectives. It also
identifies the current record that now governs each result. An old publication
may establish that a statement, computation or prediction existed at a
particular point in the programme. It cannot overrule a later V3 claim package,
source capture, correction, evidence classification or verification report.
Likewise, the creation of V3 does not erase a prior result merely because its
paper is no longer the active scientific owner. The historical record remains
available so that the route from the first formulation to the present one can
be inspected rather than reconstructed from memory.

The authoritative freeze used here is dated 31 July 2026. Its direct-source
inventory identifies Maria Smith as author and publication authority and Ernos
Labs as the organisation and open-science platform. It binds the current
papers, the live claim ledger, the final seven complete-field publication
record, the current programme ledger, the checksum-bound v6 synthesis, the
historical computational manifests, the current Protein gate and the Protein
publication receipt. The inventory classifies this monograph as a preliminary
synthesis. The final computational proofs, the remaining full-field

complete. Those boundaries govern every historical statement below.

### How historical evidence is read

Four rules prevent the chronology from becoming either an advertisement or a
retrospective demolition.

First, a version label names a document in a lineage; it is not an evidence
class. The pre-V3 catalogue contains local version sequences such as 1.0,
2.0, 3.0, 3.9-final-status, 6.3 and 6.5. Those numbers were assigned within
different papers and applications. They do not form one common scale of
scientific maturity. A Protein release numbered 3.7, a ToE release numbered
6.0 and an Unison release numbered 6.3 cannot be compared by their version
numbers alone.

Second, formal closure and empirical success are separate. A candidate census
may prove that one relation survives a registered grammar without proving that
the relation predicts an external measurement. Conversely, a programme can
record a strong measured outcome without showing that its selector was
derived under the later V3 admission constitution. Both facts remain useful,
but neither may be substituted for the other.

Third, chronology is retained at the level at which the evidence was obtained.
A development-observed relation does not become blind because a later paper
describes it more elegantly. A target-isolated output remains target-isolated
even if its global score is adverse. A post-target diagnosis may motivate a
new prospective holdout, but it is not itself a prospective result. A later
successful construction may supersede an earlier implementation while leaving
the earlier adverse measurement historically intact.

Fourth, “complete” always names a boundary. The current record distinguishes a
complete operational Foundation, a complete registered family, a complete
field-wide current-evidence census, a completed implementation and a completed
empirical protocol. None of those expressions means that the discipline, the
theory or science itself has ended. This distinction becomes especially
important when comparing the compact pre-V3 synthesis with the much larger
V3 claim graph.

### The first operational root

The first scientific question was not which physical equation should be
treated as fundamental. Any ordinary equation already presupposes symbols,
objects, relations, rules of combination and a domain in which a distinction
can be made. SFT instead began by asking what could count as an operational
counterexample to the statement *there is no nothing*. If no statement,
denial, record or proof object is presented, no counterexample has been
supplied. If a purported counterexample is presented, an occurrence has been
supplied and the presentation is not nothing. The result is deliberately
operational. It does not import numerical zero, Boolean negation,
set-theoretic emptiness or a prior universe of discourse as an SFT premise.

This boundary first supplied the programme with a starting point and later
became the root theorem identified as
SFT-ROOT-THERE-IS-NO-NOTHING. The historical foundation paper explicitly
records an important internal development: the One began chronologically as
the project’s operational axiom and was subsequently derived beneath a
machine-checked self-proof. The current V3 description therefore does not
pretend that the final zero-axiom formulation was present unchanged at the
beginning. It preserves the earlier role while assigning current authority to
the reconstructed theorem and its receipt.

From the admitted occurrence, the project sought the minimum structure that
did not add an arbitrary choice. The occurrence’s complete self-whole became
the structural One. Positive finite count, exact part, distinction, retained
orientation, reversible return and the minimal Fold were separated into
successive obligations. This sequence matters. “One”, “part” and “Fold” are
not three names for the same intuition, and a later physical use of doubling
cannot be imported backwards as the reason the Fold was selected. The V3
Foundation now owns the generated objects, while the V3 Methods paper owns the
admission protocol by which they may enter the theory.

The early paper lineage *There Is No Nothing* retains concept DOI
10.5281/zenodo.21035460 and published versions 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0 in the
catalogue. It is an historical lineage rather than the current owner. The
catalogue marks every pre-V3 version with the exact public notice
“[Depreciated and replaced by V3]” and routes this concept to two successors:
the V3 Methods paper, version 0.3.0, DOI
10.5281/zenodo.21627646, and *From Nothing to Fold*, version 1.3.0, DOI
10.5281/zenodo.21627656. The literal word “Depreciated” is retained here
because it is the public catalogue label; it is not silently corrected inside
a quoted metadata identity.

The division between those two successors is substantive. Methods owns the
premise-free operational boundary, candidate-registration rules, fail-closed
admission engine, evidence vocabulary, publication authority and open
verification platform. Foundation owns the sixteen current root and
foundation claims that generate the One, count, part, distinction, Fold and
their immediate closure structure. Under this division, the constitution does
not admit itself as though it were simply another downstream physical object,
and a branch claim cannot alter the method that judged it.

These papers did more than duplicate the large synthesis. They exposed

### Why the V3 clean-room reconstruction was required

By late July the project had accumulated a large number of equations, claims,
papers, receipts and application outputs in a very short period. Speed created
real scientific reach, but it also created a custody problem. A single paper
could place an exact derivation, a successful test suite, an observed
correspondence, a post-target reconstruction, an application benchmark and a
future prediction in adjacent paragraphs. A reader could not always tell
which statement selected which, which branch owned the result or which record
would prevail if a later source contradicted the prose.

The V3 repository was established on 23 July 2026 with an explicit clean-room
constitution and knowledge tree. The next recorded operations built the
fail-closed portable admission engine, admitted the root theorem and
structural One, and then derived positive count, exact parts and the minimal
Fold. This order prohibited the old synthesis from acting as an answer key.
Prior work could register an obligation, identify a promising carrier or
supply historical evidence, but a V3 result had to survive the new grammar
and evidence procedure.

The clean-room rule addresses contamination in both directions. Earlier
measured constants cannot choose a formal candidate. Later successful
applications cannot become premises of the theory that generated them.
External scientific convention can define a comparison or measurement
interface without being smuggled into the SFT derivation. Historical
application failures can test an implementation without being treated as
refutations of a broader relation that had not yet been implemented. Each
status is attached to the boundary that produced it.

Under V3, a claim begins as a registered obligation. Its dependency route and
candidate grammar are declared. The finite candidate surface is generated and
every candidate is retained with a decision. Admission requires one unique
survivor at the stated boundary, controls and an implementation-distinct
reconstruction. A receipt seals the result. Only then is external evidence
opened according to its own chronology and source custody. The terms theorem,
law, derivation, implementation, prediction, observation, measurement,
reconstruction, correspondence, compatibility, confirmation, adverse and
unresolved are no longer interchangeable rhetorical choices.

V3 also instituted one-owner branch boundaries. Mathematics owns generated
mathematical structures. Information Science owns representation, coding and
record relations. Classical Computation owns executable machine structures.
Reversible and Quantum Computation owns reversible and quantum carriers and
their formal boundary. Physics owns dimensional and measurement interfaces.
Chemistry owns chemical identity, bonding and reaction. Materials owns
structure–property relations across scale. Biology owns living organisation,
heredity and protein function. Medicine owns clinical and health
consequences. Consciousness owns cognitive and phenomenal claims. Earth,
Astronomy, Social and Engineering own their respective domain-specific
relations. A downstream branch can consume an upstream carrier; it cannot
retroactively validate or rewrite its source branch.

This reconstruction did not reduce the ambition of SFT. It transformed a
large flat synthesis into a directed scientific graph. The gain was not merely
administrative. The graph made non-uniform completion visible. A branch could
close its foundation without claiming a complete field. A dated return family
could be complete while a wider census remained unfrozen. A machine test could
pass while an empirical outcome remained adverse or unavailable. The
programme could now say exactly where work had reached without treating every
open boundary as a failure or every local success as total completion.

### What is preserved, what is superseded and what remains open

The root question, the One, the Fold and the project’s open-science purpose are

the V3 formulations. The early papers retain priority and development context,
but their language is not used to bypass the current candidate grammars.

The breadth of v6 is also continuous. Mathematics, physics, chemistry, life,
mind and computation remain within the programme. What changed is their
custody. A v6 proposition that now has an admitted V3 successor is read through
that successor. A proposition that was narrowed remains present at the
narrower scope. A proposition with an adverse or unavailable current record is
reported as adverse or unavailable. A proposition not yet reconstructed
remains historical or open; it is not restored merely because it appeared in
the v6 abstract.

The V2 computational programmes are preserved as empirical and engineering

The seven full-field branch publications are current. Their deposit identities

### Reconciliation as a scientific result

The movement from the first no-nothing paper to this monograph is not simply a
sequence of increasingly long documents. It is a change in the unit of
scientific accountability. The first papers asked whether a premise-free
starting point and its consequences could be articulated. The large v6
synthesis demonstrated the reach of that programme and brought formal,
physical and computational work into one visible object. The applications
showed that Fold relations could generate serious executable systems and also
exposed where restricted success failed to generalise. V3 turned those
lessons into explicit rules of ownership, chronology, candidate custody and
evidence.

That reconstruction makes the project more persuasive because it makes
disagreement local. A reader need not accept an entire theory to inspect one
claim package. A failed Protein transfer does not become a vague verdict on
the root theorem; it remains an adverse result for a named selector. A passing
proof suite does not become empirical confirmation; it remains implementation
evidence. A measured physical correspondence cannot select its own exact form;
the derivation and comparison routes remain separate. A later correction does
not require forgetting what was previously published.

The resulting corpus is both larger and more bounded than v6. It is larger in
its 2,751 claims, 892,246 candidates, 11,004 controls, sixteen-paper tree and
seven field-wide publications. It is more bounded because every use of
completion, proof, prediction and validation must name its scope. The open
frontier is no longer a paragraph appended after a totalising claim. It is
part of the dependency graph itself.

The present trajectory therefore closes with a precise publication position.
The repository contains a current V3 scientific system whose root, branch
owners, live claims and public successor papers can be audited directly. It
also contains historical papers and computational records whose favourable
and adverse results remain discoverable. The proposed standalone V3 monograph
is the first theory-wide publication designed to reconcile those two surfaces at the
scale of the current corpus. It is complete for the state it freezes and
preliminary for the operations it identifies as open. Any later version must
extend or correct this record without rewriting its chronology. That is the
end of this project-version investigation, not the end of science.
