## Abstract

Smithian Fold Theory V3 is a clean-room, machine-auditable reconstruction of a
single dependency-ordered scientific system. It begins with the operational
root theorem *there is no nothing*: a purported counterexample either presents
no statement, denial, record or proof object and therefore supplies no
counterexample, or it presents an occurrence and is not nothing. The admitted
occurrence forces its structural self-whole, the One; exact positive count,
part, distinction, return and the minimal reversible Fold are then generated
without importing arithmetic, geometry, probability, physical constants or a
continuum as premises. Every later result is separately registered. No later
law is permitted to become a retroactive assumption of an earlier proof.

The V3 corpus reported here contains **2,751 model-admitted claims** generated
from **892,246 registered candidates**, with **2,751 unique survivors** and
**11,004 passed controls**. All 2,751 receipt identities match their registered
claim identities; all 2,751 candidate censuses reconstruct; every admitted
claim traces to the single root; and the current ledger contains no missing
declared dependency. These totals cover the premise-free methods and
Foundation results; current full-field Mathematics, Information Science,
Classical Computation, Reversible and Quantum Computation, Physics, Chemistry
and Materials Science; completed foundations and dated return families in
Biology, Medicine, Consciousness and Cognitive Science, Earth and
Environmental Sciences, Astronomy and Cosmology, Social and Collective
Systems, and Engineering Translation; and the current twelve-claim
Cross-Branch Synthesis.

The work separates exact derivation from implementation, measurement,
reconstruction, correspondence, compatibility, support, confirmation and
adverse evidence. Candidate generation and elimination establish formal
status only at their declared boundaries. Natural-science claims additionally
retain source identity, measurement method, uncertainty, preregistration,
chronology, target custody, falsification conditions and every favourable,
adverse, null, failed, missing or unavailable result. Current branch status is
therefore non-uniform by design: seven field-wide branches are complete to
their frozen current-evidence scopes; Biology is at 82 of 424 registered
full-field obligations; later natural, cognitive, collective and engineering
branches possess complete foundations but do not yet claim frozen full-field
closure.

The preliminary monograph also reports the computational-proof programme.
Protein Fold V3 has an exact sequence representation, complete interaction

search and a separately closed 583-state high-attraction slice, while the

This publication is deliberately preliminary because the computational

## 2. The scientific constitution

### 2.1 The root restriction

The V3 derivation begins without an imported scientific axiom. It does not
begin by assuming space, time, number, probability, matter, energy, logic as a
formal calculus or a computational machine. Its initial question is
operational: what would count as a presented counterexample to the statement
that there is no nothing? The registered two-class census is exhaustive at
that boundary:

1. no statement, denial, record or proof object is presented, so no
   counterexample is supplied; or
2. something is presented, and the presentation is an occurrence rather than
   nothing.

The result is not a conventional numerical assertion that a quantity differs
from zero. Numerical zero has not yet been admitted. It is an operational
closure statement about the impossibility of presenting nothing as a
counterexample.

### 2.2 Clean-room sequencing

V3 is a clean-room reconstruction. Earlier SFT papers, application programs
and observations may be compared only after the relevant V3 result has sealed.
They remain historical evidence and can establish agreement, disagreement,
stronger closure, weaker closure, an implementation result or a question that
the clean reconstruction must eventually address. They cannot become hidden
premises or answer keys.

The sequencing rule is global:

1. register the obligation and admissible dependencies;
2. freeze the candidate grammar and boundary;
3. execute the complete census or a certified depth-independent closure;
4. retain one decision for every candidate;
5. identify the unique survivor or preserve the adverse no-survivor or
   multiple-survivor result;
6. run named controls and an implementation-distinct reconstruction;
7. seal the result and receipt;
8. only then open historical comparison or external target evidence when the
   protocol permits it.

### 2.3 No-free-parameter rule

A fundamental SFT law cannot be selected by a fitted, trained, discretionary,
target-derived or silently imported numerical parameter. Exact positive
rational values may appear after their part and ratio construction has been
derived. Measured values, uncertainties, calibration factors and conventional
units remain legitimate empirical records, but they do not select the prior
formal law.

This rule does not pretend that all software has no configuration or that all
experiments lack apparatus choices. It distinguishes an implementation or
measurement condition from a free parameter that chooses the purported
fundamental result. Conditions and interfaces must be declared, source-bound
and retained in the evidence record.

### 2.4 Candidate closure and uniqueness

Every admitted claim states a grammar of admissible forms. The grammar may be
finite and exhaustively enumerated or it may possess a separately proved
depth-independent certificate. The package records:

- the exact claim and why the next object is required;
- admitted dependencies and branch owner;
- carrier, boundary and relation;
- provenance and evidence class;
- candidate grammar and cardinality;
- one decision for every candidate;
- survivor count and uniqueness explanation;
- falsification condition;
- adverse controls and their results;
- source identities, source transport and chronology; and
- receipt and artefact identities.

One survivor establishes uniqueness only within the registered grammar and
boundary. A bounded census without a generality certificate remains bounded.
A foundational closure does not silently become completion of every possible
future scientific application.

### 2.5 Host/computation quarantine

The implementation necessarily uses host-language integers, indices, empty
containers, Boolean values, file paths, process codes and finite machine
resources. These implement a proof or audit; they do not become SFT proof
objects merely by appearing in code. Floating-point computation may be used
for a declared measured comparison or rendering task. Exact formal decisions
use the exact representation declared by their package.

### 4.2 Proof and evidence verbs

This paper reserves *proves* for exact formal closure, *derives* for generated
structures and laws, *implements* for executable constructions, *predicts* for
properly sealed prospective consequences, *observes* for source-bound records,
*measures* for method-bound values, *reconstructs* for inferred histories or
latent states, *corresponds* for post-derivation relations, *is compatible
with* for non-adverse but non-discriminating evidence, *supports* for relevant
but non-unique evidence, *confirms* only where the registered protocol permits
that class, *adverse* where the registered outcome conflicts, and *unresolved*
where the required evidence remains missing or incomplete.

Test success verifies what the tests cover. It does not by itself empirically
confirm the theory. A source's reputation does not prove the claim. A later
successful result does not become an earlier blind prediction unless the
sealed chronology establishes that fact.

### 4.3 Empirical sequence

For a natural-science prediction the lawful sequence is:

1. establish capability closure without target access;
2. register the exact claim, target class, selection rule, metrics, failure
   treatment and falsification criterion;
3. place target custody outside the predicting process;
4. seal the executable predictor, environment and output identity;
5. open the target or comparator output only after the seal;
6. evaluate every row, including failed, invalid and unavailable rows;
7. publish the complete chronology and outcome without selective deletion.

Development-observed, post-target and retrospective results remain useful but
retain those labels. A failed source transport is not absence of evidence; it
is a transport result. Missing evidence is not a null result. A null result is
a completed observation whose registered outcome is null.

## 7. Reading this monograph

The chapters that follow move through the dependency spine rather than through
a conventional division between “fundamental” and “applied” disciplines. Each
major branch begins by naming the upstream objects already admitted, the next
unresolved problem and the new evidential boundary. Each ends by naming the
objects handed downstream and the limits that remain with its owner.

complete claim inventory gives every admitted claim ID, title, branch, status,
dependencies, candidate count, survivor count, controls and receipt route.
The branch audit volumes retain the complete claim-level fields required by the
publication guidance. Readers should therefore use the conceptual chapter to
understand the argument, the audit volume to inspect a result's custody and the
machine package to reproduce its acceptance or rejection.

This hierarchy is not a concession to opacity. It is what permits both human
comprehension and evidential completeness. No claim is removed from the audit
or machine archive merely because its full receipt is too repetitive for the
main argument.
