The final TOE paper is assembled only after every registered branch has a
completed comprehensive branch paper and the global census has no unclassified
or unclosed frontier obligation within the frozen current-knowledge inventory.

The final paper must include every branch's complete dependency path, explain
cross-branch composition without importing circular dependencies, preserve all
empirical boundaries and provide a machine-readable global evidence map.

The final publication gate verifies:

- all required branch-paper receipts;
- the frozen global inventory;
- every source, paper and evidence-map hash;
- comprehensive derivation coverage;
- cross-branch consistency controls; and
- exact statement of any boundary that remains outside the frozen inventory.

No summary paper can replace or silently omit the branch papers. The final paper
is the synthesis of their complete admitted derivations.
