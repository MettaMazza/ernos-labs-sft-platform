# From Fold to Life

**Biology and Life Sciences Foundational Branch Paper 001 — version 1.0.0**

This open-access release presents the current-evidence-closed, extension-open
foundational Biology and Life Sciences branch of the Smithian Fold Theory V3
clean-room reconstruction.

## Headline results

- 75 laws admitted through the untouched sealed engine from 19,200 explicitly
  enumerated candidates, with exactly 75 unique survivors.
- 300 passing adverse controls, 75 implementation-distinct reconstructions and
  75 post-seal external comparisons.
- Four nucleotide coding labels generated from two held distinctions; three
  ordered positions generate 64 codon words and 16 first-two-position boxes of
  four words each.
- Exact foundational laws for living organization, compartment, metabolism,
  inheritance, evolution, genes, genomes, proteins, cells, development,
  organisms, populations, ecology and biological evidence.
- All 763 V1/V2 entries audited; all 30 Biology-owned atoms closed, including
  21 explicitly corrected evidence boundaries.

The correction ledger rejects inevitable fixation, a universal rigid one-state
protein, a parity-selected origin of biological homochirality, universal
three-quarter allometry, ubiquitous scale-free biological networks and
recurrence-only diagnoses of senescence, cancer or ecosystem stability.

The release contains the complete paper in PDF and Markdown, the complete
evidence/source archive and a checksum ledger.
