## 1. The question answered

OpenAI's public claim and this paper's target must not be conflated. OpenAI presents ten results and a Lean certificate for each. The present paper asks:

> For each exact main conclusion, does the conclusion survive a strict, structure-preserving translation into the existing SFT model?

The answer is no in all twelve atomic cases.

Let `C_i` denote the exact conclusion of atomic result `i`. Let `Holds_SFT(C_i)` mean that the strict translation of `C_i` is a lawful, inhabited conclusion of SFT. Let `Req(C_i)` be the set of carrier types, operations, quantifier ranges, comparison objects and resource measures logically required by the exact statement. Let `Adm_SFT(r)` mean that required component `r` is admitted by SFT.

Strict conclusionhood gives the following necessary rule:

```text
Holds_SFT(C_i)  ->  for every r in Req(C_i), Adm_SFT(r).
```

For every result in the source set, source inspection identifies at least one required component `r_i` and existing model-admitted SFT evidence gives:

```text

```

Therefore:

```text
Assume Holds_SFT(C_i).
Then Adm_SFT(r_i).                  [strict conclusionhood]
But not Adm_SFT(r_i).              [existing SFT result]
Contradiction.
Therefore not Holds_SFT(C_i).      [SFT disproof]
```

This rule does not use a missing SFT receipt for an OpenAI artifact as evidence of falsity. It does not use OpenAI's imported Lean axioms as a conclusion-level premise.
