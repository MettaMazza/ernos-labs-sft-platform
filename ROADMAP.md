# Dependency-ordered reconstruction roadmap

## Two-layer branch programme

Every scientific branch now has a dedicated roadmap under
[`docs/branch_roadmaps/`](docs/branch_roadmaps/README.md).  Each roadmap separates:

1. a **foundational reconstruction**, which establishes the branch's primitive
   objects, laws, evidence classes and handoffs and ends in a standalone
   foundational paper only after its protected gates pass; and
2. a **complete field reconstruction**, which expands through a frozen
   current-knowledge census at Physics-level derivational and empirical depth
   and produces sequentially versioned updates to the same branch paper.

No roadmap checkbox, prose statement or paper version admits a claim.  Current
status is determined only by the live machine census, immutable engine receipts,
categorical-owner reconciliation and publication gate.  A branch called
current-knowledge complete remains open to lawful extension, correction,
falsification and later discovery.

The complete branch set is Foundation, Mathematics, Information Science,
Classical Computational Science, Reversible and Quantum Computation, Physics,
Chemistry, Materials Science, Biology and Life Sciences, Medicine and Health
Sciences, Consciousness and Cognitive Science, Earth and Environmental
Sciences, Astronomy and Cosmology, Social and Collective Systems, and
Engineering Translation.  Medicine is a declared branch whose scientific
directory and census must be scaffolded before its derivation begins.
