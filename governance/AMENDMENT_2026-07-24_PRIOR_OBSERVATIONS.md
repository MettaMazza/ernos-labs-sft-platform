# Constitutional clarification: prior SFT work as observation

Approved by Maria Smith on 2026-07-24 in the instruction that a clean rebuild
must not be treated as blind to the still-valid V1/V2 work and that those works
are observational data.

## Defect corrected

The earlier clean-room wording correctly prohibited importing V1/V2 code,
certificates and answer tables into V3 derivations, but incorrectly delayed the
earlier stated result itself until post-seal comparison. That allowed a narrow
branch inventory to omit a known reconstruction obligation. The inverse
fine-structure result consequently entered the live V3 Physics corpus only when
Chemistry exposed it as an upstream need, after the Physics paper was frozen.

## Clarified rule

Every earlier SFT claim, value, scope and falsifier is a mandatory prior
observation and is registered before reconstruction. It may define the question,
branch census and comparison target. It may not enter the V3 executable proof
dependencies, candidate grammar, eliminator or survivor selector. The sealed V3
result is compared afterward.

## Evidence gates preserved

This clarification does not admit a prior result into V3, weaken enumeration,
permit fitting, or change an existing receipt. It strengthens completion gates
by making omission of a prior observation a publication failure.

## Required controls

- reject a prior answer artifact added to a V3 source manifest;
- reject a legacy result with no branch/disposition mapping;
- reject a branch-complete declaration with an unresolved assigned result;
- reject a downstream publication that is the first categorical registration
  of an upstream result; and
- distinguish a forced value from a relation evaluated using measured inputs.
