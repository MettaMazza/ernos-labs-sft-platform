# SFT absence-glyph policy

Authority: Maria Smith  
Recorded: 2026-07-26

## Exact rule

There is no zero in Smithian Fold Theory.

The glyph `0` may be displayed only as a familiar representation of **absence**.
It is not an SFT number, magnitude, count, coordinate, state, denominator,
occupation, energy, gap or proof value.

The native proof carrier for absence is structural `EmptyOne` / `EMPTY_ONE`.
Every present quantitative carrier is generated positively and represented by
an exact positive count, part or ratio of positive counts.

## External and interface records

An external source, conventional dataset, calculator input or user interface
may contain the glyph `0`. The glyph must be retained verbatim for provenance
while being normalized semantically to **absence** at the SFT boundary. It must
never enter candidate generation, forcing, exact arithmetic or survivor
selection as a numerical scalar.

Host-language serialization may mechanically render an absent external
displacement as `0` or `0/1`. Such text is not an admitted SFT value. Evidence
and publications must identify it as an external or host representation of
absence and must use structural `EmptyOne` in the derivation itself.

## Halt condition

Any derivation that treats `0` as a numerical SFT value rather than the glyph
for absence is invalid and must halt. Replacing the glyph with a signed,
negative, irrational, imaginary, floating or continuum value is also invalid.

This rule applies to all subsequent branches, calculations, empirical
comparisons, papers and application translations.
