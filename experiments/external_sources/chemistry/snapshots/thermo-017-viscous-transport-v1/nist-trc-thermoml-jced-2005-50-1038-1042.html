Densities, Viscosities and Derived Functions of Binary Mixtures: (Triethylene Glycol Dimethyl Ether + Water) and (N-acetylmorpholine+ Water) from 298.15 to 343.15 K

Henni, A.[Amr], Naami, A.[Abdelaziz], Tontiwachwuthlkul, P.[Paitoon]
J. Chem. Eng. Data 2005, 50, 3, 1038-1042
  • JSON
  • ThermoML
ABSTRACT
This paper reports the density and viscosity of aqueous Triethylene Glycol Dimethyl Ether solutions at five temperatures in the range 303.15 K to 343.15 K, and aqueous N-acetylmorpholine solutions at six temperatures in the range 298.15 K to 343.15 K over the whole concentration range. The results are compared with data published in the literature when available. The excess molar volumes, the partial molar volumes at infinite dilution, the Grunberg-Nissan parameters, and viscosity deviations were derived.
