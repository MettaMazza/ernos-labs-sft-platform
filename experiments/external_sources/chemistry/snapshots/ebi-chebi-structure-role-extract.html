The term chemical entity in ChEBI refers to any constitutionally or isotopically distinct atom, molecule, ion, ion pair, radical, radical ion, complex, conformer, etc. ChEBI incorporates an ontological classification, whereby relationships between chemical entities and classes are defined, enabling queries based on chemical class and role.

ChEBI entities are annotated in distinct sub-ontologies: molecular structure, in which molecular entities or parts of entities are classified according to their structure; and role, in which entities are classified on the basis of their role within a biological context.

A biological role is a role played by the molecular entity or part thereof within a biological context. Biological function is registered as a synonym of biological role.
