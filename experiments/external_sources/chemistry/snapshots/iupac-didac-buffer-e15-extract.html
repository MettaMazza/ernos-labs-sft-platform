E15 Acid-base equilibria : buffers

A buffer solution contains a weak acid together with a salt of this weak acid or a weak base together with a salt of this weak base.

The name buffer is due to its ability to maintain the pH of a solution fairly constant despite the addition of considerable quantities of a strong acid or a strong base to the solution.

Upon the addition of hydrogen ions, hydrogen carbonate ions are converted (protonated) into carbonic acid.

However, their buffering capacity is not inexhaustible !
