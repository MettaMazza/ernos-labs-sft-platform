# Level One review record 1.5.0

Date: 30 July 2026
Author: Maria Smith
Status: review; final-publication approval pending

This version gives every Level One line the same plain-language and
cause-and-effect treatment as Book One. It bundles 35 matching local Kokoro
lines and preserves the exact scene, dialogue turn and unfinished mini-game
when a mobile browser hides, reloads or discards the page.

At 390 pixels wide, the revised parcel sequence returned to Sol's exact line
after reload. After the teddy was moved, a second reload kept the activity
unfinished and left the box ready to inspect without showing the answer early.
The production build and all nine application/content tests passed.
