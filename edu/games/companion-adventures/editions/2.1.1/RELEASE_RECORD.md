## Why review 2.1.0 was rejected

Review 2.1.0 is retained unchanged in `editions/2.1.0/` as a historical review
record. It is not an accepted release. Its Level Four story used unexplained
framework language and did not make every physical step of the Welcome Sign
journey clear enough for a young child.

Its final height game also contradicted its own picture. The visible tool
reached the top edge only at height 4, while three replay variants secretly
accepted height 2 or 3. In the replay that accepted height 4, testing heights
1, 2 and 3 could darken all three try lights before the child could test the
visible answer. Width practice had the same three-attempt exhaustion risk.
Review 2.1.0 therefore must not be presented as completed, validated or ready
for approval.

## Corrections in review 2.1.1

- Renamed the companion *The Garden Gate Check* and rewrote its nine stages as
  one physical story: the light reaches the Sunrise Arch; morning visitors wait
  beyond a closed gate; the friends finish, repair, measure and check its
  Welcome Sign; four answer cards then open the gate.
- Replaced child-facing source, retained-failure, trace, fresh-path and record
  language with picture plan, wrong sign, move cards, checking without peeking
  and answer cards.
- Introduced observation, comparing, record, width, independent check, height
  and evidence only after the child performs the visible action, with each word
  explained in the spoken and written lesson.
- Made Stage 5 visibly return the bee and boot to their correct corners before
  the repaired sign reaches the measuring hooks.
- Fixed every replay so the one visibly full bottom-to-top tool, height 4, is
  the accepted height answer.
- Made width calibration, height calibration, Guess and Vote non-consuming
  learning attempts. Both measuring games now say that the child may try as
  many lengths as needed.
- Updated the E04 narration/caption set to review 1.0.1 and kept its 32 local
  Kokoro files under `public/audio/e04-v1.0.1/` for offline playback.
- Added regression checks for all height variants, visible measurement bounds,
  unlimited measuring practice, exact caption/manifest agreement and the full
  physical story sequence.

## Validation and approval boundary

The focused Level Four lifecycle suite passes 16 automated checks, including
the measurement and story regressions above. Corrected review 2.1.1 was also
played from introduction to ending through the visible 390 × 844 phone
interface. The tester deliberately tried short width and height tools, Guess,
Vote and one wrong answer-card match before completing all nine games. Height
4 visibly reached both edges and completed the formerly blocked game. The
selector and completed ending were also visually inspected at 768 × 1024 and
1440 × 900, and an in-browser tab return preserved the exact ending. A true
operating-system app switch is not claimed.

These are implementation and manual QA checks, not a claim that a child or
adult reviewer has accepted the result. No manual result from rejected review
2.1.0 is carried forward. This record does not approve a final publication,
authorise public hosting or move any book into `publications/education/`.
