## Decision

Not publication-ready. The whole-and-parts idea is useful but the pictures contradict the story. The key fit problem is not demonstrated; lantern pieces turn into picture cards without explanation; the doorway paths do not pass through the doorway; the whole is rebuilt prematurely and then unexplainedly disassembled again. Addition is included but needs visible grouping and a more natural explanation.

## Cross-page findings

- One stable OpenMoji lantern is used, a useful foundation, but mounted cropped cards are silently treated as pieces of the physical object. Decide which object the story handles and depict it consistently.
- The workshop art already shows the moon high outside while page 5 requests delivery before moonrise. Remove the arbitrary deadline or make time progression consistent.
- Many overlays cover characters' faces and hands rather than occupy the surface described.
- Some activity wording is hard-coded in renderer functions rather than read from canonical page text; current PDF and accessible text differ. Keep one content source while allowing deliberate medium-specific layout.
- Frame size and source-card scale must support the precise fitting comparison rather than require the child to imagine resizing.
- Do not claim equal pictured parts establish unrestricted general mathematical/physical claims. Keep the actual source boundary in adult materials without overwhelming the read-aloud story.

## Adult guide: all five pages inspected

Correct the author and copyright lines. Page 1's metadata runs together. Page 2 repeats the inaccurate claim that all four tracing paths run through the doorway. Pages 2–3 describe picture cards and physical lantern parts interchangeably; repair this with the book. Pages 3–4 split the exclusions list across a page boundary; reorganise the technical appendix so it does not dominate practical sharing guidance. Page 5 has useful accessibility options but lacks practical companion-game instructions and code guidance.

Support children who do not recognise numerals yet. Retain the participation options, optional large-card activity, and the explicit warning not to shorten four parts/one lantern to “four equals one.” Ensure answer explanations match the revised artwork.

## Revision direction

One uninterrupted adventure: discover lantern; visibly try doorway; safely separate pieces; count and carry each; bring two groups together; rebuild once; celebrate its light. Each activity should advance that journey, not reset it. Give comparison cards explicit picture status if used. Introduce plus and equals after showing the groups and their combined count. Do not overwrite the historical edition.
