## Scope completed

## Decision

Not publication-ready. The most serious teaching error is that spaced-out, inconsistently sized squares are presented as measurements of a sign they do not span. Several tasks ask children to use a visible plan that is absent. The story adds procedural checks after the sign has already been successfully built without establishing why the child should care. Tiny sprites and floating panels repeat the game-screen design the user rejected.

## Adult guide: all four pages

Page 1: wrong credit and run-together metadata; claims plan stays visible when it does not. Page 2: numerical answers count the drawn squares but do not establish the claimed widths/heights; do not present these as validated measurements. Page 3: useful explicit statement that story checks are not formal proof, but its separate-reconstruction analogy must match what Ivo actually saw. Page 4: colour-independent access claim is contradicted by the width/height record cards. Optional measurement instructions must say equal units, edge to edge, no gaps/overlap, same unit when comparing; hand spans vary unless held consistent. Correct copyright and add actual book/game sharing guidance.

## Revision priorities

1. Rebuild the measurement diagrams: one constant unit, contiguous tiles against the actual sign edge, clearly marked endpoints, identical scale for compared spans, preserved sign content, and real mounting-space fit.
2. Keep the plan visible whenever a task asks for observation/comparison. Cover it only for an explicitly introduced memory activity.
3. Give the garden job a human purpose before the checks; establish visitors and sign placement. Let each action advance it.
4. Preserve matching, uncertainty, retry comparison and checking one property at a time without importing adult verification jargon into child dialogue.
5. Show real character actions and a clear before/after payoff; replace repeated menu panels and correct all credits/case issues.
