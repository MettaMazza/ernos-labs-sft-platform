# E04 narrative and learning design

Working version: 1.0.0

Status: canonical design for review production

## Identity

- Book: **E04 - Look Again: How We Check**
- Story subtitle: **The Garden Welcome Sign**
- Stage: Early Years / Foundation
- Permanent leads: Mia, Sol and Tavi
- Sole new guest: Ivo, the garden checker
- Mission: rebuild and verify a four-picture Garden Welcome Sign so the garden
  gate can open before the morning visitors arrive
- Prior knowledge: E01's careful presence language, E02's complete whole and
  retained parts, and E03's declared rule, visible failed attempt and repair

Ivo is moss-green, carries a round magnifying lens and wears a checkerboard
satchel. His design is recognisable by shape and pattern without relying on
colour. He may return later only when a trace, measurement boundary, rejection
or independent check is relevant.

## Why E04 is next

The maintained master syllabus fixes E04 as the fourth Early Years book and
gives it the source sequence **trace, rejection, measurement boundary and
independent check**. E03 ends with a preserved failed route and Tavi asking how
the friends can check their work. E04 begins at that garden gate, so the new
question is caused by the preceding story rather than appearing as an unrelated
lesson.

The scientific content is bounded to:

- `SFT-FOUNDATION-DERIVATION-TRACE-001`: a complete finite derivation retains an
  ordered, replayable source- and dependency-bound record;
- `SFT-FOUNDATION-MEASURED-VALUE-BOUNDARY-001`: measurement is opened after the
  exact law and question are fixed and cannot select or mutate the law;
- `SFT-FOUNDATION-ADMISSION-ENFORCEMENT-001`: failed, rejected and unresolved
  dispositions are preserved and incomplete routes halt rather than gain
  authority; and
- `SFT-ENG-INDEPENDENT-CHECK-001`: a bounded implementation-distinct check
  reconstructs by a separately identified path.

The sign, ribbons, curtain and check boards are educational translations. They
do not independently prove these claims.

## One caused story journey

The same sign and gate remain the purpose of all 32 pages:

1. the repaired E03 trail brings the trio to the closed garden gate;
2. the gate shows one four-picture source plan and one blank welcome sign;
3. Ivo arrives as the sole new guest and states why the sign must be checked;
4. the child observes the source before choosing;
5. the team builds one complete sign while the source remains visible;
6. the source and first build are covered and Sol makes a memory rebuild;
7. the uncovered source shows that bee and boot traded places;
8. the first failed rebuild stays visible while the child finds both
   differences and the first changed step in its ordered trace;
9. the repaired sign is measured for one declared width question only;
10. Ivo independently rebuilds from the source without seeing the team answer;
11. the two builds confirm the picture positions;
12. a disagreement exposes that height has not yet been checked, so the team
    does more work with a height tool; and
13. a final checkpoint links each question to its own visible record before the
    gate opens.

No success is inferred from a pleasing picture. No failure disappears. No
measurement answers a question it was not chosen to answer.

## Experience-before-vocabulary ladder

| Experience first | Name after the reveal | Child-facing meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Look at the shown source and name the bee | observation | saying what we can see now |
| Choose without checking the shown source | guess | an answer without that check |
| Keep the source, build and rebuild | source / rebuild | the shown starting plan and a fresh build made from it |
| Leave Sol's mismatching sign on its stand | failed attempt | a try that did not match and can still help us |
| Follow four kept step cards in order | trace | a record of what happened first, next and last |
| Compare two boards corner by corner | compare | look for what is the same and different |
| Width ribbon answers only width | measurement question | the one named thing this tool is checking |
| Ivo rebuilds without seeing the team sign | independent check | a friend follows a fresh path instead of copying |
| Two checks agree | confirm | the check matches the first result |
| Checkers differ or a question is missing | disagree / more work | do not vote; find the unanswered question and check it |

Formal terms such as immutable identity, deterministic replay, disposition
receipt, target opening, implementation carrier and fail-closed admission stay
in the adult guide.

## Book and Level Four pairing

| Stage | Book pages | Level Four playable action | Optional code |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1. Look-and-Point | 6-8 | move a round lens over the plan and tag the requested observed picture | `LOOKCLOSE` |
| 2. Build-the-Board | 9-11 | drag or tap all four picture cards into symbol-marked sign spaces | `SIGNMAKER` |
| 3. Curtain Memory | 12-14 | study, cover and rebuild a varied four-picture arrangement | `KEEPFIRST` |
| 4. Difference Finder | 15-16 | inspect two full boards and mark every changed place | `SPOTCHANGE` |
| 5. Footprint Trace | 17-19 | walk a character through ordered placement footprints and identify the first changed step | `FOLLOWSTEPS` |
| 6. Measuring Ribbon | 20-22 | align the width ribbon with two hooks and answer only the declared width question | `WIDTHONLY` |
| 7. Friend Check | 23-25 | command Ivo's separate build path, then compare all four results | `FRIENDCHECK` |
| 8. Disagreement Detective | 26-27 | choose and operate the missing height check rather than guess or vote | `MOREWORK` |
| 9. Final Checkpoint | 28-30 | route each evidence card to its matching gate question; incomplete evidence cannot open the gate | `ALLCHECKED` |

Every mini-game has a wrong outcome, visible feedback, retry and at least one
safe variation. A lost round retains the attempt long enough to compare.
Keyboard and tap alternatives accompany drag, movement or timed input.

## Setup-and-answer contract

The challenge and reveal pairs are `7/8`, `10/11`, `13/14`, `15/16`, `18/19`,
`21/22`, `24/25`, `26/27` and `29/30`. A challenge may show every source and
choice required to reason, but may not label its answer. The following page
must show the same scene with the answer labelled above the recognisable image.

Optional codes appear only after a reveal. They unlock cosmetic animation,
character moments or replay variations. They never expose the next answer,
skip an attempt, add progress or gate a lesson.

## Ivo continuity record

Ivo's checking method is:

1. name one clear question;
2. keep the source and every attempt;
3. follow the recorded steps in order;
4. use a tool only for its named question;
5. make a fresh check without copying the first answer; and
6. report confirm, disagree or more work without changing the record.

Ivo never announces the correct card, changed step, measurement result or
final checkpoint before the child attempts it. A future appearance may recall
this method but cannot use it to give away a new answer.

## Exact exclusions

E04 does not introduce or rely on:

- a classroom object, generated picture or game result as proof of an SFT law;
- a measured value selecting, changing or repairing a derivation;
- a tool answering an undeclared property;
- guessing, voting, confidence or popularity as validation;
- hidden deletion of a failed or rejected attempt;
- one checker copying the first checker and being called independent;
- agreement being guaranteed, or disagreement being treated as failure;
- a successful physical fit being called scientific authority;
- semantic numerical zero, signed proof quantities, floating proof equality,
  completed infinity or an ungenerated continuum; or
- an outside curriculum, publisher, game or standard as SFT authority.

## Review gates

- Every page must retain the one sign-and-gate mission and a visible cause.
- The source plan must always use the same four picture-to-symbol mappings.
- Every child-facing challenge must precede its labelled reveal.
- The first failed rebuild and its trace must remain visible through the final
  checkpoint or be explicitly shown in the checking book.
- Width and height must be separate declared questions with separate tools.
- Ivo's check must be demonstrably separate from the team's answer.
- Disagreement must lead to a named missing check, not to a vote.
- All words and reveal labels must sit above recognisable pictures.
- Alt descriptions must identify the source, attempt, tool and result.
- The game narrator's caption and local narration must match exactly.
- No artifact may enter `publications/education/` before explicit approval.
