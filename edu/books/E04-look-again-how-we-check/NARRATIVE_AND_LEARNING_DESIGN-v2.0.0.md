# E04 narrative and learning design — review 2.0.0

## Child-facing promise

One adventure, one visible problem: help Mia, Sol and Tavi rebuild and check the Garden Welcome Sign so the closed gate can open. Ivo is the only new guest. The story starts at the gate reached in E03 and never jumps to an unexplained place or object.

## Narrative cause chain

1. The repaired E03 light trail reaches the closed garden gate.
2. A blank sign and a four-picture plan make the new job visible.
3. Ivo arrives with a lens and asks to help the permanent trio look again.
4. The child observes one picture, then chooses a complete sign using the visible plan.
5. The plan is covered and memory is checked by uncovering the same plan.
6. Sol’s changed first try stays visible beside the plan.
7. Four retained step cards expose the first changed move.
8. The child repairs the sign from the same plan.
9. Equal emoji tiles answer width and height as two different questions.
10. Ivo makes a fresh sign without seeing the team sign.
11. Four clear question records are present before the gate opens.
12. The narrator speaks directly to the child, names the learned actions and explains why checking matters.

## Experience before words

- Look at the plan and answer before the reveal names an **observation**.
- Keep two signs side by side before the adult guide uses **compare**.
- Follow cards numbered 1 to 4 before the adult guide uses **trace**.
- Count equal tiles across before naming **width** as side to side.
- Start again and count equal tiles upwards before naming **height** as bottom to top.
- Watch Ivo build without seeing the team sign before the adult guide names an **implementation-distinct check**.

The child book does not use formal receipt, derivation, disposition, admission or implementation language.

## Stable emoji vocabulary

Every repeated teaching object uses one OpenMoji 16.0 identity:

- corners: sun `2600`, moon `1F319`, leaf `1F343`, star `2B50`;
- sign pictures: sunflower `1F33B`, bee `1F41D`, apple `1F34E`, boot `1F97E`;
- measuring tiles: yellow square `1F7E8`, blue square `1F7E6`;
- answer marks: check `2705` and cross `274C`; and
- the one repeated lens symbol: magnifying glass `1F50D`.

Generated images provide the garden, work areas and characters only. They never supply a countable, matchable or answer-bearing teaching object.

## Paper-native activity sequence

| Page | Activity | Child action | Reveal |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 7 | Find the moon corner | inspect the full plan and point to one emoji | 8 |
| 10 | Which sign matches? | compare three complete signs with the visible plan | 11 |
| 13 | What was beside the star? | recall or say “I’m not sure” while the plan is covered | 14 |
| 15 | Spot the two changes | compare the retained first try with the plan | 16 |
| 17 | Where did the change begin? | follow four steps while the plan remains on the page | 18 |
| 19 | Repair the moon corner | select the emoji card required by the plan | 20 |
| 21 | How wide? | count four equal emoji tiles across | 22 |
| 23 | How tall? | count three equal emoji tiles upwards | 24 |
| 26 | Same or different? | compare the team and Ivo signs | 27 |
| 28 | Choose the picture check | match one question to its visual record | 29 |
| 30 | Is every check ready? | inspect four checked records and decide readiness | 31 |

No activity needs cutting, dragging, scrolling, loose pieces, a hidden code or a companion device. Every challenge page contains enough visible information to attempt the answer. Every answer appears later.

## Exact educational boundaries

- Sol’s first try and its step cards are retained; the change is never silently repaired.
- Width and height use separate equal emoji tile rows and answer only their named direction.
- Ivo uses the same plan with a fresh sign while the team sign is hidden.
- Matching classroom signs are an educational experience, not formal certification.
- The closed gate is a story consequence, not SFT authority.
- External curriculum informs reading level and accessibility only.
- No generated image, game result or physical fit enters SFT derivation.
