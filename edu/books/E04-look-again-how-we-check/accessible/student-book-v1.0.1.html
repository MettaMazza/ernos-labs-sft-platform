A small moss-green friend hurried from the garden. He carried a round magnifying lens and a checkerboard satchel.

"Hello! I'm Ivo. I help check things carefully. Let's look again together."

Ivo joins Mia, Sol and Tavi to solve the welcome-sign problem.

1 - LOOK BEFORE YOU GUESS

Ivo put the picture plan where everyone could see it. Each space had a symbol in one corner and a picture inside.

"Let's look closely before we answer," said Mia.

First find the moon symbol. Then look at the picture beside it.

THE BEE IS IN THE MOON CORNER

The BEE is in the space with the MOON.

You looked before you answered. Saying what you saw is called an OBSERVATION. Choosing without looking would be a GUESS.

Now the team knows what belongs in the moon space. Find the optional picture code.

Ivo opened his satchel. Inside were four picture cards and one blank wooden sign.

TRY IT - THE MEMORY REBUILD

Sol placed the pictures one at a time. Ivo kept one card showing each move. Sol's finished try was:

SUNFLOWER at SUN. BOOT at MOON. WATERING CAN at LEAF. BEE at STAR.

Does it MATCH, DOES NOT MATCH, or are you NOT SURE?

All three are honest choices while the plan is hidden. Turn the page to check.

Choices:
  • Match
  • Does Not Match
  • Not Sure

BEE AND BOOT CHANGED PLACES

BEE and BOOT are in different places. SUNFLOWER and WATERING CAN are in the same places.

The friends looked at every space, not just one.

COMPARE means looking at two things side by side to find what is the same and what is different. Find the optional picture code.

While Sol rebuilt the sign, Ivo kept one card for each picture Sol placed. Now he hung the four cards from first to last.

These four step cards show what Sol did first, next and last.

6 - WILL IT FIT SIDE TO SIDE?

After BEE and BOOT were fixed, the sign matched the plan. Now it had to fit between two hooks.

Ivo placed a ribbon from the left side to the right side. "This distance is called WIDTH. Let's see if the sign fits side to side."

This ribbon checks width. It does not check the pictures or how tall the sign is.

Visible labels above the pictures: WIDTH RIBBON; LEFT EDGE; RIGHT EDGE; GATE HOOKS

IVO'S CHECK CONFIRMS THE PICTURES

All four pictures are in the same places. Ivo made his own sign without copying, and it matched.

When a new check gives the same result, we say it CONFIRMS the answer.

A check made without copying is called an independent check. Sol's earlier try still stays with the team. Find the optional picture code.

Visible labels above the pictures: TEAM SIGN; IVO'S CHECK; FOUR MATCHES; CONFIRMED

Optional picture code: FRIENDCHECK. This code is not needed to learn or continue.

8 - WHAT HAVE WE NOT CHECKED?

"The width fits, so we're done!" said Mia.

Ivo pointed to the bottom and top. "Wait. We checked side to side, but not bottom to top."

What should the team do now: guess, ask everyone to vote, or measure bottom to top?

A vote tells which choice people prefer. It does not measure the sign.

Choices:
  • Guess
  • Vote
  • Measure Bottom to Top

THEY CHECKED BOTTOM TO TOP

The team used a second ribbon from the bottom to the top. That distance is called HEIGHT.

The sign reached the two green height marks. Its height fits too.

Ivo's words alone were not the answer. Measuring gave the team the answer. Sometimes a difference of opinion shows what to check next. Find the optional picture code.

Optional picture code: MOREWORK. This code is not needed to learn or continue.

Before opening the gate, the friends stopped for four last questions:

YOU LEARNED HOW TO CHECK

You looked at a plan, built a sign and kept Sol's first try. The step cards showed where the mix-up began. One ribbon checked width. Another checked height. Ivo made his own sign without copying.

A check can show that things match, show a difference, or show what to check next.

Checking helps us explain what we saw, find a change and know what to do next.
