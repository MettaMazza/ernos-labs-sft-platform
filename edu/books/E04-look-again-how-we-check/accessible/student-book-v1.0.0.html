MEET IVO

A small moss-green friend hurried out from the garden. He carried a round magnifying lens and a checkerboard satchel.

"Hello! I am Ivo, the garden checker. I can help you look again, but I will not choose your answers for you."

Ivo is the one new friend on this adventure. The same four friends will solve one sign problem together.

Visible labels above the pictures: IVO; MAGNIFYING LENS; CHECKERBOARD SATCHEL

1 - LOOK BEFORE YOU GUESS

Ivo stood the picture plan where everyone could see it. Each space had a corner mark and one clear picture.

"First, let us say what we can see," said Mia.

When you look at something that is shown and tell what you see, you make an observation. A guess is an answer chosen without checking what is shown.

Visible labels above the pictures: SUN CORNER; MOON CORNER; LEAF CORNER; STAR CORNER

5 - FOLLOW THE STEP TRACE

Ivo had clipped one step card to a string after every picture Sol placed. The cards kept the order of his work.

"This step trace can show us what happened first, next and last," said Ivo.

A TRACE is a kept record of the steps in the order they happened.

6 - ASK ONE MEASURING QUESTION

The friends repaired the two changed places. The pictures now matched the source, but the wooden sign still had to fit between two gate hooks.

Ivo chose a ribbon labelled WIDTH. "This tool answers one question: Is the sign narrow enough to fit between the hooks?"

WIDTH means the distance from one side to the other side. The ribbon is not checking the pictures or the height.

Visible labels above the pictures: WIDTH RIBBON; LEFT EDGE; RIGHT EDGE; GATE HOOKS

"The width fits, so the whole sign must fit," said Mia.

What should the team do next: guess, vote, or check the HEIGHT with a height tool?

A disagreement does not make the loudest person the answer. It can show that more work is needed.

YOU LEARNED HOW TO CHECK

You looked at what was shown instead of guessing. You built the sign, covered it, rebuilt it, and kept the first try when it did not match. You followed the step trace, used each measuring tool for its own question, and asked Ivo to check by a fresh path.

A check can CONFIRM, DISAGREE, or show that MORE WORK is needed.

This matters because looking again helps us say what we observed, find what changed, and know which question still needs an answer.

Visible labels above the pictures: OBSERVE; KEEP THE TRY; FOLLOW THE TRACE; MEASURE ONE QUESTION; ASK A FRIEND
