Page 3: The trail finds a gate

Last time, you helped Mia, Sol and Tavi mend the moon-and-sun light trail.

The trail now led them to a garden gate. The gate was shut.

Picture description: The repaired light trail reaches a closed garden gate. Mia, Tavi and Sol stop beside it.

Page 4: One clear job

A blank sign stood by the gate. A small plan showed four pictures.

“Let’s make the sign match the plan,” said Mia. “Then we can check it.”

Picture description: A picture plan shows sunflower with sun, bee with moon, apple with leaf and boot with star. A second sign has four empty spaces beside the closed gate.

Page 5: Meet Ivo

A moss-green friend hurried over. He carried a big round lens.

“I’m Ivo,” he said. “I look again when something needs checking. May I help?”

Picture description: Ivo joins Mia, Tavi and Sol beside the closed gate. His large round lens is easy to see.

Page 6: First, look

Ivo held up the picture plan.

“Look slowly,” said Tavi. “What picture is beside the moon?”

Picture description: The full emoji picture plan is visible beside the closed gate. The moon corner contains a bee.

Page 7: Find the moon corner

This page is a paper activity.

Find the MOON on the plan.

Which picture is beside it? Point to SUNFLOWER, BEE, APPLE or BOOT.

Look at the plan before you choose. The next page explains the answer.

Picture description: The plan stays visible above four large emoji choices: sunflower, bee, apple and boot.

Page 8: You found the bee

The BEE is beside the MOON.

You answered from something you could see. That is an observation: saying what you can see now.

Picture description: The moon and bee emoji cards are outlined together and labelled MOON and BEE beside the closed gate.

Page 9: Four cards to place

Ivo opened his bag. Out came a sunflower, a bee, an apple and a boot.

The blank sign waited. The plan stayed where everyone could see it.

Picture description: Four stable emoji picture cards wait beside the visible plan and blank sign.

Page 10: Which sign matches?

This page is a paper activity.

Look at the plan. Then look at signs A, B and C.

Which sign puts every picture beside the same corner mark?

Point to A, B or C. Check all four places before you turn the page.

Picture description: Three complete emoji signs are shown. Only sign B exactly matches the visible picture plan.

Page 11: Sign B matches

Sign B has all four pictures in the same places as the plan.

The friends used the plan while they built. Now they had a sign they could check again.

Picture description: Plan and sign B appear side by side with matching sunflower, bee, apple and boot emoji positions.

Page 12: Curtain up, curtain down

Ivo showed the plan once more. Then a golden curtain covered it.

“Can we remember the four places?” asked Sol.

Picture description: The emoji plan is shown beside a golden curtain that will cover it.

Page 13: What was beside the star?

This page is a paper activity.

The plan is covered.

Which picture was beside the STAR: BEE, APPLE or BOOT?

It is fine to say “I’m not sure.” Turn the page to look again.

Picture description: A covered plan appears above three emoji choices: bee, apple and boot.

Page 14: Look again

Ivo opened the curtain. The BOOT was beside the STAR.

Looking again is how we check a memory. “I wasn’t sure” is a useful answer before we look.

Picture description: The open plan highlights the boot beside the star emoji corner.

Page 15: Spot the two changes

This page is a paper activity.

Sol made a first try from memory. Keep it beside the plan.

Which two pictures changed places? Point to both.

Check each corner. The first try stays visible on the next page.

Picture description: The plan and Sol’s first try are side by side. Bee and boot have swapped places; sunflower and apple stayed put.

Page 16: Bee and boot swapped

BEE and BOOT changed places. SUNFLOWER and APPLE stayed put.

The friends kept Sol’s first try. It helped them see exactly what needed fixing.

Picture description: Bee and boot are outlined on the two retained emoji signs. The unchanged pictures remain visible.

Page 17: Where did the change begin?

This page is a paper activity.

Ivo kept one card for each move Sol made. Read the cards from 1 to 4.

Which is the first card that does not follow the plan?

Point to 1, 2, 3 or 4. Start at the beginning.

Picture description: Four ordered emoji step cards show Sol placing sunflower, apple, boot and bee. Step 3 is the first placement that differs from the plan.

Page 18: Step 3 changed first

Steps 1 and 2 followed the plan. Step 3 put BOOT beside MOON.

That was the first change. Keeping the steps in order showed where the mix-up began.

Picture description: All four step cards remain visible. Step 3 is clearly outlined as the first changed move.

Page 19: Repair the moon corner

This page is a paper activity.

The moon corner has the wrong picture.

Which card belongs there: SUNFLOWER, BEE, APPLE or BOOT?

Use the picture plan. Point before you turn the page.

Picture description: Sol’s retained try highlights the moon corner. Four stable emoji picture choices appear below.

Page 20: The pictures match

BEE went back beside MOON. BOOT went back beside STAR.

Now the rebuilt sign matched the plan. Next, the friends had to see if the sign would fit the gate.

Picture description: The repaired emoji sign matches the picture plan in all four positions.

Page 21: How wide is the sign?

This page is a paper activity.

WIDTH means the distance from one side to the other.

Count the yellow tiles across the sign. Is it 3, 4 or 5 tiles wide?

Touch each equal emoji tile once as you count across.

Picture description: Four equal yellow square emoji tiles run from the left edge of the sign to the right edge. Choices are 3, 4 and 5.

Page 22: Four tiles wide

The sign is 4 tiles wide. The four-tile space between the hooks is the same width. It fits.

This check answered one question: how wide is it?

Picture description: Four yellow square emoji tiles align across the sign and between the gate hooks. Both widths are labelled 4.

Page 23: How tall is the sign?

This page is a paper activity.

HEIGHT means the distance from the bottom to the top.

Count the blue tiles up the sign. Is it 2, 3 or 4 tiles tall?

Count from the bottom upwards. Width and height are different questions.

Picture description: Three equal blue square emoji tiles run from the bottom edge of the sign to the top edge. Choices are 2, 3 and 4.

Page 24: Three tiles tall

The sign is 3 tiles tall. The three-tile space at the gate is the same height. It fits.

The first tiles checked side to side. These tiles checked bottom to top.

Picture description: Three blue square emoji tiles align vertically with the sign and the gate space. Both heights are labelled 3.

Page 25: Ivo checks without peeking

The team covered its finished sign. Ivo used the same plan and a fresh set of picture cards.

He made his own sign without seeing theirs.

Picture description: A hedge hides the team sign while Ivo builds a fresh emoji sign from the same visible plan.

Page 26: Same or different?

This page is a paper activity.

Now both signs are uncovered. Look at every corner.

Are the two signs the SAME or DIFFERENT?

Point to SAME or DIFFERENT. Check all four pictures.

Picture description: The team sign and Ivo’s sign appear side by side. All four emoji pictures are in the same places.

Page 27: Ivo’s sign matches

The two signs are the same. Ivo did not copy the team’s answer. He made his own check, and it matched.

A check can agree, show a difference, or show that we need to look again.

Picture description: Four green check emoji marks pair the matching positions on the team sign and Ivo’s sign.

Page 28: Choose the picture check

This page is a paper activity.

Question 1: Do the pictures match the plan?

Which card answers that question: PLAN AND SIGN, WIDTH TILES, or HEIGHT TILES?

Choose the card that looked at picture places.

Picture description: Three clear record cards show a plan beside a sign, four horizontal tiles and three vertical tiles.

Page 29: The plan checked the pictures

The PLAN AND SIGN card answers the picture question.

The width tiles answered width. The height tiles answered height. Ivo’s own sign checked the result without copying.

Picture description: Four illustrated record cards are labelled pictures, width, height and Ivo’s own check.

Page 30: Is every check ready?

This page is a paper activity.

Look at the four question cards. Each one has a clear answer.

Is the sign ready for the gate: YES or NOT YET?

Check pictures, width, height and Ivo’s own sign before choosing.

Picture description: Four question cards each carry a green check emoji. Two answer choices say YES and NOT YET.

Page 31: The garden gate opens

YES. Every question had its own checked answer. The pictures matched. The width fit. The height fit. Ivo’s sign matched too.

The gate opened, and the morning visitors cheered.

Picture description: The garden gate stands open behind the checked emoji welcome sign. The four friends celebrate.

Page 32: You learned how to check

You looked before answering. You kept a try that did not match. You followed its steps, fixed the first change, measured one direction at a time, and used a second sign made without copying.

Checking matters because it helps us explain what we saw, find a change and decide what to do next. Beyond the garden, a new Star Door began to glow.

Picture description: The four friends stand by the open garden gate and correct emoji sign while a distant Star Door glows.
