## Plain-language story pass

- The Star Door is simply named; decorative material and shape descriptions
  were removed from the child text.
- A note now clearly slides through the letter box, lands beside Mira, and is
  picked up and opened before she reads it.
- Every move between rooms states why the friends go there and what opens the
  way.
- Sol sees his teddy inside the parcel before the child moves it. The word
  *empty* appears only after the child looks inside the box.
- Child dialogue uses ordinary speech, short sentences and first-person lines.
- Book Two received the same plain-language and cause-and-effect review before
  further Level Two production.

## Character and picture corrections

- Mira, Sol and Tavi remain the permanent team.
- Nori is the only new character in Book One and appears only from the bell room
  onwards.
- The cover no longer contains unused characters.
- Four-character scenes now render the full cast.
- The note is clearly visible beside Mira's boots, in her hand when first read,
  and in her hand when read again.
- All 32 student pages and all five adult-guide pages were rendered and visually
  inspected.

## Matching companion game

Game review 1.5.0 uses the same revised wording and 35 caption-matched local
Kokoro narration lines. Device-local progress now preserves the exact scene,
dialogue turn and unfinished activity when a mobile browser hides or reloads
the page. A 390-pixel mobile check confirmed that Sol's parcel line and the
partly completed teddy activity both return correctly.

## Scientific and publication status

The scientific source and receipt are unchanged. Generated images, local voice
models and curriculum references remain production or calibration tools, never
authorities in SFT derivation. This review build has not been copied to
`publications/education/`, publicly hosted, released with a DOI or finally
approved.
