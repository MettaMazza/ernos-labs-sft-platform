## Complete narrative rebuild

The Star Door now wakes before breakfast and physically supplies the note that
causes the whole adventure. The visible five-star route leads through the
parcel, bell gallery, paper room, letter tiles and curtain passage. Each checked
clue lights exactly one star; the fifth opens the final chamber. The two doors
resolve the note, the library recall retrieves the first clue, and only then
does a new parcel preview E02.

## Learning and visual changes

- Replaced the previous emoji/layout edition with six original generated 3D
  environments and six recognisable original traveller characters.
- Kept all child-facing words and answer labels above the illustrations.
- Put each action before its explanation: move, listen, pretend-draw, step,
  reveal, inspect both paths, then recall.
- Defined *empty*, *no ring*, *blank*, *hidden*, *word* and *remember* in the
  bounded scene where the child has just experienced them.
- Preserved six optional hidden codes as playful extras only.
- Added a coherent returning-character rule: natural story encounters or
  optional process hints after a genuine attempt, never an answer reveal.
- Added a 32-page semantic HTML edition with page-specific image descriptions.

## Matching companion

Game review 1.4.0 is a fixed full-screen moving stage rather than a menu or
read-and-scroll activity. Characters walk, idle, speak and celebrate in the
same rooms; the child manipulates the actual scene. Twenty-eight caption-matched
lines were pre-rendered from Maria Smith's local Kokoro ONNX weights for offline
play, with local interaction sound effects and device-only progress.
