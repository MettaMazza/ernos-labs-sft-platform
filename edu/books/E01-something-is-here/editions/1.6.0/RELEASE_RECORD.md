# E01 review record 1.6.0

Date: 31 July 2026
Author: Maria Smith
Status: review; final-publication approval pending

This version makes the child-facing language rule explicit: use the ordinary
word first and add a detail only when the child needs it to see, choose or act.
The opening now says that the Star Door is shut, a note comes through its letter
box, the note lands next to Mira, and Mira picks it up and opens it.

The matching Level One captions and all 35 local Kokoro files use the same
wording. During the spoken note sequence, a labelled note now lands visibly
beside Mira and moves to her hand when she picks it up.

Both 32-page student books and their five-page adult guides were rendered and
visually checked. A 390-by-724 browser test confirmed the note sequence, mobile
layout, exact reload restoration and exact return-from-another-tab restoration.

This review has not been copied into `publications/education/` and is not a
final publication.
