## Method

inspected at its rendered page resolution. Pages changed during the pass were
rendered and inspected again. The four-page adult guide was rendered and
inspected as a complete contact sheet and at readable size. PDF text extraction,
semantic HTML topology, source/receipt records and the unpublished boundary were
checked by `source/verify_e01_v2_0.py`.

## Cross-page findings

- Mia, Sol and Tavi remain the permanent trio. Nori is the only guest.
- Characters remain grounded and do not overlap headings or page furniture.
- The book contains no menu, dialogue box, progress meter, digital button or
  game-screen layout.
- Repeated learning objects use the same versioned OpenMoji asset: note, box,
  teddy, bell, blank card, pencil, door and star.
- Generated images establish the tower, character expression, movement and
  atmosphere. They do not redefine repeated learning objects.
- Child-facing object labels are above their illustrations. Activity words that
  function as destinations sit at the end of their visible tracing paths.
- No answer depends on colour alone. Shape, position, label and story context
  reinforce each distinction.
- All 32 accessible HTML page sections have a labelled image role and a full
  page description.
- Every activity is possible on paper through pointing, tracing, listening,
  speaking, a page turn or safe separate paper.

## Adult guide

The adult guide renders as four complete A4 pages with no clipped, blank or
orphan page. It explains the story order, activity answers, repeated-object
rule, accessibility and safety, exact admitted SFT statement, operational
boundary and review status.
