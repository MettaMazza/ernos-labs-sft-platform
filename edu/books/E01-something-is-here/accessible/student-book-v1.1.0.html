THE NOTHING HUNT

Page 3

Mira found a mystery map. FIND NOTHING, it said.

Pip flapped his wings. 'A treasure hunt!'

HOW TO PLAY

Page 4

Spot a clue. Say what you notice. Turn the page to check.

Keep every surprise. That is how detectives learn.

WARM-UP - SPOT 5

Page 5

What is here in Mira's room? Can you spot five things?

Point, name, sign or make a sound for each one.

CHECK THE CLUES

Page 6

Mira is here. Pip is here. The box, bell and rug are here too.

Did you find different things? They count as clues too.

CLUE 1 - THE BOX

Page 7

Mira opened the box. No toy was inside. Did she find nothing?

Look closely before you decide.

CLUE 1 - REVEAL

Page 8

The toy was not inside. But the box was here. Looking was happening.

Empty inside is not the same as no thing shown.

CLUE STAR 1

Page 9

EMPTY tells us what is missing from a container. The container can still be here.

Pip sticks the first star on the map.

CLUE 2 - THE BELL

Page 10

Mira waited. The bell did not ring. What DID happen?

Cup your hand by your ear and listen with Mira.

CLUE 2 - REVEAL

Page 11

No ring happened. Mira listened. Pip waited. The bell stayed still.

Can you spot all three happenings in the picture?

CLUE 3 - THE BLANK CARD

Page 12

Pip found a blank card. 'Nothing!' he chirped. What can YOU spot?

There are four clues before you turn the page.

CLUE 3 - REVEAL

Page 13

The card was here. Its edge was here. A feather and a magnifying glass were here.

Blank means no mark on the card. It does not make the card disappear.

CLUE 4 - HIDDEN WORD

Page 14

A word is hiding in this busy picture. Can you find NOTHING?

Look across the blocks from left to right.

CLUE 4 - REVEAL

Page 15

You found the word NOTHING. The word is something we can see and read.

A name does not turn into the thing it names.

CLUE 5 - BEHIND THE CURTAIN

Page 16

Mira hid every toy behind a curtain. Did the room become nothing?

Spot what stayed in the picture.

CLUE 5 - REVEAL

Page 17

The toys were hidden. The curtain was here. Mira and Pip were still playing.

Hidden from one view is not no thing shown.

THE MAP OPENS

Page 18

Five clue stars made the map POP open. Two mystery doors appeared.

'One last level,' said Mira.

THE TWO-DOOR GAME

Page 19

Door A says: 'Here is my nothing.' Door B gives no example. Which door can Mira check?

Follow each path with your finger.

DOOR A - CHECK

Page 20

Door A gives Mira an example. Giving it is happening. The card is here.

So Door A has shown something—not nothing.

DOOR B - CHECK

Page 21

Door B gives no example. There is nothing for Mira to point to or check.

We do not pretend a secret example was given.

TRY TO BEAT THE GAME

Page 22

Can you draw nothing on a card? Try—or choose not to draw.

Then turn the page. Both choices are allowed.

CHECK BOTH CHOICES

Page 23

Made a mark? The mark is here. Made no mark? The card is still here.

Either way, no drawn example of nothing appeared.

MIRA'S DETECTIVE RULE

Page 24

Show an example—and the showing is something. Show no example—and there is no example to use.

Every level of the Nothing Hunt gave the same clue.

TREASURE FOUND

Page 25

There is no nothing.

Mira and Pip found the starting clue of Smithian Fold Theory.

THE FAIR-PLAY RULE

Page 26

Our game checks what can be shown, said, drawn or recorded. It does not make up things no one can show.

Good detectives say where their rule stops.

FINAL SPOT-THE-CLUE

Page 27

Find four clues: an empty box, a still bell, a blank card and a hidden toy.

For each clue, say what is here and what did not happen or appear.

FINAL SPOT - ANSWERS

Page 28

Box here—no toy inside. Bell here—no ring. Card here—no mark. Curtain here—toy hidden.

You kept each missing thing separate from what was present.

SORT THE CARDS

Page 29

Which cards show something to check? Which card gives no example?

Move your finger from each picture to a basket.

SORT - ANSWERS

Page 30

Box, bell and card: something to check. No example given: no example to use.

The words empty, still and blank did not erase the things.

NOTHING-HUNT DETECTIVE

Page 31

I spotted. I wondered. I checked. I kept the surprises.

Add your name, mark or sticker with a grown-up.
