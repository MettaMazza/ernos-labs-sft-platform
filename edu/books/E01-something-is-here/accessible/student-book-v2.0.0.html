Page 3: Page 3

High above the town stood the Tower of Star Rooms.

One bright morning, Mia opened its little blue door.

Picture description: Mia steps into the warm tower at sunrise. A winding path leads towards the Star Door.

Page 4: Page 4

“Hello! I’m Tavi,” said the blue friend.

“And I’m Sol!” said the golden one. “Come and see our Star Door.”

Picture description: Tavi waves to Mia while Sol points along the tower path towards the Star Door.

Page 5: Page 5

The three new friends followed the path together.

The Star Door was shut. It only opened when an adventure was ready.

Picture description: Mia, Tavi and Sol arrive together and look up at the shut Star Door.

Page 6: Page 6

The door gave a tiny shake.

A note slid through its slot and landed by Mia’s hand.

Picture description: Mia kneels beside a clearly labelled note while Tavi and Sol look at it.

Page 7: Can you find the note?

This page is a paper activity.

Point to the note Mia found.

Picture description: A map emoji, a blue book emoji and a note emoji form three clear choices.

Page 8: Page 8

Mia picked it up and read aloud:

FIND NOTHING.
FIVE STARS WILL SHOW THE WAY.

Picture description: Mia holds the note. Sol looks puzzled and Tavi leans closer to listen.

Page 9: Page 9

“Find nothing?” Sol scratched his head. “What does nothing look like?”

Five star shapes appeared. The first one pointed to a box.

Picture description: The friends gather around a clearly labelled box on the rug. Five pale stars wait above it.

Page 10: Page 10

Sol opened the box.

“There’s my teddy!” he cheered.

Picture description: A teddy emoji is visibly inside the box emoji while the friends look at it.

Page 11: Help the teddy out

This page is a paper activity.

Start at the teddy. Trace the gold path to the rug.

Picture description: A curved gold finger trail leads from the teddy in the box to an open place on the rug.

Page 12: Page 12

The teddy sat on the rug. The box stayed where it was.

“The box is empty,” said Tavi. “The teddy is not inside it now.”

Picture description: The empty box remains on the rug and the teddy is clearly outside it. One star shines.

Page 13: Page 13

The first star lit a path through a blue door.

The friends followed it into a round room.

Picture description: Mia, Sol and Tavi enter the listening room and see Nori beside an empty bell stand.

Page 14: Page 14

A friend in a cap waved.

“I’m Nori. I like to listen carefully. Shall we try together?”

Picture description: Nori welcomes Mia, Sol and Tavi beside a clearly labelled bell.

Page 15: Page 15

Nori rang the bell once.

DING!

Then everyone waited.

Picture description: The bell is shown above the stand as the four friends listen to one ring.

Page 16: Sound ... then quiet

This page is a paper activity.

Ask a grown-up to clap once. Listen until the sound stops. Put your hands together when it is quiet.

Picture description: Nori and the friends listen beside the still bell. Two stars shine.

Page 17: Page 17

The quiet bell was still there.

The next star led them to the paper room, where a card waited with no marks on it yet.

Picture description: A plain white card emoji sits clearly on the empty table while all four friends look at it.

Page 18: Mark it—or leave it

This page is a paper activity.

On your own paper, make one mark or leave it as it is. Is the paper still there?

Picture description: A blank card and a pencil sit apart on the table for a choose-and-compare activity.

Page 19: Page 19

“The card is still here,” said Mia.

“A card with no marks is blank. Blank does not mean gone.”

Picture description: The blank card remains on the table. Three stars shine.

Page 20: A word appears

This page is a paper activity.

Point from left to right. Say each letter, then say the whole word.

Picture description: Seven large letter tiles spell N O T H I N G from left to right. Four stars shine.

Page 21: Page 21

As they left, Sol’s teddy slipped from his bag.

Bump, roll, roll—it stopped behind a curtain.

Picture description: The friends follow a trail to the closed curtain. The teddy has stopped at its edge.

Page 22: Page 22

Tavi closed the curtain.

Now they could not see the teddy.

“Did it go away?” asked Sol.

Picture description: The teddy is out of sight behind the closed curtain while the four friends remember its path.

Page 23: Where did it stop?

This page is a paper activity.

Follow the little prints with your finger. Point to the place where the teddy is hiding.

Picture description: A visible trail of small prints leads to the closed curtain for a memory and prediction game.

Page 24: Page 24

Tavi pulled the curtain slowly.

The curtain opened. There was the teddy!

“Hidden is not gone,” said Mia.

Picture description: The teddy appears beside the curtain and is clearly labelled. All five stars shine.

Page 25: Page 25

All five stars flashed. The way opened with a happy click.

The friends stepped into a room with two matching shelves.

Picture description: Mia, Sol, Tavi and Nori enter a room with two matching open shelf doorways.

Page 26: Look at both shelves

This page is a paper activity.

Point to the shelf with a card. Now point to the shelf with no card.

Picture description: The left shelf holds one plain white card. The matching right shelf has no card.

Page 27: Page 27

“This shelf has a card,” said Sol.

“This shelf has no card,” said Tavi. “But both shelves are still here.”

Picture description: The friends compare one shelf with a card and one shelf without a card.

Page 28: Page 28

Mia read the note again.

They had found empty, quiet, blank and hidden. They had not found a secret object called nothing.

Picture description: Mia holds the note while the team smiles at the mystery they have solved.

Page 29: Match the clues

This page is a paper activity.

Trace each gold trail. Say the word at the end.

Picture description: Four large emoji scenes connect by clear trails to the words empty, quiet, blank and hidden.

Page 30: You solved it

You saw an empty box, a quiet bell, a blank card, a hidden teddy and a shelf with no card.

None was a secret thing called nothing. Clear words help people understand what really happened.

Picture description: Mia, Sol, Tavi and Nori celebrate beside the finished five-star map.

Page 31: Page 31

Mia put the map in its stand. Nori waved goodbye.

Then a new parcel whooshed down the tower slide and stopped on the rug.

Picture description: The tower delivery slide is clearly visible from its wall hatch to a new parcel on the rug. Nori waves goodbye.

Page 32: Page 32

Mia read the new question:

HOW CAN ONE WHOLE HAVE MANY PARTS?

Sol grinned. “Let’s find out!”
