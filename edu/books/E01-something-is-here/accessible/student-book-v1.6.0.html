Page 3: THE TOWER OF STAR ROOMS

High above the sleeping town stood a tower full of starry rooms.

Before breakfast, three friends met inside: Mia, Sol and Tavi.

Mia loved questions. Sol loved surprises. Tavi loved looking closely.

Page 4: A NOTE ARRIVES

At the heart of the tower stood the Star Door. It opened when a new adventure was ready.

This morning, it was shut. Then a note came through its letter box and landed with a soft tap.

The note landed beside Mia. Point to the note and the letter box.

Page 5: MIA FINDS THE NOTE

Mia picked it up. “A message for us!” she said. Then she read it aloud:

FIND NOTHING.

FIVE CLUES WILL SHOW THE WAY.

Sol blinked. “How do we find nothing?” Tavi smiled. “Let’s look carefully and see.” An optional book code is hiding in the picture.

Optional picture code: ROOMSTAR

Page 6: THE FIVE-STAR MAP

The wall shimmered. A map appeared with five pale stars.

“One clue for each star,” said Mia. “When we learn what a clue shows us, its star will shine.”

A glowing arrow pointed to a small parcel. Follow the arrow with your finger.

Page 7: CLUE ONE · OPEN THE PARCEL

The friends followed the arrow. Sol lifted the lid.

“My teddy!” he cheered.

Point to the teddy. Now pretend to lift it out and place it beside the box.

First notice what is inside. Then make the move.

Page 8: LOOK INSIDE THE BOX

The teddy sat beside the box. The box had not moved.

Sol peered inside. “What is inside the box now?”

Look inside. Point to the box and the teddy. Then answer.

Page 9: STAR ONE SHINES

THE TEDDY IS OUTSIDE.

THE BOX IS STILL HERE.

“The box is empty,” said Tavi. “Empty means there is no teddy inside. The box is still here.”

You checked what moved and what stayed. Star one shone gold. Can you spot the optional book code?

Optional picture code: BOXCLUE

Page 10: MEET NORI

The map’s next arrow led through a blue door to a room full of bells.

A small purple guide waved. “I’m Nori. I listen carefully. I can help with this clue.”

Mia, Sol and Tavi said hello. Nori joined them for the rest of this adventure.

Page 11: CLUE TWO · LISTEN

The biggest bell swung once and rang. Then it stopped.

“Cup your hand to your ear,” said Nori. “Listen carefully. What is the bell doing now?”

What can you hear? Is the bell ringing now? Listen before you answer.

Page 12: STAR TWO SHINES

“The bell did not ring while we listened,” said Nori. “It was quiet, but it was still here.”

Sol whispered, “I can still hear my breathing.”

You listened before answering. A quiet bell is not nothing. Star two shone gold. Can you spot the optional code?

Optional picture code: QUIETWINGS

Page 13: THE PAPER ROOM

The next arrow led to the paper room.

On a table lay a white card and a pencil. Mia picked up the card. “There are no marks on it yet.”

First look at the card. What is there? What is not on it?

Page 14: CLUE THREE · TRY THE CARD

Choose one. Pretend to draw a mark on the card, or leave the card just as it is.

In either choice, is the card still there?

Point to the card before you turn the page.

Page 15: STAR THREE SHINES

IF WE DRAW: the card has a mark.

IF WE DO NOT DRAW: the card has no mark.

A card with no marks is blank. It is still a card.

You compared two choices and checked what stayed. Star three shone gold. Can you spot the optional code?

Optional picture code: BLANKEDGE

Page 16: SEVEN TILES LIGHT UP

Seven blank tiles lit up on the wall.

“One tile for each letter,” said Sol. “Maybe the next clue can show us the word on the note.”

The tiles blinked from left to right, ready for the next clue.

Page 17: CLUE FOUR · BUILD THE WORD

Start at the left. Point to each tile:

N O T H I N G

Now say the whole word: nothing.

You are building one word from seven letters. Point, say, then turn.

Page 18: STAR FOUR SHINES

“The letters make the word nothing,” said Tavi.

Mia tapped the tiles. “We can see the word on the tiles. But a written word is not an object called nothing.”

You found the written word, not a new object. Star four shone gold.

Page 19: THE RED CURTAIN

The fourth star sent a beam of light along a hallway. The friends followed it to a red curtain.

Sol’s teddy slipped from his bag and rolled behind the open curtain.

Everyone watched the teddy stop behind the curtain.

Page 20: CLUE FIVE · OUT OF SIGHT

Nori pulled the curtain closed. The teddy was out of sight.

“We cannot see it now,” said Mia. “Did it stop being there?”

Think about what you just saw. Decide before you turn.

Page 21: STAR FIVE SHINES

Mia slowly opened the curtain. First one paw appeared. Then two ears. Then—there was the teddy!

“It was hidden,” said Mia. “It stayed there, even when we could not see it.”

Hidden does not mean gone. You checked by opening the curtain. Star five shone gold. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: CURTAINMAP

Page 22: THE STAR DOOR OPENS

All five stars flashed—one, two, three, four, five. The Star Door opened into a room with two little doors.

“One last check,” said Tavi.

The friends went through together.

Page 23: LOOK BEHIND BOTH DOORS

Behind Door A was a shelf with a white card.

Behind Door B was a shelf with no card.

Point to the card. Then point to both shelves.

Which things can you see? Look before you answer.

Page 24: SAY WHAT YOU FOUND

Mia asked, “What did we find?”

Door A: a door, a shelf and a card.

Door B: a door and an empty shelf.

Did we find an object called nothing?

Say what is there. Then say what is missing from Door B.

Page 25: MYSTERY SOLVED

“Door A has a card,” said Sol. “Door B has an empty shelf.”

“Both doors and both shelves are still here,” said Tavi. “We did not find an object called nothing.”

You named what was there and what was missing. The mystery is solved! Find the final optional code.

Optional picture code: TWODOORS

Page 26: MIA READS THE NOTE AGAIN

Mia read the note again: FIND NOTHING.

They had found an empty box, a quiet bell, a blank card, a written word and a hidden teddy. They never found an object called nothing.

The clues taught us to say exactly what was there, what changed and what we could not see or hear.

Page 27: WHAT DO YOU REMEMBER?

The friends took the five-star map to the library. Tavi placed the box and teddy on a table.

“Which one became empty when we moved the teddy?”

Point to the box or the teddy. Use the first clue to help you.

Page 28: THE BOX BECAME EMPTY

“The box,” said Tavi. “It became empty because the teddy moved out.”

The teddy changed place. The box stayed in the room.

Remembering a clue helps you use the lesson again.

Page 29: THE MAP GOES HOME

Mia put the finished map in its library stand. Nori rang one soft bell to celebrate.

“We looked, listened, tried and checked,” said Mia. “That is how we solved the mystery.”

Nori promised to visit again when a mystery needed careful listening.

Page 30: WHAT THIS ADVENTURE TAUGHT YOU

Here is what the mystery taught you:

Empty, quiet, blank and hidden are different. Each word tells us something clear about what we found.

We did not find an object called nothing.

Why it matters: careful words help other people understand what you saw or heard.

Page 31: A NEW PARCEL ARRIVES

Just then, a small bell rang. A hatch opened high in the wall. A blue-and-gold parcel slid down the library slide and stopped beside Sol.

The slide was the tower’s special way of delivering a new adventure.

Page 32: ONE NEW QUESTION

Mia read the parcel label aloud:

HOW CAN ONE WHOLE HAVE MANY PARTS?

Sol hugged his teddy. “Let’s find out!”
