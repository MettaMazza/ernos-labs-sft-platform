Page 3: THE STAR DOOR

Before breakfast, Mia, Sol and Tavi met in the star room.

The Star Door was shut.

The door had a letter box. A parcel was across the room.

Page 4: A NOTE COMES THROUGH

A note came through the letter box and landed next to Mia.

Mia picked up the note and opened it.

Page 5: MIA FINDS A NOTE

“I found a note!” said Mia. She read it aloud:

FIND NOTHING.

FIVE CLUES WILL SHOW THE WAY.

Sol said, “That is a funny thing to look for. Let’s follow the clues!” A tiny optional book code is hiding in the room.

Optional picture code: ROOMSTAR

Page 6: FIVE STAR SHAPES

A map lit up above the door. It showed five star outlines. None were gold yet.

“Each clue will turn one star gold,” said Mia.

The first arrow pointed to the parcel. Trace the arrow with your finger.

Page 7: CLUE ONE · OPEN THE PARCEL

The friends followed the arrow. Sol opened the parcel.

His brown teddy was inside. “There is my toy!” he said.

Lift the teddy out and put it beside the box.

Move the toy before you turn the page.

Page 8: LOOK INSIDE AFTER THE MOVE

The teddy now sat beside the parcel. Sol looked into the box.

“The toy is outside now. What is inside the box?”

Point to everything that is still here before you turn.

Page 9: STAR ONE LIGHTS

TEDDY OUTSIDE.

BOX STILL HERE.

“The box is empty,” said Tavi. “Empty means there is no toy inside this box. The box is still here.”

The first star filled with gold. Find the hidden optional code.

Optional picture code: BOXCLUE

Page 10: A BLUE DOOR OPENS

They had found an empty box, not a thing called nothing.

The first star turned gold. A blue door opened. Nori was waiting beside three big bells.

“Hello! I’m Nori. I listen for tiny sounds. May I help?”

Page 11: CLUE TWO · LISTEN

The wind stopped. The biggest bell stopped moving.

Nori stood beside it. “Cup your hand to your ear. Let’s be quiet and listen.”

What can you hear? Listen before you turn.

Page 12: STAR TWO LIGHTS

“The bell did not ring while we listened,” said Nori.

“I heard my breath. The quiet bell was still here.”

The second star filled with gold. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: QUIETWINGS

Page 13: MIA FINDS A CARD

The second star turned gold. A door opened to the paper room.

Mia picked up a white card from the table. The card had no marks on it yet.

A pencil was beside the card.

Page 14: CLUE THREE · TRY THE CARD

Use your finger in the air.

Pretend to draw a mark, or do not draw.

What is still there both times?

You can choose either one. Choose before you turn.

Page 15: STAR THREE LIGHTS

DRAW: A MARK AND A CARD.

DO NOT DRAW: A BLANK CARD.

Blank means there is no mark on the card. The card is still there both times.

The third star turned gold. Find the hidden optional code.

Optional picture code: BLANKEDGE

Page 16: SEVEN TILES LIGHT UP

The card was still there, so the third star filled with gold.

Seven tiles on the wall lit up, one after another. Each tile showed one letter.

Sol pointed to the first tile. “Read them from left to right with me!”

Page 17: CLUE FOUR · READ THE WORD

Point to each tile from left to right:

N O T H I N G

Say each letter, then say the whole word.

Page 18: STAR FOUR LIGHTS

“The seven letters spell the word nothing,” said Tavi.

“We can see and read the word. The word does not show us a thing called nothing.”

The fourth star turned gold.

Page 19: THE TEDDY ROLLS AWAY

The fourth star opened a hallway with a red curtain.

As Sol walked in, his teddy rolled out of his bag. It crossed the floor and stopped behind the open curtain.

Everyone saw where the teddy went.

Page 20: CLUE FIVE · CLOSE THE CURTAIN

Nori closed the red curtain. The curtain hid the teddy, so no one could see it.

“I saw where it went,” said Sol. “Is it still there?”

Remember where the teddy stopped. Decide before you turn.

Page 21: STAR FIVE LIGHTS

Mia opened the curtain. “There is the teddy!”

“The teddy was hidden,” she said. “Hidden means it was there, but the curtain stopped us from seeing it.”

The teddy did not disappear. The fifth star turned gold. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: CURTAINMAP

Page 22: THE STAR DOOR OPENS

The fifth star turned gold.

Now all five stars were gold. The Star Door opened.

Finishing all five clues opened the door.

Page 23: TWO SMALL DOORS

Behind the Star Door were two small doors.

Door A had a white card on its shelf. Door B had an empty shelf.

Which door showed nothing?

Look behind both doors before you decide.

Page 24: LOOK AT BOTH DOORS

Door A showed a card.

Door B showed an empty shelf.

Mia asked, “Did either door show a thing called nothing?”

Only use what you can see behind the doors.

Page 25: THE FRIENDS ANSWER

“Door A showed a card. Door B showed an empty shelf,” said Tavi.

“Neither door showed a thing called nothing.”

The note’s puzzle is solved. Find the final hidden optional code.

Optional picture code: TWODOORS

Page 26: MIA READS THE NOTE AGAIN

Mia opened the same note and read it again:

FIND NOTHING.

They found a box, a quiet bell, a card, a written word and a hidden teddy—but no thing called nothing.

Nothing was not a thing they could find.

Page 27: REMEMBER THE FIRST CLUE

The friends carried the map into the library.

Tavi asked, “Which thing became empty after you moved Sol’s teddy outside?”

Choose between the open box and the teddy. Think before you turn.

Page 28: THE BOX BECAME EMPTY

EMPTY BOX.

TEDDY OUTSIDE.

The box became empty after the teddy moved outside, but the box stayed in the room.

To remember is to think of something from earlier.

Page 29: MIA PUTS THE MAP AWAY

Mia folded the note and put the finished map in its library stand.

“Now we can tell the whole story again,” she said.

The first adventure is complete.

Page 30: TAVI’S CAREFUL CHECK

1. LOOK: What can you see or hear?

2. SAY: What changed?

3. CHECK: Does your answer match what you found?

Can you tell one clue again?

Tavi can ask how you checked, but will not tell you a new answer.

Page 31: A NEW PARCEL ARRIVES

CHIME!

A little door opened high in the library wall. A blue-and-gold parcel slid down a ramp and stopped beside Sol.

Sol grinned. “Our first mystery is finished. Is this the next one?”

Page 32: ONE NEW QUESTION

Mia read the parcel label aloud:

HOW CAN ONE WHOLE HAVE MANY PARTS?
