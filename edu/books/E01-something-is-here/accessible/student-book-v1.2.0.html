THE NOTHING HUNT

Page 3

Mira found a mystery map. FIND NOTHING, it said.

Pip flapped his wings. 'A treasure hunt!'

HOW TO PLAY

Page 4

A detective looks for clues. Spot a clue. Say what you notice. Turn the page to check.

A clue is something you notice that helps you look again.

WARM-UP - SPOT 5

Page 5

Mira is in her room. Can you spot five things?

Look carefully. The next page will name the objects.

CHECK THE CLUES

Page 6

You found Mira's room! Now follow each label to its object.

Can you find one more object than the labels name?

CLUE 1 - THE BOX

Page 7

Mira opened the box. No toy was inside. Did she find nothing?

Look closely before you decide.

CLUE 1 - REVEAL

Page 8

The toy was not inside. But the box was here. Looking was happening.

Empty inside is not the same as no thing shown.

CLUE STAR 1

Page 9

A container is something that can hold things, like this box. EMPTY tells us what is missing from inside it.

The container can still be here.

CLUE 2 - THE BELL

Page 10

Mira waited. The bell did not ring. What DID happen?

Cup your hand by your ear and listen with Mira.

CLUE 2 - REVEAL

Page 11

No ring happened. Mira listened. Pip waited. The bell stayed still.

Can you spot all three happenings in the picture?

CLUE 3 - THE BLANK CARD

Page 12

Pip found a blank card. 'Nothing!' he chirped. What can YOU spot?

There are four clues before you turn the page.

CLUE 3 - REVEAL

Page 13

The card was here. A pencil was here. A feather and a magnifying glass were here.

Blank means no mark on the card. It does not make the card disappear.

CLUE 4 - HIDDEN WORD

Page 14

A word is hiding in this busy picture. Can you find NOTHING?

Look across the blocks from left to right.

CLUE 4 - REVEAL

Page 15

You found the word NOTHING. The word is something we can see and read.

A name does not turn into the thing it names.

CLUE 5 - BEHIND THE CURTAIN

Page 16

Mira put the toys behind a curtain. Part of each toy was hidden. Did the room become nothing?

Spot what stayed in the picture.

CLUE 5 - REVEAL

Page 17

Part of each toy was hidden. The curtain was here. Mira and Pip were still playing.

A view is what we can see from one place. Hidden from this view is not gone.

THE MAP OPENS

Page 18

Five clue stars made the map POP open. Two mystery doors appeared.

'One last level,' said Mira.

THE TWO-DOOR GAME

Page 19

Door A holds out a card. Door B holds out no card. Which door gives Mira something to look at?

Something shown for us to look at is called an example.

DOOR A - CHECK

Page 20

Door A shows Mira the card. Mira can look at it and point to it. This card is an example.

An example is something shown for us to look at or check.

DOOR B - CHECK

Page 21

Door B has not shown Mira a card. We have not seen an object from Door B yet.

We can say: no example was given. We do not invent one.

TRY TO BEAT THE GAME

Page 22

Can you draw nothing on a card? Try—or choose not to draw.

Then turn the page. Both choices are allowed.

CHECK BOTH CHOICES

Page 23

Made a mark? The mark is here. Made no mark? The card is still here. Neither choice made nothing appear.

We could still point to the mark, the card or the choice.

MIRA'S DETECTIVE RULE

Page 24

Show something—and we have something to look at. If no object is shown, we have no object from that door to use.

That is what no example was given means.

TREASURE FOUND

Page 25

There is no nothing.

Mira and Pip found the starting clue of Smithian Fold Theory.

THE FAIR-PLAY RULE

Page 26

Our game talks about what someone can show, say, draw or save in a note. It does not guess about what no one has shown.

A careful detective says exactly what the game can check.

FINAL SPOT-THE-CLUE

Page 27

Find four clues: an empty box, a still bell, a blank card and a partly hidden toy.

For each clue, say what is here and what did not happen or appear.

FINAL SPOT - ANSWERS

Page 28

Box here—no toy inside. Bell here—no ring. Card here—no mark. Curtain here—toy partly hidden.

You kept each missing thing separate from the things still here.

SORT THE CARDS

Page 29

Which pictures show an object you can look at? Which empty frame means: no object was shown yet?

Move your finger from each picture or frame to a basket.

SORT - ANSWERS

Page 30

Box, bell and card: objects to look at. Empty frame: no object was shown yet.

That is what NO EXAMPLE GIVEN means.

NOTHING-HUNT DETECTIVE

Page 31

I spotted. I wondered. I checked. I kept the surprises.

Add your name, mark or sticker with a grown-up.
