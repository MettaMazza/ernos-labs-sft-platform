Page 3: THE DOOR WAKES

Before breakfast, Mira heard a CLUNK.

The sleeping Star Door had woken.

What do you think made the sound?

Page 4: A NOTE SLIDES OUT

A folded note slid through the brass slot.

FIND NOTHING.

FIVE CLUES WILL SHOW THE WAY.

Mira read the note twice. The crew leaned closer.

Page 5: MEET TAVI

Sol bounced on his toes.

“Easy! I find things that are not there all the time.”

Tavi smiled. “Then let us look carefully.”

Tavi is a careful spotter. A tiny book code is hiding in the room.

Optional picture code: ROOMSTAR

Page 6: THE ROUTE BEGINS

The map showed five dark stars.

Mira tucked the note into her pocket.

The crew followed the first golden arrow.

Trace the route with your finger. Where does it begin?

Page 7: CLUE ONE · THE PARCEL

Sol opened the parcel.

“My toy!” he cheered.

Lift the toy out of the box.

Use your finger to show where the toy should go.

Page 8: LOOK AGAIN

Sol peered inside.

“Now the box has nothing in it!”

What is still here?

Say or point before you turn the page.

Page 9: STAR ONE LIGHTS

TOY OUTSIDE.

BOX HERE.

“Empty means the toy is not inside,” said Tavi.

The first star lit.

The empty box did not disappear. Find the hidden optional code.

Optional picture code: BOXCLUE

Page 10: A BLUE DOOR OPENS

The first star opened a blue door.

Beyond it, three brass bells swayed in the wind.

The crew followed Nori into the bell gallery.

Page 11: CLUE TWO · LISTEN

Then the wind stopped.

The big bell hung still.

Cup your hand to your ear. What can you hear?

Wait for one slow breath before you turn.

Page 12: STAR TWO LIGHTS

“No ring,” whispered Nori.

“But I heard my breath, and the bell stayed here.”

The second star lit.

No ring means the bell did not ring during this short listen. Find the hidden optional code.

Optional picture code: QUIETWINGS

Page 13: THE PAPER ROOM

The second star opened a paper room.

Vee found a cream card.

“It has nothing on it. Shall we test that?”

The card and a chunky pencil wait on the round table.

Page 14: CLUE THREE · TRY IT

Use your finger in the air.

Pretend to make a mark—or leave the card blank.

What stays?

Both choices are allowed. Choose before you turn.

Page 15: STAR THREE LIGHTS

MARK HERE—or BLANK CARD HERE.

A blank card is still a card.

The third star lit.

Neither choice made nothing appear. Find the hidden optional code.

Optional picture code: BLANKEDGE

Page 16: SEVEN TILES GLOW

The card made seven wall tiles glow.

Moss looked up. “They want us to step a word.”

Seven separate coloured tiles wait across the wall.

Page 17: CLUE FOUR · STEP THE WORD

Point to each tile as you read:

N O T H I N G

Go slowly from left to right.

Page 18: STAR FOUR LIGHTS

“Nothing is a word we can see, say and read,” said Moss.

The fourth star lit.

The word stayed visible after the crew named it.

Page 19: THE CURTAIN PASSAGE

The fourth star opened a curtain passage.

“I can make Sol’s toy vanish,” said Luma. “Watch!”

Luma carried the toy to a red curtain.

Page 20: CLUE FIVE · HIDDEN

Luma pulled the red curtain shut.

Sol’s toy disappeared from view.

Has the toy gone?

What can you still see? Decide before you turn.

Page 21: STAR FIVE LIGHTS

Luma opened the curtain.

“There it is! Hidden is not gone.”

The fifth star lit.

The curtain changed what the crew could see. Find the hidden optional code.

Optional picture code: CURTAINMAP

Page 22: THE STAR DOOR OPENS

Five stars blazed.

The great Star Door rolled open with a golden WHOOSH.

The whole journey had led back to the door.

Page 23: THE FINAL QUESTION

Two little doors waited.

Door A offered a card.

Door B offered no object.

Which door showed nothing?

Inspect both doors before you decide.

Page 24: KEEP BOTH PATHS

Look at Door A. It showed a card.

Look at Door B. It showed no object.

Choose carefully before you turn.

Do not add a secret object that the picture did not show.

Page 25: THE CREW ANSWERS

“Neither,” said Tavi.

“A showed a card. B showed no object, so we do not invent one.”

The note’s puzzle is solved. Find the final hidden optional code.

Optional picture code: TWODOORS

Page 26: THE NOTE MAKES SENSE

Mira read the old note again:

FIND NOTHING.

The crew had found empty, quiet, blank and hidden things—but no nothing.

Inside this careful story check: there is no nothing.

Page 27: THE FIRST STAR BLINKS

On the way to the library, the first star blinked.

Tavi asked, “What did Sol call empty?”

Think back. Do not turn yet.

The first clue is waiting somewhere in this room.

Page 28: YOU REMEMBERED

The BOX was empty.

The TOY was outside.

You brought an earlier clue back to mind.

Remember means to bring an earlier clue back to mind.

Page 29: FILE THE MAP

Mira slid the map into the library stand.

“We found no nothing,” she said, “and we never had to pretend.”

Every star now had a story the crew could retell.

Page 30: TAVI’S CAREFUL-SPOTTER TRICK

1. Name what is here.

2. Say what changed.

3. Never add a secret answer.

Can you use the trick on one clue?

Tavi may ask how you looked. Tavi never gives away a new answer.

Page 31: A NEW SOUND

The Star Door chimed.

A blue-and-gold parcel rolled into the library.

Sol grinned. “Another mystery?”

The first adventure is complete. The next one is only beginning.
