Page 3: THE PARCEL FROM THE RAMP

The parcel from the end of the first mystery was waiting in the library. It had slid down the wall ramp and stopped beside the five-star map.

The label showed three names: MIA, SOL AND TAVI.

The friends gathered around their parcel to see what was inside.

Page 4: THE MOON LANTERN

Mia opened the parcel. Inside was a round Moon Lantern and a card.

The card said: PLEASE TAKE THIS LANTERN TO THE BALCONY BEFORE THE FIRST EVENING STAR.

The picture on the card showed where to take it.

Page 5: IT WILL NOT FIT

The friends carried the lantern towards the balcony. A small door blocked the way. The lantern was too wide to go through the door.

Pax came over. “Hello! I’m Pax. I know how to help.”

The team’s question: How can the whole lantern get through the small door?

Page 6: PAX HAS A PLAN

“We can take the lantern apart,” said Pax. “Then carry every part through the door. Then put the whole lantern together again.”

First, they looked closely at the lantern.

Their plan: check the whole, count every part, carry every part, then rebuild the whole.

Page 7: TRY IT · FIND THE WHOLE LANTERN

Look at all three pictures.

One lantern has a gap. One has every part. One has an extra shape.

Which one is the whole lantern?

Choose before you turn the page.

Page 8: THE WHOLE LANTERN

WHOLE LANTERN.

NO GAP. NO EXTRA PIECE.

“This is the whole lantern,” said Pax. “Every part of this lantern is here.”

Whole means every part is here. No part is missing, and no extra part has been added. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: WHOLELIGHT

Page 9: FOUR CARRYING SPOTS

Pax placed four glowing spots on the floor. Each spot stood for one lantern part.

“We must check every spot,” said Mia. “Then no part will be left behind.”

The four spots help the team keep track of every part.

Page 10: TRY IT · CHECK EVERY SPOT

Point to the four glowing spots, one after another.

Touch each spot once. Do not miss one. Do not touch one twice.

This is how the team will keep track of all four lantern parts.

Page 11: EVERY SPOT, ONCE

ONE · TWO · THREE · FOUR.

Each spot was touched once. None was missed. None was touched twice.

The team can use the same check when they carry the four lantern parts. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: ONCEEACH

Page 12: SOL TRIES A BOUNCY WAY

Sol checked the spots: “One, two, three, three, four!”

His count did not match the four spots. What did Sol do?

Look at the footprints on every spot before you turn.

Page 13: LOOK AT SOL’S FIRST TRY

SPOT THREE WAS TOUCHED TWICE.

“The footprints show us what happened,” said Tavi.

Sol tried again and touched every spot once. Now his count matched the four parts. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: ONCEAROUND

Page 14: CHOOSE A SAFE PLAN

Now the team was ready to choose how to take the lantern apart.

Pax showed three plans. “We need four pieces that fill the whole circle. They must not lie on top of each other.”

The right plan will make four clear parts to carry through the small door.

Page 15: TRY IT · FILL THE CIRCLE

Which picture shows four same-size pieces that fill the circle?

Check that there is no gap and no piece is over another piece.

Point to one picture before you turn.

Page 16: LOOK AGAIN

Trace around each circle. Does a piece leave a gap?

Look at the pieces. Is one over another?

Are all four pieces the same size?

Check each picture before you choose.

Page 17: THE FOUR-PART PLAN

NO GAP.

NO PIECE OVER ANOTHER.

FOUR PIECES THE SAME SIZE.

“This plan will work,” said Pax.

Together, the four parts fill the same whole circle. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: FAIRFIT

Page 18: THE LANTERN COMES APART

Pax used the four-part plan. The lantern came apart into four pieces.

The four parts landed in four clear spaces. Each part was small enough to fit through the door.

The team can see all four lantern parts before they move them.

Page 19: TRY IT · CARRY EVERY PART

Move all four lantern parts through the small door.

Use each carrying spot once. Leave no lantern part behind.

Count the parts as they go through: one, two, three, four.

Page 20: ALL FOUR MADE IT THROUGH

ONE · TWO · THREE · FOUR.

All four lantern parts were on the other side. No part was left behind.

Next, the team must put the four parts back together. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: PARTPAIR

Page 21: CHECK THE PARTS

Before rebuilding the lantern, Pax checked the pieces against the plan.

Which pair has two pieces that are the same size: Pair A or Pair B?

Look at both pieces in each pair before you turn.

Page 22: EQUAL IN SIZE

PAIR A: EQUAL IN SIZE.

PAIR B: NOT EQUAL IN SIZE.

“Both pieces in Pair A are the same size,” said Pax. “They are equal in size.”

One piece in Pair B is bigger than the other.

Page 23: REBUILD THE LANTERN

On the other side of the small door, Sol began to rebuild the lantern. He filled three of its four spaces.

“Is the lantern whole yet?” he asked.

Look at all four spaces. Decide before you turn.

Page 24: ONE PIECE IS MISSING

ONE SPACE IS EMPTY.

THE LANTERN IS NOT WHOLE YET.

“We still need one more piece,” said Mia.

The team put in the fourth piece. Now all four spaces were filled.

Page 25: AN EXTRA PIECE

A loose triangle from Pax’s work table slid onto the floor and stopped beside the whole lantern.

“The lantern is already whole,” said Tavi. “This triangle does not belong to it.”

The triangle is extra. It is not part of the lantern. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: GAPCHECK

Page 26: PAX’S PRACTICE PICTURE

Before the team carried the whole lantern away, Pax showed one practice picture.

In the picture, Pax held two lantern pieces. Two more pieces waited on the tray.

How many pieces is Pax holding?

How many pieces make the whole lantern?

Look at Pax’s hands and all four pieces. Say both numbers before you turn.

Page 27: THE PICTURE HAS TWO NUMBERS

PAX IS HOLDING TWO PIECES.

FOUR PIECES MAKE THE WHOLE LANTERN.

“Two tells us what I am holding in the picture. Four tells us how many make the whole lantern,” said Pax.

Say both numbers again. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: HELDWHOLE

Page 28: COUNT THE PARTS TOGETHER

At the balcony, the four lantern parts were still separate. Pax put one part in each counting space.

1 PART + 1 PART + 1 PART + 1 PART = ? PARTS

Is the total 3, 4 or 5?

Touch each lantern part once. Then choose the total that names every visible part.

Page 29: FOUR PARTS ALTOGETHER

1 PART + 1 PART + 1 PART + 1 PART = 4 PARTS

The PLUS signs join the separate counted parts.

The EQUALS sign says both sides count the same four parts.

Nothing was lost, repeated or added. Both sides name the same total.

Page 30: JOIN THE FOUR PARTS

The equation counted FOUR SEPARATE PARTS.

Now the friends fitted those four parts together. The same Moon Lantern was ONE WHOLE again.

4 PARTS → 1 WHOLE LANTERN. This arrow shows rebuilding. It is not an equals sign.

Page 31: WHAT YOU DISCOVERED

1. Count every visible part once.

2. 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 4 PARTS.

3. PLUS joins separate counts. EQUALS says both sides have the same total.

4. Those 4 parts can fit together to rebuild 1 whole lantern.

Say both ideas: four parts altogether; those four parts rebuild one whole.

Page 32: THE FIRST STAR APPEARS

The lantern lit up as the first evening star appeared.

A blue moon picture shone. Then a gold sun picture shone. Then the blue moon came back.
