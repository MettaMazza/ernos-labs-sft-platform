Page 3: THE NEW PARCEL

Mira had just put the first adventure’s map away.

CHIME! The little wall door opened. The parcel from their last adventure rolled down the ramp.

Mira, Sol and Tavi knew it was their next mystery.

Page 4: ONE DELIVERY

Mira untied the ribbon and opened the parcel. Inside was a round Moon Lantern.

There was a card too. It said: TAKE THE MOON LANTERN TO THE BALCONY BEFORE THE FIRST EVENING STAR.

The card showed the lantern and the balcony.

Page 5: MEET PAX

The friends carried the lantern to the workshop. It was too wide to go through the door.

Pax came out. “Hello! I’m Pax. I help things fit together. I can help you carry the lantern through.”

Pax is the new friend in this adventure.

Page 6: THE ROUND TRAY

Pax put the lantern in a round tray. It fit.

Three pictures of the lantern appeared beside the door.

The door will open when the friends choose the whole lantern.

Page 7: TRY IT · FIND THE WHOLE LANTERN

Look at all three pictures.

One lantern has a gap. One has every part. One has an extra shape.

Which one is the whole lantern?

Choose before you turn the page.

Page 8: THE WHOLE LANTERN

WHOLE LANTERN.

NO GAP. NO EXTRA PIECE.

“This is the whole lantern,” said Pax. “Every part of this lantern is here.”

Whole means every part is here. No part is missing, and no extra part has been added. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: WHOLELIGHT

Page 9: THE COUNT BRIDGE

The door opened. Behind it was a short bridge made from four glowing floor tiles.

A sign said: TOUCH EVERY LIGHT ONCE.

Mira pointed from START to FINISH. They could see all four lights.

Page 10: TRY IT · TOUCH EACH LIGHT

Point to the four glowing tiles from START to FINISH.

Touch each tile once. Do not skip one or touch one twice.

Keep your finger moving from one tile to the next.

Page 11: A CAREFUL COUNT

ONE · TWO · THREE · FOUR.

Each light was touched once. No light was missed or touched twice.

To count the lights, touch each one once. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: ONCEEACH

Page 12: SOL TRIES A BOUNCY WAY

Sol bounced across: “One, two, three, three, four!”

The bridge did not open. What did Sol do?

Look at the footprints on every tile before you turn.

Page 13: LOOK AT SOL’S FIRST TRY

TILE THREE WAS TOUCHED TWICE.

“The footprints show us what happened,” said Tavi.

Sol tried again and touched every tile once. The bridge opened. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: ONCEAROUND

Page 14: THE PIECE DOOR

The bridge led to another narrow door. Beside it was a tray with four empty spaces.

“The lantern is still too wide,” said Pax. “We can take it apart, carry the pieces through and put it back together.”

Pax showed them three pictures of ways to take the lantern apart.

Page 15: TRY IT · FILL THE CIRCLE

Which picture shows four same-size pieces that fill the circle?

Check that there is no gap and no piece is over another piece.

Point to one picture before you turn.

Page 16: LOOK AGAIN

Trace around each circle. Does a piece leave a gap?

Look at the pieces. Is one over another?

Are all four pieces the same size?

Check each picture before you choose.

Page 17: THE PIECE DOOR OPENS

NO GAP.

NO PIECE OVER ANOTHER.

FOUR PIECES THE SAME SIZE.

The door opened.

These four pieces fill the circle. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: FAIRFIT

Page 18: FOUR SPACES ON THE TRAY

Pax pressed a button. CLICK! The lantern came apart into the four pieces from the picture.

Each piece landed in one space on the tray. Now the friends could carry the pieces through the door.

All four pieces are easy to see on the tray.

Page 19: TRY IT · REBUILD

Move every lantern piece from the tray into the round outline.

Use every piece once. Leave none behind.

Which edges fit together? Try before you turn.

Page 20: THE LANTERN IS WHOLE AGAIN

ALL FOUR PARTS FIT TOGETHER.

THE MOON LANTERN IS WHOLE AGAIN.

Every piece was back in the lantern, so the tray was empty.

Every lantern piece was used once. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: PARTPAIR

Page 21: THE MATCH TABLE

The next door opened into a room with a long table. Two pairs of pieces lay on it.

Which pair has two pieces that are the same size: Pair A or Pair B?

Look at both pieces in each pair before you turn.

Page 22: EQUAL IN SIZE

PAIR A: EQUAL IN SIZE.

PAIR B: NOT EQUAL IN SIZE.

“Both pieces in Pair A are the same size,” said Pax. “They are equal in size.”

One piece in Pair B is bigger than the other.

Page 23: IS THE LANTERN WHOLE?

At the next door, Sol began to put the lantern together. He filled three of its four spaces.

“Is the lantern whole now?” he asked.

Look at all four spaces. Decide before you turn.

Page 24: ONE PIECE IS MISSING

ONE SPACE IS EMPTY.

THE LANTERN IS NOT WHOLE YET.

“We still need one more piece,” said Mira.

The team put in the fourth piece. Now all four spaces were filled.

Page 25: AN EXTRA PIECE

A triangle slid across the floor and stopped beside the whole lantern.

“The lantern is already whole,” said Tavi. “This triangle does not belong to it.”

The triangle is extra. It is not part of the lantern. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: GAPCHECK

Page 26: HOW MANY IS PAX HOLDING?

Pax lifted some pieces from the lantern tray.

How many pieces is Pax holding?

How many pieces make the whole lantern?

Look at Pax’s hands and the whole tray. Say both numbers before you turn.

Page 27: TWO NUMBERS

PAX IS HOLDING TWO PIECES.

FOUR PIECES MAKE THE WHOLE LANTERN.

“Two tells us what I am holding. Four tells us how many make the whole lantern,” said Pax.

Say both numbers again. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: HELDWHOLE

Page 28: REMEMBER SOL’S FIRST TRY

Before the friends put the lantern together one last time, Tavi asked, “What happened when Sol first crossed the bridge?”

A. He missed one tile.

B. He touched one tile twice.

C. He touched every tile once.

Think back to Sol’s footprints. Pax waits without giving the answer.

Page 29: SOL TOUCHED ONE TILE TWICE

SOL TOUCHED ONE TILE TWICE.

The friends kept a picture of his first try so they could see what changed. Then Sol crossed again and touched every tile once.

The two pictures show the first try and the new try side by side.

Page 30: THE MOON LANTERN SHINES

Every lantern piece went back into its place.

The Moon Lantern was whole again. Together, the friends carried it through the last door and onto the balcony.

Before they left, they checked that every piece was in the lantern. The tray was empty.

Page 31: PAX’S THREE CHECKS

1. CHECK THE WHOLE: no gap and no piece that does not belong.

2. CHECK THE COUNT: touch each thing once.

3. CHECK THE PIECES: use every piece, keep them apart and compare their sizes.

Can you find a page that shows each check?

Page 32: THE FIRST STAR APPEARS

The lantern lit up as the first evening star appeared.

Two colours began to repeat across its round face: blue, gold, blue, gold.
