# E02 2.0.0 illustration and accessibility audit

Status: complete for GitHub review; awaiting Maria Smith’s approval
Book: *One Whole, Many Parts — The Moon Lantern Workshop*
Audience: ages 3–5 with an adult or independent picture browsing

## Visual system

- Story settings and characters use original generated 3D illustration.
- Mia, Sol and Tavi remain the permanent team on every relevant story scene.
- Pax is the only new guest and is introduced after the little-door problem.
- Repeated learning objects use stable OpenMoji 16.0 assets rather than generated story art.
- The parcel, Moon Lantern, moon, sun, door, star and blank space retain one stable design.
- The four lantern quarters are mechanical crops from the same OpenMoji lantern. They are not AI-generated substitutes.
- Every separate quarter appears in its own rounded picture card so adjacent quarters cannot be mistaken for a rebuilt whole.
- The complete lantern is shown before separation and after reassembly.

## Page-by-page visual inspection

Every one of the 32 student pages was rendered at 144 dpi and inspected at full resolution.

- Pages 1–2: cover/title identity, author, age, review and licences are clear.
- Pages 3–7: E01 parcel continuity, Moon Lantern reveal, balcony request, size obstacle and Pax introduction are visible in causal order.
- Pages 8–9: the missing, complete and extra choices are distinct; the answer follows the attempt.
- Pages 10–13: two visible seam lines produce four separate, clearly bordered quarter cards.
- Pages 14–15: one gold trail visits four separate cards; numbered reveal remains legible.
- Pages 16–18: four distinct cards start left, four paths pass through the door, and the same four cards finish on Pax’s tray.
- Pages 19–21: Pair A contains equal outer card sizes; Pair B visibly differs; the answer highlight does not rely on colour alone.
- Pages 22–23: one bottom-right place is visibly missing, answer choices are distinct, and the completed frame has no gap or overlap.
- Pages 24–26: two cards remain visible on Mia’s tray and two on Pax’s tray during the question and exact equation.
- Pages 27–29: four distinct cards surround one empty frame, trace lines identify matching positions, and the stable whole emoji returns.
- Pages 30–32: the direct lesson, closed goodbye and moon–sun–moon teaser fit without clipping or unresolved story action.

No child page contains a game menu, dashboard, digital control, device instruction or game-screen layout.

## Text and interaction inspection

- All child sentences use short familiar words where exact meaning is preserved.
- *Seam* is explained as a line where the lantern can open before the activity uses the term.
- *Whole*, *part*, *plus* and *equals* follow visible experiences.
- All eight activities are possible on paper through pointing, tracing, counting, speaking or a page turn.
- Each answer follows an attempt and never appears on the same page as its question.
- The final narrator page tells the child what they did, what it showed and why keeping an exact count matters.

## Accessible edition

- The semantic HTML has one `main` landmark and 32 ordered page sections.
- Every page has one figure with a complete `aria-label` picture description.
- Text can be resized and read independently from the PDF layout.
- The PDF has selectable text and accurate metadata but is not claimed to be a fully tagged PDF.
- Meaning is carried by words, shape, size, position and outline, never by colour alone.
- Pointing, gaze, signing, speech and communication aids are all valid response modes.
