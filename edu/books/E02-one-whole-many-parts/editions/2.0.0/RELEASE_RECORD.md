# E02 review 2.0.0

Prepared: 1 August 2026
Status: complete GitHub review edition; not finally approved

This release replaces the former screen-like companion-book layout with a complete 32-page paper-native picture book for ages 3–5.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the story around one clear problem: the complete Moon Lantern is too wide for the little door.
- Preserved the visible parcel that arrived at the end of E01.
- Kept Mia, Sol and Tavi as the permanent trio and introduced Pax as the sole E02 guest.
- Added eight actual paper activities with answers after the child’s attempt.
- Introduced *whole*, *part*, *seam*, *plus* and *equals* only after visible experience.
- Kept the exact equations `2 parts + 2 parts = 4 parts` and `1 part + 1 part + 1 part + 1 part = 4 parts` distinct from the rebuilding statement `Four parts make 1 whole lantern`.
- Replaced generated repeated objects with stable OpenMoji assets.
- Mechanically derived all four lantern-quarter cards from the same stable lantern emoji.
- Added a complete adult guide, semantic HTML edition, exact claim map and full visual/accessibility audit.
