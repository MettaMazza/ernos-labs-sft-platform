five-page adult guide, semantic HTML, exact claim-and-receipt map, original
illustrations and the complete Level Two companion adventure.

using Maria Smith's local Kokoro weights.

whenever it keeps the meaning exact. Technical scope and SFT receipt details are
kept in the adult guide and manifests.

`publications/education` until Maria Smith explicitly approves this exact book.

## Story-clarity revision - 31 July 2026

The review story now holds one child-facing question from the first obstacle to
the ending: how can the whole lantern get through the small door? Pax gives the
team a four-step plan - check the whole, take it apart, carry every part, and
build the same whole again. The former chain of unexplained doors and rooms has
been removed. Carrying spots now keep track of the actual lantern parts, and
the final summary retells the same journey rather than listing disconnected
terms.

The revised student and adult PDFs were rendered and inspected page by page.
The matching Level Two captions were regenerated with the local Kokoro weights,
and all nine stages were replayed from start to finish at phone size. The opening
mission and responsive bounds were also checked at tablet and desktop sizes.
