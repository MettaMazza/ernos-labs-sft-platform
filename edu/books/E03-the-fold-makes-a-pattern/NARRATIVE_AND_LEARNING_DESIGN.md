# E03 narrative and learning design

Working version: 0.1.0  
Status: canonical design for review production

## Identity

- Book: **E03 — The Fold Makes a Pattern**
- Story subtitle: **The Turning-Light Trail**
- Stage: Early Years / Foundation
- Permanent leads: Mira, Sol and Tavi
- One new guest: Vee, the pattern-path keeper
- Prior knowledge: E01's bounded habit of naming what is presented and E02's
  habit of retaining one whole, every exact part and every visible attempt

## Why E03 is next

The maintained master syllabus fixes E03 as the third of four Early Years
books. The Foundation dependency roadmap places the minimal Fold and its two
held labels after the One, positive finite count and exact positive parts taught
in E02. E03 therefore begins with those admitted dependencies and does not
select a topic from an external curriculum.

The scientific content is bounded to:

- `SFT-FOUNDATION-FOLD-001`: the minimal parameter-free Fold has two complete,
  disjoint, equal fibres with two distinct held labels and a return relation;
- `SFT-FOUNDATION-FOLD-DYNAMICS-001`: exact Fold transition retains two fibre
  roles and contains the first nonstatic turn-and-return recurrence.

The child activities are educational translations and demonstrations. They are
not independent proofs and do not claim that a paper tile or lamp is the exact
Fold object.

## One caused story journey

E02 ends when the rebuilt Moon Lantern begins to shine blue, gold, blue, gold.
E03 opens at that exact moment. The alternating light should lead from the
balcony to the Sunrise Arch, but the trail stops at a turning gate. Vee arrives
from the path station and explains the concrete mission:

> **Restore the turning-light trail before sunrise.**

The child holds this one purpose through the complete book. Every scene changes
the same trail rather than presenting an unrelated pattern quiz:

1. the Moon Lantern supplies the repeating blue-and-gold light;
2. the dark trail creates the problem and Vee joins the permanent trio;
3. one two-sided trail tile gives the child two visible roles to retain;
4. the first gate turns blue to gold;
5. the second gate turns gold back to blue;
6. the child copies and predicts the same turn-and-return rule;
7. a preserved wrong tile shows exactly where the rule broke;
8. an over/under bridge transfers the two-role rule without changing it;
9. the repaired trail reaches the Sunrise Arch; and
10. a delayed recall and new star/leaf example test whether the child can use
    the rule without Vee exposing the answer.

Nothing opens, moves or lights without a shown cause. A character may ask the
child to inspect a move, but no character announces a new answer before the
child attempts it.

## Experience-before-vocabulary ladder

| Experience first | Name after the reveal | Child-facing meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Hold one tile and inspect its blue face and gold face | two sides / two roles | the same tile has two different sides we can keep apart |
| Turn the tile once and see a different face | turn | one move changes which named side is showing |
| Turn it again and see the first face | return | the next lawful move brings the first side back |
| Copy blue, gold, blue, gold | pattern | a rule that tells us what move comes next |
| Keep blue, gold, blue, blue visible | broken rule | one shown move does not follow the declared rule |
| Use over/under and star/leaf with the same alternation | role | the names can change while the two-role turn-and-return rule stays declared |

The exact phrases *held label*, *fibre*, *phase translation*, *positive rational
part* and *two-cycle* remain in the adult guide. They are not unexplained child
vocabulary.

## Book and Level Three pairing

| Story stage | Book pages | Level Three mini-game | Optional code |
|---|---:|---|---|
| The lantern starts the trail | 3–5 | catch the two lights and reveal the stopped route | `TRAILLIGHT` |
| Keep the two sides distinct | 6–8 | inspect and label both faces of one turning tile | `TWOSIDES` |
| One turn, then return | 9–14 | turn the tile through both visible states | `TURNBACK` |
| Copy and predict | 15–17 | continue the declared blue/gold recurrence | `NEXTLIGHT` |
| Preserve and repair the break | 18–21 | find the first unlawful move, keep it visible and repair it | `KEEPTHETRY` |
| Over and under transfer | 22–24 | move through two named route roles in lawful order | `OVERUNDER` |
| Choose the complete route | 25–27 | compare one lawful trace with incomplete and broken traces | `RIGHTROUTE` |
| Recall and new-role transfer | 28–32 | recall the earlier transition, then apply it to star/leaf roles | `NEWROLES` |

Each game stage will contain a distinct, replayable mini-game with direct input,
visible progress, sound feedback, a wrong path and a book-page bridge. Book
codes unlock only harmless story variations or previews.

## Vee continuity record

Vee is curious, wears large workshop goggles and keeps the turning-light path.
Vee's memorable method is:

1. name both roles;
2. show the current role;
3. make one declared move;
4. compare the result with the rule; and
5. keep a wrong move visible so it can be repaired.

Vee never tells the child which tile, move or route is correct before an
attempt. In a later book Vee may return only when a declared transition,
recurrence or broken-rule trace is relevant. Vee may ask “What was showing?” or
“What does one move do?” but cannot state a new result.

## Exact exclusions

E03 does not introduce or rely on:

- conventional binary digits, bit axioms or Boolean authority;
- positive/negative numerical labels;
- semantic numerical zero;
- floating-point equality or inexact orbit tests;
- completed infinity or an ungenerated infinite pattern;
- a claim that every colourful alternation is an SFT Fold;
- a physical mechanism as proof of the admitted Fold law;
- hand-picked rules without a stated transition; or
- a conventional curriculum, publisher or game as scientific authority.

## Review gates before Level Three production

- Every page must retain the one Sunrise Arch mission and a visible cause.
- Every challenge must precede its labelled reveal.
- Every new word must follow the experience that gives it meaning.
- Child-facing words and reveal labels must sit above recognisable imagery.
- The blue/gold roles must also have shape or name cues so colour is never the
  only distinction.
- Alt descriptions must say which role is visible, which move happened and
  where any broken move appears.
- The adult guide must map every result to the exact receipt-backed boundary.
