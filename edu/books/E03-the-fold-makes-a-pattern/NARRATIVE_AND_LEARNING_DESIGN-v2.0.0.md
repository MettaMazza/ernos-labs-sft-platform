# E03 narrative and learning design — review 2.0.0

## One caused journey

Book Two ends with the rebuilt Moon Lantern showing moon, sun, moon and one blank place. Book Three begins at that exact moment. The lights run into the garden, stop at a dark stone, and leave the Sunrise Arch asleep. Vee, the sole new guest, asks Mia, Sol, Tavi and the child to discover the trail rule and mend the path before morning.

Every page changes or checks the same trail. No unrelated quiz interrupts the journey.

## Experience before vocabulary

1. The child sees two sides of one tile before meeting *side*.
2. The child turns a hand or card before meeting *turn*.
3. The second turn brings the first picture back before meeting *return*.
4. The child copies and predicts moon–sun before meeting *pattern*.
5. The child keeps Sol’s first try visible before meeting *break* and *repair*.
6. The same two-role rule transfers to over/under and star/leaf before *role* is discussed in the adult guide.

Child-facing Fold phrase: **turn to the other side, then return to the first**.

## Paper-native activity loop

Each activity is complete on the page and needs no screen, account or companion game. A child may point, speak, sign, gaze, trace, turn a hand, or direct an adult.

## Stable item contract

Moon, sun, star, leaf, blank place and Moon Lantern are versioned OpenMoji assets. Each recurrence of an item uses the same asset. Scene art and character sprites supply only setting, emotion and one-off story action. Generated decorative marks are not counted, matched or treated as lesson evidence.

## Exact SFT boundary

- `SFT-FOUNDATION-FOLD-001`: two complete, disjoint, equal fibres with distinct held labels and a complete return relation.
- `SFT-FOUNDATION-FOLD-DYNAMICS-001`: an exact declared transition that retains the two roles and supplies the first nonstatic turn-and-return recurrence.

The tile, hand turn, path and emoji rows are educational translations. They are not proofs and do not make external models authoritative.

Excluded: binary digits, signed labels, semantic zero, floating equality, completed infinity, physical mechanism as proof, and the claim that every colourful alternation is an SFT Fold.

## Ending

The narrator speaks directly to the child and states what they did, what the Fold rule meant in the story, how the repeated move made a pattern, and why a clear rule helps prediction and repair. Vee’s garden problem closes. Tavi’s question about checking work opens E04 without supplying its answer.
