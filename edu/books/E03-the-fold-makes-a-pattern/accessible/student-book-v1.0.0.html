Page 3: THE LANTERN STARTS TO SHINE

The first evening star appeared. The same Moon Lantern that the friends had rebuilt began to shine.

A BLUE MOON picture lit up. Then a GOLD SUN picture lit up. Then the blue moon came back.

Mia, Sol and Tavi watched the two pictures take turns.

Page 4: THE LIGHT MAKES A TRAIL

The moon and sun lights jumped from the balcony onto the garden path. They travelled towards the Sunrise Arch.

Then the next stone stayed dark. The light could go no farther.

The friends could see the lit stones, the first dark stone, and the arch at the far end of the garden.

Page 5: MEET VEE

Vee hurried over from the little path hut.

“Hello! I’m Vee. I look after this garden path,” Vee said. “The Sunrise Arch lights the way to the garden gate each morning, but the path has stopped. Will you help me fix it before sunrise?”

The friends have one clear job: help the light reach the Sunrise Arch. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: TRAILLIGHT

Page 6: ONE TILE, TWO SIDES

Vee picked up one trail tile.

One side showed a BLUE MOON. The other side showed a GOLD SUN.

Both sides belong to the same tile.

Page 7: TRY IT · CHECK BOTH SIDES

Look at the tile in Vee’s hands.

Which side is showing now?

Point to the matching picture before you turn.

Both answer pictures stay where you can see them. Choose before you turn.

Page 8: THE BLUE MOON SIDE IS SHOWING

BLUE MOON SIDE SHOWING.

GOLD SUN SIDE UNDERNEATH.

The two side names help us keep the sides apart.

A side name tells us which face we are looking at. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: TWOSIDES

Page 9: THE FIRST TURNING GATE

The first gate was stuck. Vee put the tile inside with its BLUE MOON side facing up.

Sol pulled the curved handle. The gate turned the tile over one time.

Watch what happens before naming the side that comes out.

Page 10: TRY IT · ONE TURN

Put one hand flat like the tile.

Turn your hand over one time.

Which side of Vee’s tile will show after one turn?

Choose the blue moon or the gold sun before you turn the page.

Page 11: ONE TURN CHANGES THE SIDE

BLUE MOON turned to GOLD SUN.

One turn changed which side was showing.

The blue moon side still belongs to the tile. It is now underneath.

Page 12: TRY IT · TURN AGAIN

The GOLD SUN side is showing now.

The next gate will turn the tile one more time.

Which side will show next?

Use your hand again. Choose before you turn the page.

Page 13: THE BLUE MOON SIDE RETURNS

GOLD SUN turned to BLUE MOON.

The first side came back.

Return means the earlier side is showing again.

Page 14: TURN, THEN RETURN

BLUE MOON → GOLD SUN.

GOLD SUN → BLUE MOON.

One turn changes the side. The next turn brings the first side back.

Say the moves with Mia: turn, then return. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: TURNBACK

Page 15: COPY THE LIGHTS

Point as the lights take turns:

BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN · BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN

Go slowly from left to right. Each light follows the same turn-and-return rule.

Page 16: TRY IT · WHAT COMES NEXT?

BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN · BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN · ?

Which light follows the rule?

Choose before you turn. Vee keeps both choices visible.

Page 17: BLUE MOON COMES NEXT

BLUE MOON comes next.

A PATTERN is a rule that tells us what move comes next.

This pattern follows the turn-and-return rule the friends just used. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: NEXTLIGHT

Page 18: THE TRAIL STARTS AGAIN

The repaired gate lit the next garden stones. Sol hurried ahead and turned one tile the wrong way. Two blue moons met, and the light stopped again.

The friends left Sol’s whole first try in place so they could see what happened.

Page 19: TRY IT · FIND THE BREAK

BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN · BLUE MOON · BLUE MOON

Point to the first tile that does not follow the rule.

Start at the left and check one move at a time before you turn.

Page 20: THE FOURTH TILE BREAKS THE RULE

The fourth tile should show GOLD SUN.

Sol’s BLUE MOON tile stays in the picture so we can see what happened.

A broken rule means one shown move does not follow the declared rule.

Page 21: KEEP THE TRY, REPAIR THE TRAIL

Sol turned the fourth tile to GOLD SUN. The light moved forward.

“We kept the first try,” said Tavi. “Then we repaired the move.”

The trail now reads blue moon, gold sun, blue moon, gold sun. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: KEEPTHETRY

Page 22: THE BRIDGE HAS TWO WAYS

The light reached a bridge.

One lamp travelled OVER the arch. The next lamp travelled UNDER the arch.

Over and under are new names. The path still changes from one to the other after every move.

Page 23: TRY IT · CHOOSE THE NEXT WAY

OVER · UNDER · OVER · ?

Which way comes next if over and under keep taking turns?

Choose over or under before you turn the page.

Page 24: UNDER COMES NEXT

OVER · UNDER · OVER · UNDER

The pictures and names changed, but the take-turns rule stayed the same.

The light can now cross the whole bridge. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: OVERUNDER

Page 25: THREE ROUTES TO THE ARCH

Three short light trails waited at the last gate.

One stopped early. One broke the rule. One followed the rule all the way.

The team needs a route that reaches the arch and takes turns all the way.

Page 26: TRY IT · CHOOSE THE WHOLE ROUTE

Look at Route A, Route B and Route C.

Which route reaches the Sunrise Arch and follows every turn?

Point to one route before you turn the page.

Page 27: ROUTE C REACHES THE ARCH

ROUTE C follows every turn and reaches the Sunrise Arch.

The light travelled across the repaired trail.

The light can now travel across the whole garden. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: RIGHTROUTE

Page 28: REMEMBER THE FIRST GATE

At the first gate, BLUE MOON went in. The gate turned the tile once.

Which side came out?

Think back to the first gate. Choose the picture you remember.

Page 29: GOLD SUN CAME OUT

BLUE MOON turned to GOLD SUN.

You brought an earlier move back to mind.

The before and after pictures show the same one-turn change again.

Page 30: TRY THE RULE WITH NEW PICTURES

STAR · LEAF · STAR · ?

The pictures are new. The take-turns rule stays the same. Which picture comes next?

Choose star or leaf before you turn.

Page 31: LEAF COMES NEXT

STAR · LEAF · STAR · LEAF

The pictures changed. The take-turns rule stayed the same. The Sunrise Arch lit up!

Tell the journey: name both pictures, make one move, check what happened, and repair the first move that broke the rule. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: NEWROLES

Page 32: THE MORNING LIGHT ARRIVES

The path was ready before sunrise. The arch lit the way to the garden gate. Vee kept Sol’s first-try picture beside the repaired map.

Tavi looked at both pictures. “Next time, can a friend check our work too?”
