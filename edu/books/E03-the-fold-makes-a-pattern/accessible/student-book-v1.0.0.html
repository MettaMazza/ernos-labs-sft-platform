Page 3: LAST TIME: ONE WHOLE, FOUR PARTS

Last time, Mia, Sol and Tavi helped Pax take the Moon Lantern apart. They counted all four parts and put the same whole lantern back together.

As the first evening star appeared, the lantern began to glow: BLUE MOON, GOLD SUN, BLUE MOON.

You already know that all four parts can rebuild one whole. Now watch what the two lights do.

Page 4: A NEW LIGHT TRAIL

The lights skipped from the balcony onto the garden path: BLUE MOON, GOLD SUN, BLUE MOON. Then the next stone stayed dark.

On this adventure, you will turn a tile, see what comes back, and discover how the lights take turns again and again.

Your mission is clear: help the light reach the Sunrise Arch before morning.

Page 5: MEET VEE

Vee hurried over from the small hut beside the path.

“Hello! I’m Vee. I care for this path. The Sunrise Arch must shine before morning, but the light has stopped. Will you help me find out why and fix it?”

The team knows where it is going and why: fix this one trail before morning. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: TRAILLIGHT

Page 6: ONE TILE, TWO SIDES

Vee lifted one trail tile and showed it slowly to the friends.

Mia saw a BLUE MOON on one side. Sol saw a GOLD SUN on the other. It was one tile with two different sides.

A side is one flat face of an object. This helps you notice that one thing can have two sides.

Page 7: TRY IT · CHECK BOTH SIDES

Vee held the tile still. Only one side faced up.

Which picture can you see now: the BLUE MOON or the GOLD SUN?

Point before Vee turns the tile.

You are learning to name what is showing now, rather than guess about the side you cannot see.

Page 8: THE BLUE MOON SIDE IS SHOWING

BLUE MOON is showing. GOLD SUN is underneath, where we cannot see it from above.

Showing means we can see it now. Underneath means it is on the lower side.

Both pictures still belong to the same tile. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: TWOSIDES

Page 9: THE FIRST TURNING GATE

Mia carried the tile to the first gate. She put it in with the BLUE MOON facing up.

Sol pulled the handle once. The gate rolled the tile all the way over.

Once means one complete turn. Watch the movement before you name the side that comes out.

Page 10: TRY IT · ONE TURN

Hold one hand flat with your palm facing up. Turn it over once.

Vee’s tile began with BLUE MOON facing up. Which side will face up after the same turn?

Do the movement before answering. It helps you feel what one turn changes.

Page 11: ONE TURN CHANGES THE SIDE

GOLD SUN now faces up. It is still the same tile.

Turning the tile brought its other side into view, where we can see it.

One turn changed which side was showing. BLUE MOON is still there underneath.

Page 12: TRY IT · TURN AGAIN

GOLD SUN faces up now. The second gate will roll the same tile over once more.

Turn your hand again. Which side will face up next?

Use what you discovered about one turn. Choose before you turn the page.

Page 13: THE BLUE MOON SIDE RETURNS

BLUE MOON faces up again. It was showing before, went underneath, and came back after the second turn.

When something from earlier comes back, we call it a RETURN.

Return means the earlier side is showing again.

Page 14: THE FOLD: TURN, THEN RETURN

START: BLUE MOON is showing.

ONE TURN: GOLD SUN is showing.

NEXT TURN: BLUE MOON returns.

In this trail story, the FOLD is the two-part turn-and-return rule.

Say the journey with Mia: blue moon, gold sun, blue moon returns. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: TURNBACK

Page 15: COPY THE LIGHTS

Vee placed four tiles in a row. Point from left to right and say each picture:

BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN · BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN

The same order starts again. Repeat means to do the same order another time.

Page 16: TRY IT · WHAT COMES NEXT?

BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN · BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN · ?

The same order is repeating. Which light should come next?

Use the repeated order to work it out. Choose before you turn the page.

Page 17: BLUE MOON COMES NEXT

BLUE MOON comes next. The order BLUE MOON, then GOLD SUN, keeps repeating.

A PATTERN is an order that repeats. Here, repeating the Fold’s turn-and-return rule makes the pattern.

The rule is: blue moon, then gold sun, then begin the same order again. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: NEXTLIGHT

Page 18: THE TRAIL STARTS AGAIN

The repaired gate sent the light farther along the path. Sol placed the fourth tile with its BLUE MOON facing up. This put two BLUE MOON tiles together, and the light stopped again.

“Let’s keep my first try where we can see it,” Sol said.

The team does not hide the try. Keeping it visible will help them find what needs to change.

Page 19: TRY IT · FIND THE BREAK

BLUE MOON · GOLD SUN · BLUE MOON · BLUE MOON

Start at the left. Check one move at a time. Which tile is the first one that does not follow the BLUE MOON, GOLD SUN order?

You are finding the exact place where the repeated order stops working. Point before you turn the page.

Page 20: THE FOURTH TILE BREAKS THE RULE

The fourth tile is the first break. After BLUE MOON, the rule says GOLD SUN should come next. Instead, another BLUE MOON is showing.

Sol’s first try stays in the picture.

A break is the first place where the shown order stops following its rule.

Page 21: KEEP THE TRY, REPAIR THE TRAIL

Sol kept a small picture of his first try. Then he turned the fourth tile to GOLD SUN. The light moved forward.

“Keeping the try helped us find and fix the break,” said Tavi.

To repair means to fix what broke. The repeated order now works again. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: KEEPTHETRY

Page 22: THE BRIDGE HAS TWO WAYS

The light reached a small bridge with two ways across. The first light travelled OVER the curved bridge. The next light travelled UNDER it.

The pictures had changed, but the two ways could still take turns.

OVER means above the bridge. UNDER means below it. Watch their order before naming what comes next.

Page 23: TRY IT · CHOOSE THE NEXT WAY

OVER · UNDER · OVER · ?

The ways are taking turns in the same order. Which way should come next?

You are using a familiar rule with new words. Choose OVER or UNDER before you turn the page.

Page 24: UNDER COMES NEXT

OVER · UNDER · OVER · UNDER

UNDER comes next. Each way has a role, or job, in the repeated order: first OVER, then UNDER.

The names changed, but the take-turns rule stayed the same. The light can cross the bridge. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: OVERUNDER

Page 25: THREE ROUTES TO THE ARCH

Beyond the bridge, three routes led towards the Sunrise Arch. Only one route reached the arch and kept the lights taking turns at every step.

Vee asked the team to check each route from start to finish.

A route is a path from one place to another. The team needs one that is complete and follows the rule.

Page 26: TRY IT · CHOOSE THE WHOLE ROUTE

Ask two questions about each route:

1. Does it reach the arch?

2. Do the lights take turns all the way?

Which route has two YES answers?

You are using two checks instead of guessing. Point to one route before you turn the page.

Page 27: ROUTE C REACHES THE ARCH

ROUTE C does both jobs: it reaches the Sunrise Arch, and every light follows the rule.

Route A stops early. Route B breaks the order. The three tries stay visible so we can compare them.

The light can now travel across the whole garden. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: RIGHTROUTE

Page 28: REMEMBER THE FIRST GATE

The light reached the Sunrise Arch, but the arch did not shine yet. It showed a picture of BLUE MOON entering the first gate.

The gate turned the tile once. Which side came out?

Remembering means bringing something from earlier back to mind. Choose the picture you remember.

Page 29: GOLD SUN CAME OUT

GOLD SUN came out after one turn. You remembered the result from earlier before the answer was shown.

The picture now shows BLUE MOON before the turn and GOLD SUN after it.

Remembering an earlier result helps you use what you have already learned.

Page 30: TRY THE RULE WITH NEW PICTURES

The arch showed a new row: STAR · LEAF · STAR · ?

“The pictures have changed,” said Mia. “Can their jobs in the repeated order stay the same?” Which picture comes next?

Use the take-turns rule with the new pictures. Choose STAR or LEAF before you turn the page.

Page 31: LEAF COMES NEXT

STAR · LEAF · STAR · LEAF

LEAF comes next. STAR has the first job in the order. LEAF has the second. The pictures are new, but the same take-turns rule still works. The Sunrise Arch lit up!

A pattern comes from its repeated order, not from one special colour or picture. Find the optional code.

Optional picture code: NEWROLES

Page 32: THE MORNING LIGHT ARRIVES

You discovered how the Fold’s turn-and-return rule works. One turn showed the other side. The next turn brought the first side back. When this order repeated, it made a pattern. You used the pattern to predict, find a break, and repair the trail.
