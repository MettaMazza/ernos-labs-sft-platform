Page 3: The lantern begins

Last time, Mia, Sol and Tavi carried every part of the Moon Lantern and rebuilt the same whole.

Now the lantern glowed: moon, sun, moon.

Picture description: The friends watch the same complete lantern. Stable moon, sun and moon emoji cards show its three lights.

Page 4: The light runs away

The three lights skipped into the garden. Then the next path stone stayed dark.

Far ahead, the Sunrise Arch was still asleep.

Picture description: Moon, sun and moon emoji cards lead to one clearly dark path stone. The Sunrise Arch waits in the distance.

Page 5: Meet Vee

A purple path keeper hurried over.

“I’m Vee. This trail should wake the Sunrise Arch. Will you help us find the rule and mend the dark path?”

Picture description: Vee joins Mia, Sol and Tavi beside the stopped trail. The dark stone and distant arch keep the mission visible.

Page 6: One tile, two sides

Vee lifted one thick trail tile. One side showed a moon. The other side showed a sun.

It was one tile with two different sides.

Picture description: Two views of the same tile sit side by side: one has the stable moon emoji and one has the stable sun emoji.

Page 7: What is showing?

This page is a paper activity.

Look at Vee’s tile. Only one side faces up.

Point to the picture you can see now: MOON or SUN.

Make your choice. The next page shows the answer.

Picture description: A large tile shows the moon side. Separate moon and sun emoji answer cards appear below it.

Page 8: The moon side is showing

The moon is showing because we can see it now.

The sun is underneath. It is still part of the same tile, even though we cannot see it from above.

Picture description: The moon emoji is labelled SHOWING. A dim sun emoji below it is labelled UNDERNEATH.

Page 9: The first gate

Mia slid the tile into a round gate with the moon facing up.

Sol pulled the handle once. The gate turned the tile all the way over.

Picture description: A moon-side tile enters the round turning gate. One clear curved arrow shows one complete turn.

Page 10: Turn once

This page is a paper activity.

Hold one hand flat with your palm up. Turn it over once.

The tile began with MOON showing. Which side will show after one turn?

Point to MOON or SUN. Then turn the page.

Picture description: A moon card, one turning arrow and an empty after-card appear above moon and sun answer choices.

Page 11: One turn shows the sun

The sun now faces up. It is still the same tile.

A turn is the one agreed move that changes which side we can see.

Picture description: A before card shows moon. An arrow marked ONE TURN leads to an after card showing sun.

Page 12: Turn once more

This page is a paper activity.

The sun faces up now. Turn your hand over once more.

Which side will show next?

Point to MOON or SUN. The answer is on the next page.

Picture description: A sun card, one turning arrow and an empty after-card appear above moon and sun answer choices.

Page 13: The moon returns

The moon faces up again. It showed first, went underneath, and came back.

Return means an earlier side is showing again.

Picture description: A sun before-card turns into a moon after-card. The moon card is labelled RETURNS.

Page 14: Turn, then return

Start with moon. One turn shows sun. The next turn brings moon back.

We call this a Fold rule: turn to the other side, then return to the first.

Picture description: Three stable emoji cards show moon, sun and moon, joined by two one-turn arrows.

Page 15: Follow the lights

This page is a paper activity.

Point from left to right. Say each picture:

MOON · SUN · MOON · SUN

What do you notice about the order?

Picture description: Four large emoji cards form a row: moon, sun, moon, sun.

Page 16: What comes next?

This page is a paper activity.

MOON · SUN · MOON · SUN · ?

The same order is starting again. Which picture belongs in the empty place?

Point to MOON or SUN. Check on the next page.

Picture description: Moon, sun, moon and sun emoji cards are followed by one white blank card. Moon and sun choices appear below.

Page 17: Moon comes next

Moon comes next because the order starts again: moon, sun, moon, sun, moon.

A pattern is an order that repeats. The Fold’s turn-and-return rule made this pattern.

Picture description: The empty place is filled by the stable moon emoji. The full repeated row is visible.

Page 18: Sol tries a tile

The lights moved farther. Sol placed the next tile with its moon side up.

Now the row said moon, sun, moon, moon. The trail stopped again.

Picture description: The visible row is moon, sun, moon, moon. Sol's last moon card stays visible at the stopped trail.

Page 19: Find the first break

This page is a paper activity.

Check the row from left to right:

MOON · SUN · MOON · MOON

Which card is the first one that does not follow the moon-sun order?

Touch each card once. Point to the first break.

Picture description: Four numbered emoji cards show moon, sun, moon, moon. No answer is marked.

Page 20: The fourth card breaks the rule

The fourth card is the first break. After moon, the rule needs sun. Sol’s card shows moon instead.

We kept the first try so we could compare it.

Picture description: The fourth moon card is ringed and labelled FIRST BREAK. A sun card beside it is labelled THE RULE NEEDS SUN.

Page 21: The trail moves again

Sol kept a little picture of his first try. Then he turned the fourth tile to sun.

Moon, sun, moon, sun! The repaired trail glowed.

Picture description: A small inset keeps the original moon-sun-moon-moon row. The main row shows the repaired moon-sun-moon-sun sequence.

Page 22: Two ways across

The light reached a bridge. One beam travelled over the bridge. The next travelled under it.

The pictures changed, but two ways could still take turns.

Picture description: One labelled route goes over the bridge and another labelled route goes under it.

Page 23: Which way comes next?

This page is a paper activity.

Point to the way that keeps the same take-turns rule.

Page 24: Under comes next

Over, under, over, under. UNDER comes next.

The names changed, but the two-part take-turns rule stayed the same.

Picture description: Four labelled route cards show over, under, over, under across the bridge.

Page 25: Three paths to the arch

Three paths waited beyond the bridge.

The friends needed one path that reached the Sunrise Arch and kept moon and sun taking turns all the way.

Picture description: Three clearly separated paths are labelled A, B and C. Each uses the same stable moon and sun emoji cards.

Page 26: Choose the complete path

Page 27: Path C does both jobs

Path C reaches the arch, and every light follows the rule.

Path A stops early. Path B breaks the order. Keeping all three helps us compare.

Page 28: Remember the first gate

This page is a paper activity.

Which side came out?

Use what you remember. Choose MOON or SUN.

Page 29: Sun came out

Sun came out after one turn. You brought an earlier result back to mind.

Remembering helps us use what we have already learned.

Picture description: A before card shows moon and an after card shows sun, joined by one turn arrow.

Page 30: New pictures, same rule

This page is a paper activity.

Point to STAR or LEAF. Then turn the page.

Page 31: Leaf comes next

Star, leaf, star, leaf. LEAF comes next.

The pictures changed, but the same two-part rule still worked. The Sunrise Arch shone!

Picture description: The complete star-leaf-star-leaf emoji row glows beneath the Sunrise Arch as the four friends celebrate.

Page 32: You mended the trail

You found two different sides and one clear move between them. One turn showed the other side. The next turn brought the first side back.

Repeating that move made a pattern. You used the rule to predict, find a break and repair the path. A clear rule helps us know what should come next and check when something changes.

At the garden gate, Tavi asked, “How can we check our work?”
