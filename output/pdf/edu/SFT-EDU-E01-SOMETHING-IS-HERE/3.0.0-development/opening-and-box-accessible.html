Page 3

Mia loved finding things.
Today, she was looking for a new adventure.
A little blue door stood open. Mia peeked inside.

Picture description: Mia leans through an open blue entrance into the warm tower; her feet remain on the outside step. Sol and Tavi can be glimpsed inside.

Page 4

“Hello! I'm Sol,” said a golden friend.
“I'm Tavi,” said a blue one.
“I'm Mia. Can I come and play?”

Picture description: Sol waves, Tavi welcomes Mia with an open hand, and Mia smiles back. Each name is printed above its character.

Page 5

“Yes! Let's try the Star Door,” said Sol.
The friends walked across the room.
The big door was shut.

Picture description: The trio have crossed the room and stand looking at the closed Star Door. A gold-framed letter box is visible below its large star. The smaller entrance is out of view.

Page 6

A note slid through the letter box.
It landed on the rug by Mia.
Can you spot the little sheet of paper?

Picture description: The OpenMoji note lies on the rug in front of kneeling Mia. A red book and a world map lie separately on either side. The letter box is visible behind them. No choice has an answer label.

Page 7

“Here it is!” Mia picked up the note.
“There's a message!” she said.
Sol and Tavi came closer to listen.

Picture description: Mia holds out her hands beneath the same note emoji. Sol and Tavi lean close and look at it. The label Note is printed clearly above the paper. The map is shown on the following page.

Page 8

Mia read the message.
A map showed where to go.
Can you find nothing?
Look in these five places.
Tell us what you find.
A star will shine each time.
Five stars open the Star Door.

Picture description: The note's message is printed above a map. Arrows lead from box to bell to card, down to the curtain, then left to the shelves and back to the Star Door. Each of the five places has one hollow star. Object names sit above their pictures.

Page 9

“Find nothing? Let's look!” said Sol.
The map led to a box on the rug.
Sol opened the flaps.

Picture description: Mia, Sol and Tavi gather round the rug. A clearly open cardboard box has its flaps spread; the same teddy emoji is visibly inside, with the near wall in front of its lower body. The map retains five hollow stars.

Page 10

“My teddy!” said Sol.
He wanted to take it out.
Would the box go away too?

Picture description: A large open-box diagram makes the teddy visibly inside. There is no second teddy or solved arrow. Space on the rug beside the box is clear.

Page 11

Sol lifted the teddy out.
The box stayed on the rug.
Point inside the box. Now find the teddy.

Picture description: The identical open-box diagram is shown empty, including its complete interior floor. The same teddy emoji sits fully outside to the right. Labels Box and Teddy sit above the relevant objects.

Page 12

“The box is empty now,” said Tavi.
“No toys inside. But the box is still here!”
Their first star shone.

Picture description: The empty open-box diagram remains visible, with the same teddy fully outside. The trio's expressions change to pleased discovery. On the map, only the first star is gold; four remain hollow.
